Asset Frameworks

The semantic layer above tag stores. An asset framework maps physical equipment (pumps, turbines, heat exchangers) onto tag hierarchies, giving context to raw time-series values. Without one, you have data — not information.

AVEVA Asset Framework (PI AF)
Ships with PI System
Dominant

Hierarchical asset model sitting above the PI tag store. Element → Attribute → Template structure. Templates define asset types; elements are instances. PI Web API queries traverse this hierarchy. The AF database is the de facto asset registry at most PI-installed sites — and the natural ingestion control table for migration pipelines.

Key Standards

The standards landscape is fragmented but a small set appear repeatedly across architecture conversations, RFPs, and security assessments.

ISA-95 / IEC 62264
Enterprise–Control Integration
Defines the five-level functional hierarchy (Enterprise → Site → Area → Work Centre → Work Unit) and the data exchange models between Levels 3 and 4. The language of MES/ERP/historian integration. Every serious OT architect references it.
IEC 62443
ICS Cybersecurity
The OT security standard. Zone/conduit model, security levels (SL 1–4), CSMS requirements. The basis for NIS2 compliance in industrial environments. Use this for customer security conversations — it's the reference framework for CIS hardening in OT context.
OPC-UA (IEC 62541)
Unified Architecture
The platform-independent, service-oriented protocol standard for OT data exchange. Transport-agnostic (TCP, HTTPS, MQTT). Information modelling, security (certificates, RBAC), and pub/sub (OPC-UA PubSub) all defined. The dominant northbound protocol for modern industrial equipment.
Sparkplug B
MQTT Payload Specification
Eclipse Foundation spec defining MQTT topic namespace, payload format (Protobuf), and birth/death certificate mechanism for MQTT in industrial environments. Built on ISA-95 naming. Ignition is Sparkplug B native. The UNS enabler at the message layer.
ISO 14224
Reliability & Maintenance Data
Equipment taxonomy and failure data standard. Dominant in oil & gas for asset hierarchy definitions and RCM (Reliability-Centred Maintenance). Feeds into how asset types are defined in PI AF templates in upstream energy contexts.
NAMUR NE 107 / NE 153
Diagnostics & OPC-UA Profile
NE 107 defines the four-state diagnostic status model for field devices (Failure, Out of Specification, Maintenance Required, Function Check). NE 153 defines the NAMUR Open Architecture (NOA) OPC-UA companion specification — the standard for how field data reaches the IT layer in process industries.
IEC 61850
Power Systems Communication
The communication standard for electrical substations. Defines logical nodes, GOOSE messaging, and Sampled Values. Dominant in utilities and grid infrastructure. If a customer has substations, this is the protocol underneath — DNP3 is the older alternative still widely deployed in distribution.
W3C WoT TD
Thing Description
JSON-LD semantic description for IoT devices. Properties, actions, events, and protocol bindings. The IRIS project adopts this as the canonical asset definition format. Siemens, Bosch, and Fujitsu have active implementations. Increasingly relevant as the open alternative to proprietary asset frameworks.

Analyst Coverage

Who produces the market intelligence that shows up in RFPs, customer briefings, and internal board presentations.

ARC Advisory Group

The reference for industrial automation market share numbers. PI's ~65% process historian share comes from ARC reports. Their Industrial IoT and DCS market share studies are the ones vendors cite in competitive conversations. Subscription-based but summaries surface frequently.

LNS Research

Manufacturing operations and IIoT strategy research. Strong on MES, APM, and connected worker topics. More practitioner-oriented than ARC. Good for manufacturing vertical accounts (Saint-Gobain, Rio Tinto) where operational KPIs drive the conversation.

Gartner / IDC

Less OT-specific but relevant for IIoT platform Magic Quadrant content and market sizing figures. CIOs and CDOs will have seen these. IDC's Manufacturing Insights practice covers the IT/OT convergence narrative in terms your L-suite audience recognises.

Verdantix

Strong on operational risk, EHS, and asset performance management. Relevant for energy and utilities accounts where ESG and safety analytics are part of the OT conversation alongside operational data.

MESA International

Manufacturing Enterprise Solutions Association. Not an analyst firm but a practitioner community that publishes ISA-95 implementation guides and MES reference architectures. The people writing your customer RFPs have often read MESA white papers.

Open Process Automation Forum (OPAF)

ExxonMobil-originated open architecture initiative. Defines the O-PAS standard for vendor-interoperable DCS. Increasingly relevant as large process industry customers push back on vendor lock-in at the control layer — directionally aligned with the open lakehouse narrative at the data layer.

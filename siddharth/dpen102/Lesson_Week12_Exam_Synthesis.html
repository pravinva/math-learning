Mental model. Every synthesis question is a sequence of known blocks — name the block before calculating.

Method. FBD first, equilibrium second, material/section third, energy only if the prompt is motion/height/spring.

Watch for. The ten error killers in this lesson — skim them before every mock.

Run each paper as a genuine timed set (60–90 minutes), not a leisurely read-through. Three passes: first, solve the high-confidence questions quickly; second, work through medium-difficulty ones with full structure; final, attempt the hardest items with explicit stated assumptions. After each paper, tag every mistake by type, concept, sign, units, diagram, algebra, or interpretation, and check it against the list below.

Top 10 error killers
  1. Units not converted first. Mixing mm and m, or N and kN, mid-calculation.
  2. A force missing from the FBD. Usually weight, friction, or one support reaction.
  3. Inconsistent moment sign convention. Mixing clockwise and counter-clockwise within the same equation.
  4. \(\mu_s\) vs \(\mu_k\) confusion. Using the static coefficient after the object has already started sliding, or vice versa.
  5. Assuming \(N=W\) automatically. Forgetting other vertical forces (angled ropes, applied pushes) also affect \(N\).
  6. Wrong angle reference. Confusing "from horizontal" with "from vertical" in incline or vector problems.
  7. Skipping reactions before internal actions. Attempting \(V\), \(M\), or a truss cut before the global reactions are known.
  8. Tension/compression sign mix-up. Losing track of which assumed direction (tension) a positive or negative result actually confirms.
  9. Using a piece's own centroidal \(I\) instead of applying the parallel axis theorem to shift it to the composite axis.
  10. Not recognising a physically impossible result (negative KE, negative required friction) as a signal to re-solve for the actual limiting case.

PAPER A · 10 questions, mixed weeks · target 60–75 minutes
A1Wk 1

A 6 kg block rests on a table. Find the normal force.

Solution

\(N=6(9.8)=58.8\text{ N}\).

A2Wk 2

Convert 3400 N to kN.

Solution

\(3.4\text{ kN}\).

A3Wk 3

A force of 120 N acts at 35° above horizontal. Find its components.

Solution

\(F_x=120\cos35^\circ=98.3\text{ N}\). \(F_y=120\sin35^\circ=68.8\text{ N}\).

A4FBD / Wk 3

A 40 kg crate is pulled by a horizontal rope on a frictionless floor, accelerating at 1.5 m/s². Find the tension.

Solution

\(T=ma=40(1.5)=60\text{ N}\).

A5Wk 4

A rod, diameter 14 mm, carries 25 kN. Find the stress.

Solution

\(A=\pi(7)^2=153.9\text{ mm}^2\). \(\sigma=25{,}000/153.9=162.4\text{ MPa}\).

A6Wk 5

A beam, span 7 m, single 300 N load at 3 m from A. Find the reactions.

Solution

\(R_B=300(3)/7=128.57\text{ N}\). \(R_A=171.43\text{ N}\).

A7Wk 6

At joint P, two non-collinear members meet, no external load. State their forces.

Solution

Both are zero-force members (Rule 1).

A8Wk 7

A 12 kg block on an 18° incline, \(\mu_s=0.4\). Does it stay in place?

Solution

\(W=117.6\text{ N}\). \(N=111.85\text{ N}\). \(F_s^{max}=44.74\text{ N}\). Required \(=36.34\text{ N}\). \(36.34<44.74\): stays.

A9Wk 8

Two rectangles: \(A_1=15\) at \((2,3)\), \(A_2=10\) at \((6,1)\). Find the composite centroid.

Solution

\(\bar x=\dfrac{15(2)+10(6)}{25}=3.6\). \(\bar y=\dfrac{15(3)+10(1)}{25}=2.2\).

A10Wk 9

A 4 kg block starts at rest, 60 J of net work is done on it. Find the final speed.

Solution

\(v=\sqrt{2(60)/4}=\sqrt{30}=5.48\text{ m/s}\).

PAPER B · 10 questions, mixed weeks · target 60–75 minutes
B1Wk 1

A 12 kg object accelerates at 3 m/s². Find the net force.

Solution

\(F=12(3)=36\text{ N}\).

B2Wk 2

Convert 0.045 m to mm.

Solution

\(45\text{ mm}\).

B3Wk 3

A force has components \(F_x=-8\text{ N}, F_y=-6\text{ N}\). Find its magnitude and angle from the positive \(x\)-axis.

Solution

\(F=\sqrt{64+36}=10\text{ N}\). Reference angle \(=36.87^\circ\); Quadrant III: \(\theta=180+36.87=216.87^\circ\).

B4FBD

A 60 N sign hangs symmetrically from two ropes, each at 50° above horizontal. Find the tension in each.

Solution

\(2T\sin50^\circ=60 \Rightarrow T=60/(2\times0.766)=39.16\text{ N}\).

B5Wk 4

A column, area 1800 mm², carries 150 kN. Find the stress in MPa.

Solution

\(\sigma=150{,}000/1800=83.33\text{ MPa}\) (N/mm² = MPa shortcut).

B6Wk 5

A cantilever, fixed at A, length 2.5 m, single 200 N load at the free end. Find the reaction moment at A.

Solution

\(M_A=200(2.5)=500\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}\).

B7Wk 6

A truss spans 10 m, pin-roller, 600 N load at 6 m from A. Find the reactions.

Solution

\(R_B=600(6)/10=360\text{ N}\). \(R_A=240\text{ N}\).

B8Wk 7

A belt wraps 200° (3.49 rad), \(\mu=0.3\). \(T_1=180\text{ N}\). Find \(T_2\).

Solution

\(\mu\beta=0.3(3.49)=1.047\). \(e^{1.047}=2.849\). \(T_2=180(2.849)=512.8\text{ N}\).

B9Wk 8

A rectangle, \(b=90\text{ mm}, h=160\text{ mm}\). Find the centroidal \(I\).

Solution

\(I=\dfrac{90(160)^3}{12}=30{,}720{,}000\text{ mm}^4\).

B10Wk 9

A spring \(k=350\text{ N/m}\), compressed 0.12 m, launches a 0.6 kg block (frictionless). Find the launch speed.

Solution

\(U_s=\dfrac12(350)(0.0144)=2.52\text{ J}\). \(v=\sqrt{2(2.52)/0.6}=\sqrt{8.4}=2.898\text{ m/s}\).

PAPER C · 10 questions, mixed weeks · target 60–75 minutes
C1Wk 1 / 3

A 20 kg crate is pulled by a 180 N force at 25° above horizontal on a frictionless floor. Find horizontal acceleration and normal force.

Solution

\(F_x=180\cos25^\circ=163.1\text{ N}\), \(F_y=180\sin25^\circ=76.1\text{ N}\).

\(a=F_x/m=163.1/20=8.15\text{ m/s}^2\). Vertical equilibrium: \(N+F_y-mg=0\Rightarrow N=196-76.1=119.9\text{ N}\).

C2Wk 2 / 4

A bar carries 48 kN and has area 450 mm². Find stress in MPa and Pa, then state whether it passes if allowable is 130 MPa.

Solution

\(\sigma=48{,}000/450=106.67\text{ N/mm}^2=106.67\text{ MPa}\).

In Pa: \(106.67\text{ MPa}=106.67\times10^6\text{ Pa}\). Since \(106.67<130\), it passes.

C3Wk 5

A simply supported beam span 9 m has UDL \(w=0.8\text{ kN/m}\) full span and a 2.5 kN load at \(x=6\) m from A. Find reactions.

Solution

UDL total \(=0.8(9)=7.2\text{ kN}\) at \(x=4.5\) m.

\(\Sigma M_A\): \(R_B(9)=7.2(4.5)+2.5(6)=47.4\Rightarrow R_B=5.267\text{ kN}\).

\(R_A=7.2+2.5-5.267=4.433\text{ kN}\).

C4Wk 5 / 11

For beam C3, find moment at \(x=4\) m. If section at that location has \(I=22\times10^6\text{ mm}^4,\ c=95\) mm, find bending stress.

Solution

Using left segment: \(M(4)=R_A(4)-w(4)(4/2)=4.433(4)-0.8(4)(2)=17.732-6.4=11.332\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}\).

\(\sigma=Mc/I=(11.332\times10^6)(95)/(22\times10^6)=48.93\text{ MPa}\).

C5Wk 6

A truss support reaction check gives \(R_A=14\) kN, \(R_B=10\) kN while applied vertical loads total 23 kN. Identify the issue and correct one reaction if \(R_A\) is trusted.

Solution

Equilibrium in \(y\) requires \(R_A+R_B=\Sigma W\). Current sum is \(24\) kN, inconsistent.

If \(R_A=14\) kN is correct, \(R_B\) must be \(23-14=9\) kN.

C6Wk 7

A 16 kg crate on a \(22^\circ\) incline has \(\mu_s=0.32\), \(\mu_k=0.24\). Determine if it moves from rest; if yes find acceleration.

Solution

\(W=156.8\text{ N}\). Downslope \(=W\sin22^\circ=58.74\text{ N}\). \(N=W\cos22^\circ=145.37\text{ N}\).

\(F_s^{max}=0.32N=46.52\text{ N}\). Since required 58.74 N is larger, it slides.

\(f_k=0.24N=34.89\text{ N}\). Net \(=58.74-34.89=23.85\text{ N}\). \(a=23.85/16=1.49\text{ m/s}^2\).

C7Wk 8

Composite area: \(A_1=2000\) at \((x,y)=(15,25)\), \(A_2=1200\) at \((80,25)\), hole \(A_3=-400\) at \((50,60)\). Find \((\bar x,\bar y)\).

Solution

Total area \(=2000+1200-400=2800\).

\(\bar x=\dfrac{2000(15)+1200(80)-400(50)}{2800}=\dfrac{106000}{2800}=37.86\).

\(\bar y=\dfrac{2000(25)+1200(25)-400(60)}{2800}=\dfrac{56000}{2800}=20.00\).

C8Wk 9

A 2.5 kg block starts from rest 2.0 m high, descends frictionless, then compresses a spring \(k=700\text{ N/m}\) on level ground with friction force 8 N during compression only. Find maximum compression.

Solution

Energy at spring contact: \(mgh=2.5(9.8)(2)=49\text{ J}\).

During compression \(x\): \(49=\dfrac12(700)x^2+8x\Rightarrow 350x^2+8x-49=0\).

\(x=\dfrac{-8+\sqrt{64+68600}}{700}=\dfrac{-8+262.04}{700}=0.363\text{ m}\).

C9Wk 4 / 11

A member with area 540 mm² is subjected to fluctuating axial load between 20 kN and 68 kN tension. For allowable stress 115 MPa, check worst-case pass/fail and margin.

Solution

Worst case uses \(68\) kN: \(\sigma_{max}=68{,}000/540=125.93\text{ MPa}\).

\(125.93>115\), so it fails by \(10.93\text{ MPa}\) or \(9.5\%\).

C10Capstone Mix

In a single line each, list the best equation family to start with for: (i) support reactions, (ii) member stress, (iii) spring+height motion, (iv) composite centroid.

Solution

(i) \(\Sigma F_x=0,\ \Sigma F_y=0,\ \Sigma M=0\). (ii) \(\sigma=F/A\) or \(\sigma=Mc/I\). (iii) \(K_1+U_1+W_{nc}=K_2+U_2\). (iv) \(\bar x=\Sigma A_ix_i/\Sigma A_i,\ \bar y=\Sigma A_iy_i/\Sigma A_i\).

PAPER D · 10 questions, mixed weeks · target 60–75 minutes
D1Wk 3 / FBD

A 90 N sign is hung by two symmetric cables each at 40° to horizontal. Find cable tension and horizontal pinching force at each support.

Solution

\(2T\sin40^\circ=90\Rightarrow T=90/(2\times0.6428)=69.99\text{ N}\).

Horizontal component each side: \(T\cos40^\circ=69.99(0.7660)=53.6\text{ N}\) toward center.

D2Wk 5

A simply supported beam span 6 m has point loads 1.2 kN at \(x=2\) m and 1.8 kN at \(x=5\) m from A. Find reactions and moment at \(x=4\) m.

Solution

\(R_B(6)=1.2(2)+1.8(5)=11.4\Rightarrow R_B=1.9\text{ kN}\). \(R_A=3.0-1.9=1.1\text{ kN}\).

\(M(4)=R_A(4)-1.2(4-2)=1.1(4)-1.2(2)=2.0\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}\).

D3Wk 6

At a truss joint with three members, two are collinear and no external load is applied at the joint. State the force in the third member and explain briefly.

Solution

The non-collinear third member is zero-force. With no joint load, equilibrium perpendicular to collinear members requires zero in that third member.

D4Wk 7 / 9

A 5 kg block moves up a \(30^\circ\) incline at 6 m/s and slides 2.4 m before stopping. Find average friction force magnitude along incline.

Solution

Initial KE \(=0.5(5)(6^2)=90\text{ J}\). Gravitational rise \(=mgd\sin30^\circ=5(9.8)(2.4)(0.5)=58.8\text{ J}\).

Remaining \(90-58.8=31.2\text{ J}\) is friction loss. \(f=31.2/2.4=13.0\text{ N}\).

D5Wk 8 / 11

A rectangular section \(100\times180\) mm carries \(M=14\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}\). Find max bending stress and required FoS against yield if \(\sigma_y=260\) MPa.

Solution

\(I=\dfrac{100(180^3)}{12}=48.6\times10^6\text{ mm}^4\), \(c=90\text{ mm}\).

\(\sigma=14\times10^6(90)/(48.6\times10^6)=25.93\text{ MPa}\).

Implied FoS to yield \(=260/25.93=10.0\), very high safety against yield.

D6Wk 4

A stepped bar has minimum section 320 mm² and must carry 52 kN. Material yield is 280 MPa. Find actual FoS at the critical section.

Solution

\(\sigma_{work}=52{,}000/320=162.5\text{ MPa}\).

\(FoS=\sigma_y/\sigma_{work}=280/162.5=1.72\).

D7Wk 9

A \(0.7\) kg block is launched by spring \(k=420\text{ N/m}\), compression 0.18 m, up a frictionless ramp. Find maximum vertical rise.

Solution

\(U_s=\dfrac12(420)(0.18^2)=6.804\text{ J}\). At top, \(mgh=U_s\).

\(h=6.804/(0.7\cdot9.8)=0.992\text{ m}\).

D8Wk 5 / 7

A 2 m cantilever carries a tip load 500 N and uniform load 120 N/m. On this beam, a 3 kg tool slides with kinetic friction coefficient 0.2. Find wall moment and the friction force on the tool.

Solution

UDL total \(=240\text{ N}\) at 1 m. Wall moment \(=500(2)+240(1)=1240\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}\).

Tool friction on horizontal beam: \(f_k=\mu_kN=0.2(3)(9.8)=5.88\text{ N}\).

D9Wk 8

Two areas: \(A_1=1800\) at \(x_1=12\) mm, \(A_2=2200\) at \(x_2=64\) mm. A circular hole \(A_3=-300\) at \(x_3=40\) mm. Find \(\bar x\).

Solution

Total area \(=1800+2200-300=3700\).

\(\bar x=\dfrac{1800(12)+2200(64)-300(40)}{3700}=\dfrac{150400}{3700}=40.65\text{ mm}\).

D10Meta Exam

You compute a negative final kinetic energy in a multi-step problem. Write the exact two corrective actions you should take in an exam script.

Solution

(1) State that negative KE is physically impossible and indicates the assumed endpoint is unreachable.

(2) Re-solve with limiting condition \(K=0\) to find the true maximum reachable distance/height, then report that as final answer.

Q1 W10 Simply supported beam span 12 m with 4 kN/m UDL full span. Find reactions.
Total load \(=48\) kN at midspan, so \(R_A=R_B=24\) kN.
Q2 W10 For Q1, find maximum bending moment.
\(M_{max}=wL^2/8=4(144)/8=72\text{ kN·m}\).
Q3 W11 Section at Q2 has \(I=45\times10^6\) mm⁴, \(c=120\) mm. Find bending stress.
\(M=72\times10^6\) N·mm. \(\sigma=Mc/I=(72\times10^6)(120)/(45\times10^6)=192\) MPa.
Q4 W11 If allowable stress is 160 MPa, what is verdict for Q3?
Fail, because \(192>160\) MPa.
Q5 W11 Under 58 kN tension with \(\sigma_{allow}=145\) MPa, find required area and equivalent diameter.
\(A=58000/145=400\) mm². \(d=\sqrt{4A/\pi}=22.57\) mm.
Q6 W11 Material has \(\sigma_y=360\) MPa and FoS 3.2. Find allowable.
\(\sigma_{allow}=360/3.2=112.5\) MPa.
Q7 W10 Beam span 10 m with 20 kN at 3 m and 15 kN at 8 m. Find reactions.
\(\Sigma M_A: R_B(10)=20(3)+15(8)=180\Rightarrow R_B=18\) kN. \(R_A=35-18=17\) kN.
Q8 W10 For Q7, find moment at \(x=5\) m using left segment.
\(M(5)=R_A(5)-20(5-3)=17(5)-40=45\text{ kN·m}\).
Q9 W11 Q8 location has \(I=30\times10^6\) mm⁴ and \(c=90\) mm. Find stress.
\(\sigma=(45\times10^6)(90)/(30\times10^6)=135\) MPa.
Q10 W12 Give the first two triage questions to ask when reading a new exam problem.
1) What is the demanded output (reaction, force, stress, deflection surrogate, safety verdict)? 2) Which governing model applies first (equilibrium, constitutive stress check, or energy)?
Q11 W12 Why should you box the sign convention before computing moments?
It prevents hidden sign flips across equations and keeps all rotational terms consistent, avoiding self-contradictory balances.
Q12 W12 If reactions do not sum to external load, what does that imply?
At least one equation, sign, or load value is wrong; proceed no further until global equilibrium is corrected.
Q13 Challenge Composite section area 5200 mm² carries 420 kN axial. \(\sigma_y=300\) MPa, FoS 2.5. Pass/fail and margin %.
\(\sigma_{work}=420000/5200=80.77\) MPa. \(\sigma_{allow}=120\) MPa. Pass. Margin \(=(120-80.77)/120=32.7\%\).
Q14 Challenge Beam span 9 m, UDL 2.4 kN/m full span and 12 kN point load at 6 m. Find reactions.
UDL total \(=21.6\) kN at 4.5 m. \(\Sigma M_A: R_B(9)=21.6(4.5)+12(6)=169.2\Rightarrow R_B=18.8\) kN. \(R_A=33.6-18.8=14.8\) kN.
Q15 Challenge For Q14, find moment at 4 m.
\(M(4)=R_A(4)-w(4)(2)=14.8(4)-2.4(4)(2)=59.2-19.2=40.0\text{ kN·m}\).
Q16 Challenge Q15 section has \(I=22\times10^6\) mm⁴, \(c=100\) mm. Find \(\sigma_b\).
\(\sigma=(40\times10^6)(100)/(22\times10^6)=181.8\) MPa.
Q17 Challenge Allowable for Q16 material is 170 MPa. By what percent is it overstressed?
Overstress \(=(181.8-170)/170=6.94\%\).
Q18 Challenge Design target: carry 95 kN with \(\sigma_{allow}=125\) MPa. Choose minimum standard diameter from [28, 30, 32] mm.
\(A_{req}=95000/125=760\) mm². Required \(d=\sqrt{4A/\pi}=31.11\) mm, so choose 32 mm.
Q19 Challenge A solution reports \(R_A=-4\) kN upward for an all-downward-loaded simply supported beam. What should you check first?
Check moment equation signs and load positions; negative reaction may be physically possible in uplift cases, but usually indicates sign/geometry error in basic beams.
Q20 Challenge Why is "reasonable magnitude" check essential after algebraically correct work?
Algebra can be consistent with wrong inputs/units; reasonableness catches 10x-1000x scaling mistakes that pure symbol manipulation cannot.
Q21 Challenge Build a one-line triage for this prompt: "Find if beam is safe under given loading."
Reactions \(\rightarrow\) internal moment at critical section \(\rightarrow\) compute stress (\(Mc/I\)) \(\rightarrow\) compare with allowable and state pass/fail margin.
Q22 Challenge A member has \(\sigma_{work}=98\) MPa and \(\sigma_{allow}=100\) MPa. Is this automatically "good design"?
It passes formally but has only 2% headroom; discuss uncertainty, load variability, and whether additional margin is needed in practical design.
Q23 W12 List five items in a pre-submit exam checklist.
Complete diagram, unit consistency, sign convention consistency, equilibrium back-check, boxed final answer with physical interpretation.
Q24 Capstone Write a compact template answer sentence for any pass/fail result.
"Using \(X\)-model, computed \(\sigma_{work}=...\) MPa against \(\sigma_{allow}=...\) MPa, therefore section passes/fails with margin of ...%."

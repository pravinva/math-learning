Before you start · Weeks 1–6 at a glance

Week 1 — Newton's laws & equilibrium

Topics

Force, weight, normal reaction; equilibrium vs acceleration.

Main ideas / remember

  • Net force causes acceleration; zero net force ⇒ no acceleration.
  • Draw an FBD and fix +x/+y before writing \(\Sigma F\).
Key: \(\Sigma F=ma\); \(W=mg\); equilibrium \(\Rightarrow\Sigma F_x=\Sigma F_y=0\).

Week 2 — Units, method & moments

Topics

SI conversions; solving method; moments of forces.

Main ideas / remember

  • Convert units once at the start (kN↔N, MPa↔Pa, mm↔m).
  • Moment \(M=Fd_\perp\); pick a moment centre that kills an unknown.
Key: \(M=Fd_\perp\); \(\Sigma M=0\).

Week 3 — Vectors, components & FBDs

Topics

Resolving forces; resultant; 2D FBD equilibrium.

Main ideas / remember

  • Resolve first: \(F_x=F\cos\theta\), \(F_y=F\sin\theta\).
  • Check quadrant when reporting the resultant angle.
Key: \(F_x=F\cos\theta\), \(F_y=F\sin\theta\); \(|R|=\sqrt{R_x^2+R_y^2}\).

Week 4 — Stress, strain & internal actions

Topics

Stress/strain; FoS and allowable stress; cut for \(N,V,M\).

Main ideas / remember

  • Material check: \(\sigma_{\mathrm{work}}\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}=\sigma_y/\mathrm{FoS}\).
  • Find reactions, then cut one side for internals.
Key: \(\sigma=F/A\); \(\varepsilon=\Delta L/L_0\); \(A_{\mathrm{req}}=F/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}\).

Week 5 — SFD & BMD

Topics

Shear and moment diagrams; UDL vs point loads; max moment.

Main ideas / remember

  • Reactions first; shear must close to zero at the far end.
  • \(M\) peaks where \(V=0\); full-span UDL \(\Rightarrow M_{\max}=wL^2/8\).
Key: \(dM/dx=V\); \(M_{\max}=wL^2/8\) (SS + full UDL).

Week 6 — Truss method of sections

Topics

Zero-force members; reactions; cut ≤3 members; tension/compression signs.

Main ideas / remember

  • Assume tension; negative ⇒ compression.
  • Moment about the intersection of two unknowns isolates the third.
Key: \(\Sigma F_x=\Sigma F_y=\Sigma M=0\) on the cut piece.
Q1 W1 18 kg body has net horizontal force 63 N. Find acceleration.
\(a=F/m=63/18=3.50\text{ m/s}^2\).
Q2 W1 A 10 kg block is at rest on level ground while pushed right by 45 N and opposed by friction. Find friction magnitude.
At rest, \(\Sigma F_x=0\), so friction must match push: \(45\text{ N}\) left.
Q3 W2 Convert 2.75 kN to N and 350 MPa to Pa.
\(2.75\text{ kN}=2750\text{ N}\). \(350\text{ MPa}=350\times10^6\text{ Pa}\).
Q4 W2 Moment about O from 420 N applied at 0.65 m perpendicular distance.
\(M=Fd=420(0.65)=273\text{ N·m}\).
Q5 W3 Resolve 160 N force at 28° above horizontal into components.
\(F_x=160\cos28=141.23\text{ N}\), \(F_y=160\sin28=75.12\text{ N}\).
Q6 W3 Vector has components \((-9,12)\) N. Find magnitude and direction from +x.
Magnitude \(=15\text{ N}\). Reference \(=53.13^\circ\). Quadrant II so \(\theta=126.87^\circ\).
Q7 W4 Bar area 320 mm² carries 38 kN tension. Find stress in MPa.
\(\sigma=38000/320=118.75\text{ MPa}\).
Q8 W4 If \(\sigma_y=300\) MPa and FoS \(=2.5\), does Q7 pass?
\(\sigma_{allow}=120\) MPa. Since \(118.75<120\), pass (very small margin).
Q9 W5 Simply supported beam span 8 m carries central point load 24 kN. Find reactions.
By symmetry \(R_A=R_B=12\text{ kN}\).
Q10 W5 Beam span 6 m with full UDL \(w=5\) kN/m. Find max moment.
\(M_{max}=wL^2/8=5(36)/8=22.5\text{ kN·m}\).
Q11 W6 At unloaded joint with two non-collinear members only, classify forces.
Both members are zero-force members.
Q12 W6 Member force from analysis is \(-11\) kN assuming tension. Interpret.
Actual member force is 11 kN compression.
Q13 Mixed A 30 kg crate is pulled by 150 N at 25° on frictionless floor. Find acceleration and normal.
\(F_x=150\cos25=135.95\text{ N}\), \(F_y=63.39\text{ N}\). \(a=F_x/m=4.53\text{ m/s}^2\). \(N=mg-F_y=294-63.39=230.61\text{ N}\).
Q14 Mixed Beam span 10 m has loads 8 kN at 2 m and 14 kN at 7 m from A. Find reactions.
\(\Sigma M_A: R_B(10)=8(2)+14(7)=114\Rightarrow R_B=11.4\) kN. \(R_A=22-11.4=10.6\) kN.
Q15 Mixed For Q14, find moment at \(x=5\) m.
Using left segment: \(M(5)=R_A(5)-8(5-2)=10.6(5)-24=29\text{ kN·m}\).
Q16 Mixed Section at Q15 has \(I=20\times10^6\) mm⁴ and \(c=85\) mm. Find bending stress.
\(\sigma=Mc/I=(29\times10^6)(85)/(20\times10^6)=123.25\text{ MPa}\).
Q17 Mixed Allowable stress is 110 MPa. Does Q16 pass?
No, \(123.25>110\) MPa, so fail.
Q18 Mixed Required area for 72 kN axial tension with allowable 125 MPa.
\(A_{req}=72000/125=576\text{ mm}^2\).
Q19 Mixed Convert Q18 area to required diameter for circular rod.
\(d=\sqrt{4A/\pi}=\sqrt{4(576)/\pi}=27.08\text{ mm}\).
Q20 Challenge Vector sum: \(F_1=(40,15)\) N, \(F_2=(-18,26)\) N, \(F_3=(-12,-19)\) N. Find resultant magnitude/direction.
\(R=(10,22)\) N. \(|R|=\sqrt{10^2+22^2}=24.17\) N. \(\theta=\tan^{-1}(22/10)=65.56^\circ\) (Quadrant I).
Q21 Challenge Why is solving reactions before internal forces non-negotiable in W5/W6?
Internal shear/moment/member forces depend on boundary reactions; without correct reactions, every downstream internal value is shifted and unreliable.
Q22 Challenge A reaction solution gives \(R_A+R_B=26\) kN but external downward load is 24 kN. What now?
Stop and debug immediately; equilibrium violation means at least one equation/sign/load is wrong.
Q23 Challenge In one line: how do you detect a hidden unit bug in a stress answer?
Back-substitute dimensions: if \(F/A\) does not reduce to \(N/mm^2\) or Pa consistently with your chosen unit system, units are mixed.
Q24 Capstone Write the minimum exam answer skeleton for a cumulative W1-6 problem.
Diagram/FBD + knowns/unknowns, sign convention, governing equations (\(\Sigma F,\Sigma M,\sigma=F/A\) as needed), clean solve, and final verification statement.

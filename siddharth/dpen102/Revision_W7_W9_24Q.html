Friction systems, centroid/inertia, and work-energy with exam-level multi-step combinations.

Scoring habit: explicitly decide friction state first (stick or slip), mark hole areas negative in centroid, and track energy losses clearly in one equation line.
Q1 W7 20 kg block on 18° incline, \(\mu_s=0.30\). Will it move?
\(W\sin18=60.58\) N. \(N=20(9.8)\cos18=186.43\) N. \(F_{s,max}=0.30N=55.93\) N. Required friction exceeds max, so it moves.
Q2 W7 For Q1 with \(\mu_k=0.22\), find acceleration.
\(f_k=0.22(186.43)=41.01\) N. Net downslope \(=60.58-41.01=19.57\) N. \(a=19.57/20=0.978\text{ m/s}^2\).
Q3 W7 Belt friction: \(\mu=0.28\), wrap \(=210^\circ\), \(T_1=160\) N. Find \(T_2\).
\(\beta=210^\circ=3.665\) rad. \(T_2=T_1e^{\mu\beta}=160e^{1.026}=446.6\) N.
Q4 W7 Angle of repose observed as 27°. Estimate \(\mu_s\).
\(\mu_s=\tan27^\circ=0.510\).
Q5 W7 Why can \(N\neq mg\) on an incline?
Normal is perpendicular reaction, so it balances only perpendicular component of weight and other perpendicular forces, not total weight.
Q6 W7 Horizontal pull 120 N at 30° upward acts on 25 kg block, \(\mu_s=0.4\). Find maximum static friction.
\(N=mg-P\sin30=245-60=185\) N. \(F_{s,max}=0.4(185)=74\) N.
Q7 W8 Composite centroid: \(A_1=1200\) at \(x=20\), \(A_2=800\) at \(x=70\). Find \(\bar x\).
\(\bar x=(1200(20)+800(70))/2000=40\) mm.
Q8 W8 Add a hole \(A_3=-300\) at \(x=50\). Recompute \(\bar x\).
\(\bar x=(1200(20)+800(70)-300(50))/(1700)=38.24\) mm.
Q9 W8 Rectangle \(b=80\) mm, \(h=200\) mm. Find centroidal \(I_x\).
\(I_x=bh^3/12=80(200^3)/12=53.33\times10^6\text{ mm}^4\).
Q10 W8 Using Q9, shift axis by 40 mm with area 16000 mm². Find new \(I\).
\(I_{new}=I_c+Ad^2=53.33\times10^6+16000(40^2)=78.93\times10^6\text{ mm}^4\).
Q11 W8 Explain why area of a hole is negative in centroid and \(I\) calculations.
A hole removes material, so its geometric contribution must subtract from total first and second area moments.
Q12 W8 Which shape has higher \(I\): same-area solid rectangle or I-section with material farther from neutral axis?
I-section, because \(I\) increases strongly with distance squared from neutral axis (\(Ad^2\) effect).
Q13 W9 A 5 kg object starts from rest. Net work 90 J. Find final speed.
\(W_{net}=\Delta K=\frac12 mv^2\Rightarrow v=\sqrt{2(90)/5}=6\text{ m/s}\).
Q14 W9 A 2 kg mass drops 3.5 m (no losses). Find impact speed.
\(mgh=\frac12mv^2\Rightarrow v=\sqrt{2gh}=\sqrt{68.6}=8.28\text{ m/s}\).
Q15 W9 Spring \(k=600\) N/m compressed 0.18 m launches 1.5 kg block on frictionless level. Find speed.
\(\frac12kx^2=\frac12mv^2\Rightarrow v=\sqrt{kx^2/m}=\sqrt{600(0.0324)/1.5}=3.60\text{ m/s}\).
Q16 W9 Why is work by kinetic friction negative?
Friction force opposes displacement, so \(W=\vec F\cdot\vec s=Fs\cos180^\circ=-Fs\), reducing mechanical energy.
Q17 W7-9 8 kg block slides 5 m down 20° incline with \(\mu_k=0.18\), starts from rest. Find final speed.
\(W_g=mg(s\sin20)=8(9.8)(5)(0.342)=134.06\) J. \(N=mg\cos20=73.66\) N, \(f_k=13.26\) N, \(W_f=-66.3\) J. \(\Delta K=67.76\) J. \(v=\sqrt{2\Delta K/m}=4.12\text{ m/s}\).
Q18 W8-9 Beam section at critical point has \(I=26\times10^6\) mm⁴, \(c=75\) mm, moment 28 kN·m. Find stress.
\(\sigma=Mc/I=(28\times10^6)(75)/(26\times10^6)=80.77\text{ MPa}\).
Q19 Challenge Pulley system has \(T_1=240\) N and \(\mu=0.25\), \(\beta=\pi\). Find \(T_2\) and net tangential force \(T_2-T_1\).
\(T_2=T_1e^{\mu\beta}=240e^{0.785}=526.8\) N. Net \(=286.8\) N.
Q20 Challenge Composite centroid y: \(A_1=1800,y_1=20\), \(A_2=1200,y_2=75\), hole \(A_3=-400,y_3=50\). Find \(\bar y\).
\(\bar y=(1800(20)+1200(75)-400(50))/(2600)=40.77\) mm.
Q21 Challenge 3 kg block with speed 5 m/s compresses spring \(k=900\) N/m on frictionless path. Find max compression.
\(\frac12mv^2=\frac12kx^2\Rightarrow x=v\sqrt{m/k}=5\sqrt{3/900}=0.2887\) m.
Q22 Challenge Same as Q21 but 18 J lost to friction before spring. Find new compression.
Initial KE \(=37.5\) J. Energy to spring \(=19.5\) J. \(\frac12(900)x^2=19.5\Rightarrow x=0.208\) m.
Q23 Challenge In one sentence, connect friction analysis and work-energy analysis.
Friction force found from statics/dynamics directly determines dissipation term \(W_f=-f_k s\) in the energy equation.
Q24 Capstone Give a 5-line solution template for mixed W7-9 problems.
State motion/friction regime → compute geometric components (\(N,mg\sin\theta\)) → write force/energy equation with signs → solve unknown → verify units and whether result is physically plausible.

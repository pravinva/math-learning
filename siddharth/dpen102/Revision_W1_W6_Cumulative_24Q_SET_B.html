Cumulative Revision Set B: Weeks 1-6 (24 Questions)

A second, reasoning-rich checkpoint: linked beam and truss chains, diagnostics, interpretation, design checks, and capstone synthesis.

Back to DPEN102 Index
Diagrams required. Every question asks you to draw or sketch something first (FBD, loaded beam, cut section, SFD/BMD, truss joint, cross-section, or energy sketch). Formula-only answers are not enough.
Use as a mock: 90-110 minutes. Q1-12 build foundations; Q13-19 are linked chains; Q20-23 are diagnostics; Q24 is the capstone. Use g = 9.8 m/s².

Before you start · Weeks 1–6 at a glance

Week 1 — Newton's laws & equilibrium

Topics

Force, weight, normal reaction; equilibrium vs acceleration.

Main ideas / remember

  • Net force causes acceleration; zero net force ⇒ no acceleration.
  • Draw an FBD and fix +x/+y before writing \(\Sigma F\).
Key: \(\Sigma F=ma\); \(W=mg\); equilibrium \(\Rightarrow\Sigma F_x=\Sigma F_y=0\).

Week 2 — Units, method & moments

Topics

SI conversions; solving method; moments of forces.

Main ideas / remember

  • Convert units once at the start (kN↔N, MPa↔Pa, mm↔m).
  • Moment \(M=Fd_\perp\); pick a moment centre that kills an unknown.
Key: \(M=Fd_\perp\); \(\Sigma M=0\).

Week 3 — Vectors, components & FBDs

Topics

Resolving forces; resultant; 2D FBD equilibrium.

Main ideas / remember

  • Resolve first: \(F_x=F\cos\theta\), \(F_y=F\sin\theta\).
  • Check quadrant when reporting the resultant angle.
Key: \(F_x=F\cos\theta\), \(F_y=F\sin\theta\); \(|R|=\sqrt{R_x^2+R_y^2}\).

Week 4 — Stress, strain & internal actions

Topics

Stress/strain; FoS and allowable stress; cut for \(N,V,M\).

Main ideas / remember

  • Material check: \(\sigma_{\mathrm{work}}\le\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}=\sigma_y/\mathrm{FoS}\).
  • Find reactions, then cut one side for internals.
Key: \(\sigma=F/A\); \(\varepsilon=\Delta L/L_0\); \(A_{\mathrm{req}}=F/\sigma_{\mathrm{allow}}\).

Week 5 — SFD & BMD

Topics

Shear and moment diagrams; UDL vs point loads; max moment.

Main ideas / remember

  • Reactions first; shear must close to zero at the far end.
  • \(M\) peaks where \(V=0\); full-span UDL \(\Rightarrow M_{\max}=wL^2/8\).
Key: \(dM/dx=V\); \(M_{\max}=wL^2/8\) (SS + full UDL).

Week 6 — Truss method of sections

Topics

Zero-force members; reactions; cut ≤3 members; tension/compression signs.

Main ideas / remember

  • Assume tension; negative ⇒ compression.
  • Moment about the intersection of two unknowns isolates the third.
Key: \(\Sigma F_x=\Sigma F_y=\Sigma M=0\) on the cut piece.
Q1 W1 A 1200 kg lift accelerates upward at 0.80 m/s². Draw its FBD, then determine the cable tension and explain why it exceeds the weight.
FBD — accelerating lift
Sign convention

+y upward.

Working

\(W=mg=1200(9.8)=11760\text{ N}\).

\(\Sigma F_y=ma:\ T-11760=1200(0.80)\).

\(T=12720\text{ N}=12.72\text{ kN}\); it exceeds \(W\) because a positive upward resultant is required.

Answer: \(T=12.72\text{ kN}\) upward
Q2 W1 A 25 kg trolley is pushed right by 110 N against 40 N resistance. A student says it moves at constant speed. Draw the FBD, diagnose the claim, and find the actual acceleration.
FBD — equilibrium claim under audit
Sign convention

+x right; +y upward.

Working

Vertical forces close: \(N-W=0\).

Horizontal resultant \(=110-40=70\text{ N}\), not zero.

\(a=70/25=2.80\text{ m/s}^2\) right, so constant speed is impossible under the stated forces.

Answer: claim rejected; \(a=2.80\text{ m/s}^2\) right
Q3 W2 The bracket shown has forces at 450 mm and 0.800 m from O. Redraw the moment diagram, convert units consistently, and find the signed net moment about O.
Given diagram

The 600 N force acts downward; the 350 N force acts upward.

Moment audit — competing rotations
Sign convention

Anticlockwise positive.

Working

\(450\text{ mm}=0.450\text{ m}\).

\(M_O=-600(0.450)+350(0.800)=-270+280\).

\(M_O=+10.0\text{ N·m}\), a small anticlockwise remainder; the near-cancellation is a useful reasonableness check.

Answer: \(M_O=+10.0\text{ N·m}\) (anticlockwise)
Q4 W2 A 500 N force acts at 35° to a 0.60 m handle. Draw the perpendicular-component sketch, find the moment about the pivot, and diagnose the error in using \(500(0.60)\).
Moment diagram — only the perpendicular component turns
Sign convention

Anticlockwise positive.

Working

\(F_\perp=500\sin35^\circ=286.79\text{ N}\).

\(M_O=F_\perp L=286.79(0.60)=172.07\text{ N·m}\).

\(500(0.60)\) wrongly treats the whole force as perpendicular to the handle.

Answer: \(M_O=172.07\text{ N·m}\); using \(FL\) directly overstates it
Q5 W3 Three concurrent forces are shown. Sketch their component table and resultant on axes, then find the resultant magnitude and direction from +x.
Given diagram

Angles are measured anticlockwise from +x.

Vector polygon — resultant of three forces
Sign convention

Resolve into signed x- and y-components first.

Working

\(R_x=80+65\cos120^\circ+50\cos240^\circ=22.50\text{ N}\).

\(R_y=65\sin120^\circ+50\sin240^\circ=12.99\text{ N}\).

\(|R|=\sqrt{22.50^2+12.99^2}=25.98\text{ N}\), \(\theta=\tan^{-1}(12.99/22.50)=30.00^\circ\).

Answer: \(R=25.98\text{ N}\) at \(30.00^\circ\) from +x
Q6 W3 Two forces are \((35,-20)\) N and \((-12,44)\) N. Draw the force and equilibrant vectors, then find the equilibrant in component, magnitude, and direction form.
Vector equilibrium — resultant and opposite equilibrant
Sign convention

Direction measured anticlockwise from +x.

Working

\(R=(35-12,\,-20+44)=(23,24)\text{ N}\).

\(E=-R=(-23,-24)\text{ N}\).

\(|E|=\sqrt{23^2+24^2}=33.24\text{ N}\); \(\theta_E=180^\circ+\tan^{-1}(24/23)=226.22^\circ\).

Answer: \(E=(-23,-24)\text{ N}=33.24\text{ N at }226.22^\circ\)
Q7 W4 A stepped tie carries 54 kN tension through areas 450 mm² and 300 mm². Sketch both cut sections, calculate both stresses, and decide whether each passes for yield 320 MPa with FoS 2.0.
Stepped axial tie — the smaller area governs
Sign convention

Tension positive; use N and mm so stress is in MPa.

Working

\(\sigma_{allow}=320/2=160\text{ MPa}\).

\(\sigma_1=54000/450=120\text{ MPa} \lt 160\text{ MPa}\): pass.

\(\sigma_2=54000/300=180\text{ MPa} \gt 160\text{ MPa}\): fail. A single failed segment means the tie fails.

Answer: 450 mm² segment passes; 300 mm² segment fails
Q8 W4 A 1.80 m steel bar, area 500 mm², carries 40 kN tension; \(E=200\) GPa. Draw the elongation sketch, calculate extension, and diagnose a reported answer of 720 mm.
Axial deformation — consistent N-mm-MPa units
Sign convention

Convert \(L\) to mm and \(E\) to N/mm².

Working

\(\Delta L=FL/(AE)\).

\(\Delta L=40000(1800)/[500(200000)]=0.720\text{ mm}\).

The 720 mm report is \(1000\times\) too large: metres and millimetres were mixed.

Answer: \(\Delta L=0.720\text{ mm}\); 720 mm is a unit error
Q9 W5 A 7 m simply supported beam carries 10 kN at 2 m and 18 kN at 5 m from A. Draw the loaded beam and SFD, find reactions, and verify shear closes to zero.
Loaded beam and SFD — two point-load steps
Sign convention

Upward forces produce positive shear on the global sweep.

Working

\(R_B(7)=10(2)+18(5)=110\Rightarrow R_B=15.714\text{ kN}\).

\(R_A=28-15.714=12.286\text{ kN}\).

SFD levels: \(+12.286\), then \(+2.286\), then \(-15.714\), then \(0\) after \(R_B\). Closure confirms \(\Sigma F_y=0\).

Answer: \(R_A=12.286\text{ kN}\), \(R_B=15.714\text{ kN}\); SFD closes at zero
Q10 W5 An 8 m simply supported beam carries a full 3 kN/m UDL plus 12 kN at \(x=2\) m. Draw the loaded beam and SFD/BMD logic, locate \(V=0\), and find the maximum moment.
BMD — maximum found from zero shear
Sign convention

Sagging moment positive.

Working

UDL resultant \(=3(8)=24\text{ kN}\) at 4 m. \(R_B(8)=24(4)+12(2)\), so \(R_B=15\text{ kN}\), \(R_A=21\text{ kN}\).

For \(x \gt 2\): \(V=21-12-3x=9-3x\). Thus \(V=0\) at \(x=3.00\text{ m}\).

\(M(3)=21(3)-12(3-2)-3(3^2)/2=37.50\text{ kN·m}\).

Answer: \(M_{\max}=37.50\text{ kN·m}\) at \(x=3.00\text{ m}\)
Q11 W6 At an unloaded joint, two members are collinear and a third is diagonal. Draw the joint FBD and identify the zero-force member, explaining why the two collinear members need not be zero.
Joint FBD — zero-force member pattern
Sign convention

Resolve perpendicular to the collinear pair.

Working

Only the diagonal has a component perpendicular to the horizontal pair, so equilibrium requires \(F_d=0\).

The two collinear forces can be equal and opposite, so neither is forced to zero.

This conclusion depends on the joint having no external load or support reaction.

Answer: the diagonal is the zero-force member
Q12 W6 A symmetric triangular truss spans 6 m, rises 2 m, and carries 18 kN downward at the apex. Draw the truss and apex/left-joint FBDs, then find each rafter force and the bottom-tie force.
Triangular truss — reactions, rafters and tie
Sign convention

Assume member tension; negative results are compression.

Working

At the unloaded bottom-centre joint, the two bottom-chord members are collinear, so the vertical king-post member is zero-force.

\(\sin\theta=2/\sqrt{3^2+2^2}=2/\sqrt{13}\). At the apex, \(2F\sin\theta=18\), giving \(F=16.225\text{ kN}\) compression.

At the left support, the rafter horizontal component is \(16.225(3/\sqrt{13})=13.50\text{ kN}\).

The bottom tie balances it in tension: \(F_{tie}=13.50\text{ kN}\).

Answer: each rafter 16.225 kN compression; bottom tie 13.50 kN tension
Q13 Beam chain 1/4 For the beam shown, draw the global FBD, replace the partial UDL by its resultant, and determine both support reactions.
Given diagram

Use the dimensions and loads shown; solve reactions before opening the answer.

Beam chain — global reactions
Sign convention

Up positive; moments about A anticlockwise positive.

Working

The UDL becomes \(16\text{ kN}\) at \(x=7\text{ m}\).

\(R_B(9)=18(3)+16(7)=166\Rightarrow R_B=18.444\text{ kN}\).

\(R_A=34-18.444=15.556\text{ kN}\). Check: reactions sum to \(34\text{ kN}\).

Answer: \(R_A=15.556\text{ kN}\), \(R_B=18.444\text{ kN}\)
Q14 Beam chain 2/4 Using Q13, draw the left cut segment at \(x=6\) m and find the signed shear and bending moment there.
Beam chain — cut at x = 6 m
Sign convention

Positive shear is upward on the left cut face; sagging moment positive.

Working

Left of the cut, the UDL covers only \(1\text{ m}\), so its resultant is \(4\text{ kN}\) at \(x=5.5\text{ m}\).

\(V=15.556-18-4=-6.444\text{ kN}\).

\(M=15.556(6)-18(3)-4(0.5)=37.333\text{ kN·m}\).

Answer: \(V(6)=-6.444\text{ kN}\), \(M(6)=37.333\text{ kN·m}\)
Q15 Beam chain 3/4 At the Q14 cut, \(I=32\times10^6\) mm⁴ and \(c=90\) mm. Sketch the section and linear stress distribution, then calculate the extreme-fibre bending stress.
Beam chain — bending stress at the cut
Sign convention

Convert kN·m to N·mm; use the stress magnitude.

Working

\(M=37.333\times10^6\text{ N·mm}\).

\(\sigma_{max}=Mc/I=(37.333\times10^6)(90)/(32\times10^6)\).

\(\sigma_{max}=105.00\text{ MPa}\), tension at one extreme fibre and compression at the other.

Answer: \(|\sigma_{max}|=105.00\text{ MPa}\)
Q16 Beam chain 4/4 The beam material yields at 280 MPa and requires FoS 2.4. Draw the working-versus-allowable check, give a pass/fail verdict, and quantify the reserve margin.
Beam chain — final safety verdict
Sign convention

Pass only when \(\sigma_{work}\leq\sigma_y/\mathrm{FoS}\).

Working

\(\sigma_{allow}=280/2.4=116.667\text{ MPa}\).

\(105.00 \lt 116.667\), so the section passes.

Reserve relative to allowable \(=(116.667-105)/116.667=10.0\%\).

Answer: PASS with 10.0% reserve relative to allowable
Q17 Truss chain 1/3 At joint J the applied 20 kN force has components 12 kN right and 16 kN down. Redraw the prompt as a joint FBD and verify the force magnitude and direction.
Given diagram

Member H is horizontal left; member D is at 120° from +x.

Truss/vector chain — applied load resolution
Sign convention

+x right, +y upward.

Working

\(|P|=\sqrt{12^2+(-16)^2}=20.0\text{ kN}\).

The direction is \(\tan^{-1}(16/12)=53.13^\circ\) below +x.

The signed component form \((12,-16)\) is the safest input for the joint equations.

Answer: \(P=20.0\text{ kN}\), \(53.13^\circ\) below +x
Q18 Truss chain 2/3 Using Q17, draw tension arrows for members H (leftward horizontal) and D (120° from +x), solve both member forces, and classify tension/compression.
Truss/vector chain — joint equilibrium
Sign convention

Assume both unknown member forces pull away from J.

Working

\(\Sigma F_y=0:\ F_D\sin120^\circ-16=0\Rightarrow F_D=18.475\text{ kN}\).

\(\Sigma F_x=0:\ -F_H+F_D\cos120^\circ+12=0\).

\(-F_H-9.238+12=0\Rightarrow F_H=2.762\text{ kN}\). Both are positive, so both are in tension.

Answer: D = 18.475 kN tension; H = 2.762 kN tension
Q19 Truss chain 3/3 Size circular member D from Q18 for an allowable tensile stress of 150 MPa. Sketch the section, find minimum area and diameter, and state a practical rounding rule.
Truss/vector chain — axial member sizing
Sign convention

Use \(A\geq F/\sigma_{allow}\); never round a minimum size down.

Working

\(A_{req}=18475/150=123.17\text{ mm}^2\).

\(d_{min}=\sqrt{4A/\pi}=\sqrt{4(123.17)/\pi}=12.52\text{ mm}\).

Specify the next available stock diameter at or above 12.52 mm, for example 13 mm if available.

Answer: \(A_{req}=123.17\text{ mm}^2\), \(d_{min}=12.52\text{ mm}\)
Q20 Challenge For an 8 m beam with 8 kN at 2 m and 18 kN at 6 m, a solution gives \(R_A=9\) kN and \(R_B=17\) kN. Draw the equilibrium audit, identify what check passes and what fails, then correct the reactions.
Diagnostic FBD — force closure can hide a moment error
Sign convention

Moments about A anticlockwise positive.

Working

The claimed reactions pass force closure: \(9+17=26=8+18\text{ kN}\).

But their moment residual is \(17(8)-8(2)-18(6)=12\text{ kN·m}\neq0\).

Correctly, \(R_B=[8(2)+18(6)]/8=15.5\text{ kN}\), then \(R_A=10.5\text{ kN}\).

Answer: moment check fails by 12 kN·m; \(R_A=10.5\text{ kN}\), \(R_B=15.5\text{ kN}\)
Q21 Challenge A student draws a straight-line BMD for a 6 m simply supported beam under a full 5 kN/m UDL. Sketch the correct SFD and BMD, diagnose the shape error, and mark the maximum moment.
Diagram diagnosis — UDL means linear V and parabolic M
Sign convention

A distributed load changes shear continuously; \(dM/dx=V\).

Working

Reactions are \(wL/2=15\text{ kN}\), so \(V=15-5x\): a straight line from +15 to -15 kN.

Integrating linear shear gives \(M=15x-2.5x^2\), a parabola, not a straight line.

At \(V=0\), \(x=3\text{ m}\), and \(M_{max}=5(6^2)/8=22.5\text{ kN·m}\).

Answer: linear SFD, parabolic BMD; maximum 22.5 kN·m at midspan
Q22 Challenge A 64 kN tie uses area 500 mm²; yield is 250 MPa and FoS is 2.0. Sketch the inequality check, test the proposed area, and find the true minimum area.
Design diagnosis — a near miss is still a failure
Sign convention

Design requires \(F/A\leq\sigma_y/\mathrm{FoS}\).

Working

\(\sigma_{allow}=250/2=125\text{ MPa}\).

Proposed stress \(=64000/500=128\text{ MPa} \gt 125\text{ MPa}\): fail.

\(A_{req}=64000/125=512\text{ mm}^2\). The selected area must be at least 512 mm².

Answer: 500 mm² fails; \(A_{min}=512\text{ mm}^2\)
Q23 Challenge At a joint, a horizontal-right member and a 120° diagonal balance a 10 kN downward load. A student reports the diagonal as 11.55 kN compression. Draw the joint FBD and use component signs to diagnose and correct both member forces.
Truss sign diagnosis — vertical equilibrium decides the sense
Sign convention

Assume tension away from the joint.

Working

\(\Sigma F_y=0:\ F_D\sin120^\circ-10=0\Rightarrow F_D=11.547\text{ kN}\) tension.

\(\Sigma F_x=0:\ F_H+F_D\cos120^\circ=0\Rightarrow F_H=5.774\text{ kN}\) tension.

The reported compression direction gives a downward diagonal component, worsening rather than balancing the applied load.

Answer: diagonal 11.547 kN tension; horizontal 5.774 kN tension
Q24 Capstone A 6 m simply supported beam carries 30 kN at midspan and has a 60 mm by 120 mm rectangular section. Yield is 240 MPa and FoS 2.0. Draw the loaded beam, SFD/BMD and stress sketch; find reactions, maximum moment, bending stress, verdict, and required \(I\) at the same depth.
Capstone synthesis — load to reaction to moment to design
Sign convention

Sagging positive; compare stress with allowable, not directly with yield.

Working

Symmetry gives \(R_A=R_B=15\text{ kN}\). The SFD is +15 then -15 kN; the triangular BMD peaks at \(M_{max}=15(3)=45\text{ kN·m}\).

\(I=bh^3/12=60(120^3)/12=8.64\times10^6\text{ mm}^4\), \(c=60\text{ mm}\).

\(\sigma=Mc/I=(45\times10^6)(60)/(8.64\times10^6)=312.5\text{ MPa}\).

\(\sigma_{allow}=240/2=120\text{ MPa}\), so it fails. At the same \(c\), \(I_{req}=Mc/\sigma_{allow}=22.5\times10^6\text{ mm}^4\), 2.604 times the present \(I\).

Answer: \(R_A=R_B=15\text{ kN}\), \(M_{max}=45\text{ kN·m}\), \(\sigma=312.5\text{ MPa}\): FAIL; \(I_{req}=22.5\times10^6\text{ mm}^4\)

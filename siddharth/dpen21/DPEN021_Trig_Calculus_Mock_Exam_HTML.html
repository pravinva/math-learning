DPEN021 Enabling Mathematics For Engineers — Trigonometry & Calculus Mock Exam

HTML edition with expected answers included for every question.

Exam Instructions

This examination is 90 minutes long. Total marks: 50. All questions are compulsory.

Radian measure should be used for trigonometric calculations unless otherwise specified. Approved calculator permitted.

Section 1 — Multiple Choice (10 marks)

Q1 (1 mark)

A circle has radius 2 cm. Find area of sector with angle \(1.2\) radians.

  1. 4.8 cm\(^2\)
  2. 1.2 cm\(^2\)
  3. 2.4 cm\(^2\)
  4. 0.6 cm\(^2\)
Expected answer: \(A=\frac12r^2\theta=\frac12(2^2)(1.2)=2.4\text{ cm}^2\). Option C.
Q2 (1 mark)

Period of \(y=3\sin(2x)\)?

  1. 2
  2. \(\pi\)
  3. \(2\pi\)
  4. \(\pi/2\)
Expected answer: \(T=\frac{2\pi}{n}=\frac{2\pi}{2}=\pi\). Option B.
Q3 (1 mark)

Derivative of \(f(x)=\cos(3x)\)?

  1. \(3\sin(3x)\)
  2. \(-3\sin(3x)\)
  3. \(-\sin(3x)\)
  4. \(-3\cos(3x)\)
Expected answer: Chain rule: \(\frac{d}{dx}\cos(ax)=-a\sin(ax)\), so \(-3\sin(3x)\). Option B.
Q4 (1 mark)

Pendulum length 1.5 m swings angle 0.2 rad. Arc length?

  1. 0.15 m
  2. 0.45 m
  3. 0.30 m
  4. 7.5 m
Expected answer: \(s=r\theta=1.5(0.2)=0.30\) m. Option C.
Q5 (1 mark)

Transformation from \(y=\cos x\) to \(y=\cos x-2\)?

  1. Left 2
  2. Right 2
  3. Up 2
  4. Down 2
Expected answer: Vertical shift down by 2. Option D.
Q6 (1 mark)

Express \(\log_a x+\log_a y-3\log_a z\) as one log.

  1. \(\log_a(x+y-3z)\)
  2. \(\log_a\left(\frac{x+y}{z^3}\right)\)
  3. \(\log_a\left(\frac{xy}{z^3}\right)\)
  4. \(\log_a\left(\frac{xy}{3z}\right)\)
Expected answer: \(\log_a(xy)-\log_a(z^3)=\log_a\left(\frac{xy}{z^3}\right)\). Option C.
Q7 (1 mark)

150° in radians?

  1. \(2\pi/3\)
  2. \(5\pi/3\)
  3. \(3\pi/4\)
  4. \(5\pi/6\)
Expected answer: \(150\cdot\frac{\pi}{180}=\frac{5\pi}{6}\). Option D.
Q8 (1 mark)

\(\int \sin(x)\,dx\) equals?

  1. \(-\sin x+C\)
  2. \(-\cos x+C\)
  3. \(\cos x+C\)
  4. \(\sin x+C\)
Expected answer: \(-\cos(x)+C\). Option B.
Q9 (1 mark)

Maximum value of \(f(x)=5\cos x+2\)?

  1. 7
  2. 3
  3. 2
  4. 5
Expected answer: Max \(=2+5=7\). Option A.
Q10 (1 mark)

Vertical asymptote of \(y=\ln(x-4)\)?

  1. \(x=0\)
  2. \(x=-4\)
  3. \(y=4\)
  4. \(x=4\)
Expected answer: \(x-4=0\Rightarrow x=4\). Option D.

Section 2 — Short Answer (20 marks)

Q11 (2 marks)

Exact perimeter of sector with \(r=6\), \(\theta=\pi/3\).

Expected answer: \(P=2r+r\theta=12+2\pi\) cm.
Q12 (2 marks)

Domain and range of \(f(x)=e^x+4\).

Expected answer: Domain \((-\infty,\infty)\), Range \((4,\infty)\).
Q13 (2 marks)

Exact area under \(y=\cos x\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=\pi/2\).

Expected answer: \(\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx=[\sin x]_0^{\pi/2}=1\).
Q14 (2 marks)

Sketch \(f(x)=2\sin x+3\) on \(0\le x\le 2\pi\), with y-intercept and range.

Expected answer: Intercept \(=3\), max \(=5\), min \(=1\), range \([1,5]\).
Q15 (2 marks)

Solve \(3e^{x-1}=10\) (3 d.p.).

Expected answer: \(x=1+\ln(10/3)\approx2.204\).
Q16 (2 marks)

Differentiate \(y=x^2\sin x\).

Expected answer: \(y'=2x\sin x + x^2\cos x\).
Q17 (2 marks)

If sector has \(s=2r\), find area in terms of \(r\).

Expected answer: \(\theta=s/r=2\), so \(A=\frac12r^2(2)=r^2\).
Q18 (2 marks)

Triangle \(OAB\) in circle of radius 6, \(\angle AOB=\pi/3\). Find area of \(\triangle OAB\).

Expected answer: \(A=\frac12ab\sin C=\frac12(6)(6)\sin(\pi/3)=9\sqrt3\text{ cm}^2\).
Q19 (2 marks)

Solve \(\log_{10}x+\log_{10}(x-1)=1\), valid solution only.

Expected answer: \(x^2-x-10=0\Rightarrow x=\frac{1\pm\sqrt{41}}{2}\). Domain \(x>1\), so \(x=\frac{1+\sqrt{41}}{2}\approx3.70\).
Q20 (2 marks)

Line through \((2,2)\) perpendicular to \(y=1\).

Expected answer: \(y=1\) is horizontal, so perpendicular is vertical through \(x=2\).

Section 3 — Long Answer (20 marks)

Q21 (5 marks)

Two intersecting unit circles centered at \(O(0,0)\) and \(C(1,0)\). Show \(\triangle OAC\) equilateral; find sector \(OAB\); find total logo area.

Expected answer (summary): \(OA=OC=AC=1\Rightarrow\triangle OAC\) equilateral. \(\angle AOB=2\pi/3\), sector area \(=\pi/3\). Intersection area \(=2\left(\frac{\pi}{6}-\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\right)=\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\). Total union area \(=2\pi-\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)=\frac{5\pi}{3}+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\).
Q22 (5 marks)

Tide model \(H(t)=4\cos\left(\frac{\pi}{6}t\right)+6\), \(0\le t\le 12\). Find first low tide and safe-entry times for \(H\ge8\).

Expected answer: Low tide when cosine \(-1\): \(t=6\) h. For \(H\ge8\): \(\cos\left(\frac{\pi t}{6}\right)\ge0.5\), so \(t\in[0,2]\cup[10,12]\).
Q23 (5 marks)

Region under \(y=\sin x\) from \(0\) to \(\pi\), rotated about x-axis. Find volume and \(\frac{dA}{dx}\) at \(x=\pi/4\) where \(A(x)=\pi y^2\).

Expected answer: \(V=\pi\int_0^\pi\sin^2x\,dx=\pi\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{\pi^2}{2}\). Also \(A(x)=\pi\sin^2x\Rightarrow A'(x)=2\pi\sin x\cos x=\pi\sin2x\), hence \(A'(\pi/4)=\pi\).
Q24 (5 marks)

\(P(t)=100e^{kt}\), doubles in 5 h. Find \(k\), time to 1000, and graph features.

Expected answer: \(200=100e^{5k}\Rightarrow k=\frac{\ln2}{5}\). Then \(1000=100e^{kt}\Rightarrow t=\frac{\ln10}{k}=\frac{5\ln10}{\ln2}\approx16.61\) h. Graph is increasing exponential through \((0,100)\).

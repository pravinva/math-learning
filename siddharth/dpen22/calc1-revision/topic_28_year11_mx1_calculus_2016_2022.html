Questions Answers (separate section)

Questions

Q1 · C1.1 Rates of Change (2 marks)

A particle has displacement \(x(t)=t^3-2t^2+3t\) metres. Find its acceleration at \(t=4\) s.

Q2 · C1.1 Rates of Change (3 marks)

For \(x(t)=t^3-2t^2+3t\), find: (i) initial velocity, (ii) times when particle is stationary.

Q3 · C1.1 Rates of Change (2 marks)

A cooler has volume \(V(t)=25\left(1-\frac{t}{60}\right)^2\), \(0\le t\le60\). Find the draining rate \(\frac{dV}{dt}\) when the cooler is one-quarter full.

Q4 · C1.2 Exponential Growth/Decay (3 marks)

Water in a room at \(25^\circ\text{C}\) follows \(\frac{dT}{dt}=k(T-25)\), with \(T(0)=5\), \(T(8)=10\). Estimate the time when \(T=20\).

Q5 · C1.2 Exponential Growth/Decay (2 marks)

A bacteria model is \(N(t)=1000e^{kt}\). Given \(N(20)=2000\), find \(k\) and then \(N(120)\).

Q6 · C1.2 Exponential Growth/Decay (2 marks)

Carbon-14 decays as \(C(t)=Ae^{kt}\). If half-life is 5730 years, find \(k\). Then find the age when \(C(t)=0.9A\).

Q7 · C1.2 Exponential Growth/Decay (2 marks)

A quantity satisfies \(\frac{dx}{dt}=0.004(500-x)\), with \(x(0)=0\). Find \(x(t)\).

Q8 · C1.3 Related Rates (2 marks)

A ripple radius grows at \(\frac{dr}{dt}=5\) cm/s. Find \(\frac{dA}{dt}\) when \(r=15\), where \(A=\pi r^2\).

Q9 · C1.3 Related Rates (3 marks)

A spherical bubble radius increases at \(0.2\) mm/s. Find \(\frac{dV}{dt}\) when \(r=0.6\) mm.

Q10 · C1.3 Related Rates (3 marks)

A pontoon is linked to a jetty by a 4 m walkway. Let \(h\) be vertical difference and \(x\) horizontal separation.

(i) Express \(x\) in terms of \(h\). (ii) Find \(\frac{dx}{dt}\) when \(h=1\) and \(\frac{dh}{dt}=-0.3\) m/h (rising tide).

Q11 · C1.3 Related Rates (3 marks)

A conical dispenser has radius 5 cm, height 20 cm. Soap level has radius \(r\), height \(h\), volume \(V=\frac13\pi r^2h\). Show \(r=\frac{h}{4}\), then find \(\frac{dV}{dh}\).

Q12 · C1.3 Related Rates (3 marks)

Distance \(A\) and \(B\) satisfy \(A=\frac{9}{B}\). If \(\frac{dB}{dt}=0.2\) m/s, find \(\frac{dA}{dt}\) when \(A=12\).

Answers (separate section)

A1. \(v=\frac{dx}{dt}=3t^2-4t+3,\ a=\frac{dv}{dt}=6t-4\). At \(t=4\): \(\boxed{a=20\ \text{m/s}^2}\).
A2. (i) \(v(0)=3\) m/s. (ii) \(3t^2-4t+3=0\) has \(\Delta=16-36=-20<0\), so no real stationary time.
A3. One-quarter full \(\Rightarrow V=25/4\), so \((1-t/60)^2=1/4\), valid \(t=30\). \(\frac{dV}{dt}=25\cdot2(1-t/60)(-1/60)\). At \(t=30\): \(\boxed{\frac{dV}{dt}=-\frac{5}{12}\ \text{L/s}}\).
A4. \(T=25+Ae^{kt}\), \(A=-20\). From \(T(8)=10\): \(e^{8k}=3/4\Rightarrow k=\frac{\ln(3/4)}{8}\). For \(T=20\): \(20=25-20e^{kt}\Rightarrow e^{kt}=1/4\), so \(t=\frac{\ln(1/4)}{k}\approx\boxed{38.6\ \text{min}}\).
A5. \(2000=1000e^{20k}\Rightarrow k=\frac{\ln2}{20}\approx0.0347\). \(N(120)=1000e^{120k}=1000e^{6\ln2}=1000\cdot64=\boxed{64\,000}\).
A6. Half-life: \(e^{5730k}=1/2\Rightarrow k=\frac{\ln(1/2)}{5730}\approx\boxed{-1.21\times10^{-4}}\). For \(0.9A\): \(e^{kt}=0.9\Rightarrow t=\frac{\ln0.9}{k}\approx\boxed{8.7\times10^2\ \text{years}}\).
A7. Solve linear ODE: \(x=500+Ce^{-0.004t}\). \(x(0)=0\Rightarrow C=-500\). \(\boxed{x(t)=500\left(1-e^{-0.004t}\right)}\).
A8. \(A=\pi r^2\Rightarrow \frac{dA}{dt}=2\pi r\frac{dr}{dt}=2\pi(15)(5)=\boxed{150\pi\ \text{cm}^2/\text{s}}\).
A9. \(V=\frac43\pi r^3\Rightarrow \frac{dV}{dt}=4\pi r^2\frac{dr}{dt}=4\pi(0.6)^2(0.2)=\boxed{0.288\pi\approx0.905\ \text{mm}^3/\text{s}}\).
A10. Geometry: \(x^2+h^2=16\Rightarrow x=\sqrt{16-h^2}\). Differentiate: \(2x\frac{dx}{dt}+2h\frac{dh}{dt}=0\Rightarrow \frac{dx}{dt}=-\frac{h}{x}\frac{dh}{dt}\). At \(h=1\), \(x=\sqrt{15}\), \(\frac{dh}{dt}=-0.3\): \(\boxed{\frac{dx}{dt}=\frac{0.3}{\sqrt{15}}\approx0.077\ \text{m/h}}\).
A11. Similar triangles give \(\frac{r}{h}=\frac{5}{20}=\frac14\Rightarrow r=\frac{h}{4}\). Then \(V=\frac13\pi(\frac{h}{4})^2h=\frac{\pi}{48}h^3\), so \(\boxed{\frac{dV}{dh}=\frac{\pi}{16}h^2}\).
A12. \(A=9B^{-1}\Rightarrow \frac{dA}{dt}=-9B^{-2}\frac{dB}{dt}\). When \(A=12\), \(B=9/12=3/4\). So \(\frac{dA}{dt}=-9\cdot\frac{0.2}{(3/4)^2}=-\frac{1.8}{0.5625}=\boxed{-3.2\ \text{m/s}}\).
All answers are rounded only at the final step unless exact form is requested.

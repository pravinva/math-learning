A1

\(A=\frac12rs=\frac12(12)(10\pi)=60\pi\). Correct option: B.

A2

Period \(=\frac{2\pi}{4}=\frac{\pi}{2}\). Option A.

A3

\(\frac{7\pi}{6}\) is QIII with reference \(\pi/6\), so sine is negative: \(-\frac12\). Option B.

A4

\(s=r\theta=9\cdot\frac{\pi}{3}=3\pi\). Option A.

A5

Amplitude \(=|2|=2\). Option B.

A6

For \(\tan(kx)\), period is \(\pi/|k|\). So \(\pi/3\). Option C.

A7

\(s=r\theta=5(0.8)=4\) m. Option B.

A8

\(\cos x=-\sqrt3/2\) at \(x=5\pi/6,7\pi/6\). Given options: \(5\pi/6\). Option B.

B9

Perimeter \(P=2r+r\theta=r(2+\theta)\).

\(35=r\left(2+\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)\Rightarrow r=\frac{35}{2+5\pi/6}\approx8.17\).

B10

\(y=\sin x+1\) is vertical translation up by 1.

Original range \([-1,1]\), new range \([0,2]\).

B11

\(\sin x=\sqrt3/2\Rightarrow x=\pi/3,2\pi/3\) in \([0,2\pi)\).

B12

Amplitude \(=3\). Period \(=2\pi/2=\pi\). Phase shift from \(2x-\pi=2(x-\pi/2)\): right by \(\pi/2\). Vertical shift \(+4\).

B13

\(A=\frac12r^2\theta=\frac12(14^2)(0.9)=88.2\text{ cm}^2\).

B14

\(54=\frac12(6^2)\theta=18\theta\Rightarrow\theta=3\) radians.

B15

\(s=r\theta\Rightarrow\theta=s/r\). Substitute into \(A=\frac12r^2\theta\): \(A=\frac12r^2\frac{s}{r}=\frac12rs\).

B16

Triangle area \(=\frac12r^2\sin\theta=\frac12(6^2)\sin30^\circ=18\cdot\frac12=9\text{ cm}^2\).

C17

Set \(h=4\): \(\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6}(t-1)\right)=0\Rightarrow \frac{\pi}{6}(t-1)=n\pi\Rightarrow t=1+6n\).

In \([0,24]\): \(t=1,7,13,19\).

C18

\(h\ge5.5\Rightarrow \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6}(t-1)\right)\ge0.5\).

Thus angle in \([\pi/6,5\pi/6]+2k\pi\), so \(t\in[2,6]+12k\).

Within \(0\le t\le24\): \(t\in[2,6]\cup[14,18]\).

C19

(i) \(s=r\theta=2.4(1.1)=2.64\) m.

(ii) \(A=\frac12r^2\theta=\frac12(2.4^2)(1.1)=3.168\text{ m}^2\).

(iii) \(d=2r\sin(\theta/2)=4.8\sin(0.55)\approx2.51\) m.

C20

Amplitude 2 means \(|a|=2\). Period \(\pi\Rightarrow b=2\). Crossing origin increasing suggests \(c=0\), positive \(a\).

One valid equation: \(\boxed{y=2\sin(2x)}\).

C21

Equal radii \(r=5\), centre distance \(d=6\). Let \(\theta=\cos^{-1}(3/5)\).

Lens area \(=2\left(\frac12r^2(2\theta)-\frac12r^2\sin(2\theta)\right)=25(2\theta-\sin2\theta)\).

\(\sin2\theta=2\cdot\frac45\cdot\frac35=\frac{24}{25}\), so \(A=50\cos^{-1}(3/5)-24\approx22.36\text{ m}^2\).

C22

\(2\cos(2x-\pi/3)=1\Rightarrow \cos(2x-\pi/3)=\frac12\).

\(2x-\frac{\pi}{3}=2k\pi\pm\frac{\pi}{3}\Rightarrow x=k\pi\) or \(x=k\pi+\frac{\pi}{3}\).

For \(0\le x<2\pi\): \(\boxed{x=0,\frac{\pi}{3},\pi,\frac{4\pi}{3}}\).

C23

Segment area \(=\) sector area \(-\) triangle area:

\(A=\frac12r^2(\theta-\sin\theta)=\frac12(10^2)\left(1.2-\sin1.2\right)=50(1.2-\sin1.2)\text{ cm}^2\approx13.40\text{ cm}^2.\)

C24

For \(y=4\sin(x-\pi/6)+3\): max \(=3+4=7\), min \(=3-4=-1\).

\(y=3\Rightarrow \sin(x-\pi/6)=0\Rightarrow x-\pi/6=n\pi\Rightarrow x=\pi/6+n\pi\).

First positive solution: \(\boxed{x=\pi/6}\).

D25

\(H(t)=6+2\cos(\pi t/6)\).

Low tide at \(\cos(\pi t/6)=-1\Rightarrow t=6\), height \(=4\).

\(H\ge7\Rightarrow \cos(\pi t/6)\ge1/2\Rightarrow \pi t/6\in[-\pi/3,\pi/3]+2k\pi\).

On \(0\le t\le12\): \(\boxed{0\le t\le2 \text{ and } 10\le t\le12}\).

D26

Area \(=\int_0^\pi 2\sin x\,dx=2[-\cos x]_0^\pi=4\).

Average value \(=\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\pi2\sin x\,dx=\boxed{\frac{4}{\pi}}\).

D27

Principal values: \(\arcsin(\sqrt2/2)=\pi/4\), \(\arccos(-1/2)=2\pi/3\), \(\arctan(\sqrt3)=\pi/3\).

D28

(i) Area \(=\frac12\theta(R^2-r^2)=\frac12\cdot\frac{2\pi}{5}(81-25)=\boxed{\frac{56\pi}{5}}\).

(ii) Boundary length \(=R\theta+r\theta+2(R-r)=9\frac{2\pi}{5}+5\frac{2\pi}{5}+8=\boxed{\frac{28\pi}{5}+8}\).

D29

(i) Arc distance \(s=r\theta=14(0.9)=12.6\) m.

(ii) Displacement \(\overline{AB}=2r\sin(\theta/2)=28\sin(0.45)\approx12.18\) m.

D30

\(\sin\theta=3/5\), QII \(\Rightarrow \cos\theta=-4/5\), \(\tan\theta=-3/4\).

\(\sin2\theta=2\sin\theta\cos\theta=2(3/5)(-4/5)=-24/25\).

Triangle area \(=\frac12ab\sin C=\frac12(8)(11)(3/5)=\boxed{\frac{132}{5}}\).

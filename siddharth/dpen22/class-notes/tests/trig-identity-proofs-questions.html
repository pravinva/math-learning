Trigonometric Identity Proof Tests

Eleven focused tests of ten proofs each. Every question gives a left-hand side and a right-hand side. Choose the identity pattern, work on the left-hand side, and finish at the right-hand side.

Exam level: the official sample identity (Q14) uses a conjugate \(1\pm\sin\theta\) and a Pythagorean identity. Drill Sets 1–3 (core) then Sets 4–7 (conjugates and squared binomials). Sets 8–9 are power-reduction and double-angle, useful for equations more than for the sample proof. Sets 10–11 are extra stretch.

Set 1 — Core Identity Recognition

10 questions · suggested time: 35 minutes
  • Prove each identity by transforming the LHS into the RHS.
  • Write the identity used beside each major step.
  • Assume all expressions are taken where both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos^2x}{\sin x}=\sin x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sec^2x-1}{\tan x}=\tan x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 2x}{\sin x}=2\sin x.\)[3 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\cos 2x}{\cos x}=2\cos x.\)[3 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin 2x}{1+\cos 2x}=\tan x.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 2x}{\sin 2x}=\tan x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle (1+\tan^2x)\cos^2x=1.\)[3 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle (\csc x-\cot x)(\csc x+\cot x)=1.\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}=\frac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}.\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x+\cos x)^2=1+\sin 2x.\)[4 marks]

Set 2 — Core Identities Only

10 questions · suggested time: 35 minutes
  • Use only the Pythagorean identities, reciprocal identities, and quotient identities.
  • No double-angle, no sum-to-product, no compound-angle formulas.
  • Write the identity used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\tan x}{\sec x}=\sin x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\csc x}{\sec x}=\cot x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin x+\cos x}{\cos x}=1+\tan x.\)[3 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin x}{\csc x}+\frac{\cos x}{\sec x}=1.\)[3 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos^2x}{\sin x}+\sin x=\csc x.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sec^2x}{\tan^2x}=\csc^2x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\tan^2x}{1+\cot^2x}=\tan^2x.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle \sin^4x+2\sin^2x\cos^2x+\cos^4x=1.\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\tan^2x}{\sec x-1}=\sec x+1.\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin^3x+\cos^3x}{\sin x+\cos x}=1-\sin x\cos x.\)[5 marks]

Set 3 — Core Practice (Exam Pitch)

10 questions · suggested time: 35 minutes
  • Core identities only: Pythagorean, reciprocal, and quotient. This is the usual exam proof pitch.
  • No double-angle and no sum-to-product on this paper.
  • Write the identity used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos^2x}{\cos^2x}=\tan^2x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle (\csc x-1)(\csc x+1)=\cot^2x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \cos x\tan x=\sin x.\)[3 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sec^2x-1}{\sec^2x}=\sin^2x.\)[3 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\csc^2x-1}{\csc^2x}=\cos^2x.\)[3 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x+\cos x)^2+(\sin x-\cos x)^2=2.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin^4x-\cos^4x}{\sin^2x-\cos^2x}=1.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\sin x}{\cos x}=\frac{\cos x}{1+\sin x}.\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin x}{\csc x}-\frac{\cos x}{\sec x}=\sin^2x-\cos^2x.\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \tan x+\cot x=\sec x\csc x.\)[4 marks]

Set 4 — Conjugate Binomials

10 questions · suggested time: 40 minutes
  • Use \((a-b)(a+b)=a^2-b^2\), or multiply by a conjugate \(1\pm\sin x\) or \(1\pm\cos x\).
  • Write the identity used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sec x-\tan x)(\sec x+\tan x)=1.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle (1-\sin x)(1+\sin x)=\cos^2x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle (1-\cos x)(1+\cos x)=\sin^2x.\)[3 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sec x-1)(\sec x+1)=\tan^2x.\)[3 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}=\csc x-\cot x.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos x}{1-\sin x}=\sec x+\tan x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cot^2x}{\csc x+1}=\csc x-1.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle (1+\tan x)(1-\tan x)=2-\sec^2x.\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{\sec x+\tan x}=\sec x-\tan x.\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\tan x}{1+\tan x}=\frac{\cot x-1}{\cot x+1}.\)[4 marks]

Set 5 — Conjugate Binomials (Exam Pitch)

10 questions · suggested time: 40 minutes
  • Multiply by a conjugate \(1\pm\sin x\) or \(1\pm\cos x\), or expand \((a-b)(a+b)\).
  • This is the move used on the official sample identity. Write the identity used beside each major step.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos^2x}{1-\sin x}=1+\sin x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin^2x}{1-\cos x}=1+\cos x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\sin x}{\cos^2x}=\frac{1}{1+\sin x}.\)[4 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos x}{\sin^2x}=\frac{1}{1+\cos x}.\)[4 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sec x+\tan x)(1-\sin x)=\cos x.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle (\csc x-\cot x)(1+\cos x)=\sin x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{\csc x+\cot x}=\csc x-\cot x.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle (\tan x-1)(\tan x+1)=\sec^2x-2.\)[3 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle (\cot x-1)(\cot x+1)=\csc^2x-2.\)[3 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\sin x}{\cos x}=\frac{\cos x}{1-\sin x}.\)[4 marks]

Set 6 — Squared Binomials

10 questions · suggested time: 40 minutes
  • Expand \((a\pm b)^2\), then match Pythagorean or double-angle pieces.
  • Write the identity used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x-\cos x)^2=1-\sin 2x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x+\cos x)^2-(\sin x-\cos x)^2=2\sin 2x.\)[4 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sec x-\tan x)^2=\frac{1-\sin x}{1+\sin x}.\)[5 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle (\csc x+\cot x)^2=\frac{1+\cos x}{1-\cos x}.\)[5 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x+\cos x)^2-2\sin 2x=(\sin x-\cos x)^2.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle (\tan x+1)^2+(\tan x-1)^2=2\sec^2x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle (\cot x+1)^2+(\cot x-1)^2=2\csc^2x.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle 1+(\sin x+\cos x)^2=2+\sin 2x.\)[3 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle 2-(\sin x-\cos x)^2=1+\sin 2x.\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle (\csc x+\sin x)^2-(\csc x-\sin x)^2=4.\)[4 marks]

Set 7 — Squared Binomials (Exam Pitch)

10 questions · suggested time: 40 minutes
  • Expand \((a\pm b)^2\), then use a Pythagorean identity.
  • Write the identity used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle (1+\sin x)^2+(1-\sin x)^2=2+2\sin^2x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle (1+\cos x)^2+(1-\cos x)^2=2+2\cos^2x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle (1+\tan x)^2=\sec^2x+2\tan x.\)[3 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle (1-\cot x)^2=\csc^2x-2\cot x.\)[3 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sec x+\tan x)^2+(\sec x-\tan x)^2=4\sec^2x-2.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle (\csc x+\cot x)^2+(\csc x-\cot x)^2=4\csc^2x-2.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle (2\sin x+\cos x)^2+(\sin x-2\cos x)^2=5.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x+1)^2-\cos^2x=2\sin x(1+\sin x).\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle (\cos x-1)^2-\sin^2x=2\cos x(\cos x-1).\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sin x+3)^2+\cos^2x=10+6\sin x.\)[3 marks]

Set 8 — Power-Reduction Forms

10 questions · suggested time: 35 minutes
  • Use \(1-\cos 2u=2\sin^2u\) and \(1+\cos 2u=2\cos^2u\).
  • Write the form used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 2x}{2}=\sin^2x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\cos 2x}{2}=\cos^2x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{2\sin^2x}{1+\cos 2x}=\tan^2x.\)[4 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{2\cos^2x}{1-\cos 2x}=\cot^2x.\)[4 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 4x}{2}=\sin^22x.\)[3 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\cos 4x}{2}=\cos^22x.\)[3 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin^2x}{1-\cos 2x}=\frac12.\)[3 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos^2x}{1+\cos 2x}=\frac12.\)[3 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 2x}{\sin^2x}=2.\)[3 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\cos 2x}{\cos^2x}=2.\)[3 marks]

Set 9 — Double-Angle Identities

10 questions · suggested time: 40 minutes
  • Use \(\sin 2x=2\sin x\cos x\) and the three forms of \(\cos 2x\).
  • Write the form used beside each major step. Assume both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin 2x}{2\sin x}=\cos x.\)[3 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin 2x}{2\cos x}=\sin x.\)[3 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \cos 2x=2\cos^2x-1.\)[3 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \cos 2x=1-2\sin^2x.\)[3 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \sin 2x\cot x=2\cos^2x.\)[4 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \sin 2x\tan x=2\sin^2x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{2\tan x}{1+\tan^2x}=\sin 2x.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\tan^2x}{1+\tan^2x}=\cos 2x.\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \cos 2x+2\sin^2x=1.\)[3 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\tan 2x}{2\tan x}=\frac{1}{1-\tan^2x}.\)[4 marks]

Set 10 — Fractions, Conjugates and Angle Pairing

10 questions · suggested time: 45 minutes
  • Expect reciprocal identities, common denominators, conjugates, and sum-to-product formulas.
  • Choose the side with more structure as the starting side.
  • Assume all expressions are taken where both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \sec x-\tan x=\frac{\cos x}{1+\sin x}.\)[4 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1}{1-\sin x}-\frac{1}{1+\sin x}=2\tan x\sec x.\)[5 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin x}{1-\cos x}-\frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}=2\cot x.\)[5 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle (\tan x+\cot x)^2=\sec^2x\,\csc^2x.\)[5 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos x-\cos 3x}{\sin 3x+\sin x}=\tan x.\)[5 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin 3x+\sin x}{\cos x-\cos 3x}=\cot x.\)[5 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\cos x}{\sin x}=\csc x+\cot x.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle (\sec x+\tan x)^2=\frac{1+\sin x}{1-\sin x}.\)[5 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin A+\sin B}{\cos A+\cos B}=\tan\!\left(\frac{A+B}{2}\right).\)[5 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos A-\cos B}{\sin A+\sin B}=-\tan\!\left(\frac{A-B}{2}\right).\)[5 marks]

Set 11 — Mixed Multi-Step Pattern Selection

10 questions · suggested time: 50 minutes
  • Several questions require two identity families in sequence.
  • Record the visual clue, the first identity chosen, and the simplification route.
  • Assume all expressions are taken where both sides are defined.
  1. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos 2x+\cos 4x}{\sin 4x+\sin 2x}=\cot 3x.\)[5 marks]
  2. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sin 4x}{\sin x\cos x}=4\cos 2x.\)[5 marks]
  3. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 4x}{1-\cos 2x}=4\cos^2x.\)[5 marks]
  4. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\cos 4x-\cos 2x}{\sin 4x+\sin 2x}=-\tan x.\)[5 marks]
  5. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\tan x-\sin x}{\tan x+\sin x}=\frac{1-\cos x}{1+\cos x}.\)[5 marks]
  6. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\sec x-\cos x}{\tan x}=\sin x.\)[4 marks]
  7. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{\csc x-\sin x}{\cot x}=\cos x.\)[4 marks]
  8. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1+\sin 2x}{\sin x+\cos x}=\sin x+\cos x.\)[4 marks]
  9. Prove \(\displaystyle \frac{1-\cos 2x}{1+\cos 2x}=\tan^2x.\)[4 marks]
  10. Prove \(\displaystyle \left(\frac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}\right)^2=\frac{1-\cos x}{1+\cos x}.\)[5 marks]

Checking rule: a proof must show a continuous chain from the LHS to the RHS. Testing a few numerical angles can detect an error, but it does not prove the identity.

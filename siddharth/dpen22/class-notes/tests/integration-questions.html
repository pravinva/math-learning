Questions 1–6: Multiple choice

  1. [Primitive] Best first step for \(\displaystyle\int\left(x-\dfrac{3}{x}\right)^2 dx\)?
    (A) Substitution
    (B) Expand
    (C) Differentiate first
    (D) Common denominator
    (E) none
  2. [Power of \(x\)] \(\displaystyle\int\left(\sqrt{x}+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}+\ln|x|+C\)

    (C) \(x^{1/2}-\dfrac{1}{x}+C\)
    (D) \(\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}-\dfrac{1}{x}+C\)
    (E) other
  3. [Definite] \(\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{2}(2x^3+2)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(14\)
    (B) \(25/2\)
    (C) \(9\)
    (D) \(12\)
    (E) \(27/2\)
  4. [Area] Area between \(f(x)=x^3-4x^2-x+4\) and the \(x\)-axis on its roots needs
    (A) one integral only
    (B) split where \(f\) changes sign
    (C) ignore negatives
    (D) differentiate
    (E) none
  5. [Exp/log/trig] \(\displaystyle\int 3\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)dx=\)
    (A) \(9\sin(x/3)+C\)
    (B) \(3\sin(x/3)+C\)
    (C) \(\sin(3x)+C\)
    (D) \(\tfrac13\sin(x/3)+C\)
    (E) \(-3\sin(x/3)+C\)
  6. [Substitution] For \(\displaystyle\int(6x-2)\sqrt{3x^2-2x}\,dx\), a good substitution is
    (A) \(u=6x-2\)
    (B) \(u=3x^2-2x\)
    (C) \(u=\sqrt{x}\)
    (D) \(u=3x\)
    (E) \(u=x^2\)

Questions 7–16: Long answer

  1. [Applications of primitive] If \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=1+3x\) and the curve passes through \((4,10)\), find \(y\).[2 marks]
  2. [Power of \(x\)] Find \(\displaystyle\int(9x^5+3x^2)\,dx\) in positive-index form.[2 marks]
  3. [Power of \(x\)] Find \(\displaystyle\int\left(3x^3-\dfrac{4}{x}\right)dx\).[2 marks]
  4. [Area] Find the area between \(y=x-x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis from \(0\) to \(1\).
    [3 marks]
  5. [Area] Use algebra to show that \(y=x^2\) and \(y=2-x^2\) meet at \((\pm1,1)\), then find the area of the region bounded by the graphs shown below.
    [4 marks]
  6. [Exp/log/trig] Find \(\displaystyle\int(e^{2x}+4x)\,dx\).[3 marks]
  7. [Exp/log/trig] Find \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{5+x}\,dx\).[2 marks]
  8. [Definite exp/log/trig] Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}(1-\sin2x)\,dx\).[4 marks]

  10. [Substitution] Use substitution to evaluate \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt{x^2+9}}\,dx\).[3 marks]

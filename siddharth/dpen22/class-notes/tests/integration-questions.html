Test 1

  1. Best first step for \(\displaystyle\int\left(x-\dfrac{3}{x}\right)^2 dx\)?
    (A) Substitution
    (B) Expand
    (C) Differentiate first
    (D) Common denominator
    (E) none
  2. \(\displaystyle\int\left(\sqrt{x}+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}+\ln|x|+C\)
    (B) \(\tfrac{3}{2}x^{3/2}+\ln|x|+C\)
    (C) \(x^{1/2}-\dfrac{1}{x}+C\)
    (D) \(\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}-\dfrac{1}{x}+C\)
    (E) other
  3. \(\displaystyle\int_{-1}^{2}(2x^3+2)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(14\)
    (B) \(25/2\)
    (C) \(9\)
    (D) \(12\)
    (E) \(27/2\)
  4. Area between \(f(x)=x^3-4x^2-x+4\) and \(x\)-axis on roots needs
    (A) one integral only
    (B) split where \(f\) changes sign
    (C) ignore negatives
    (D) differentiate
    (E) none
  5. \(\displaystyle\int 3\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)dx=\)
    (A) \(9\sin(x/3)+C\)
    (B) \(3\sin(x/3)+C\)
    (C) \(\sin(3x)+C\)
    (D) \(\tfrac13\sin(x/3)+C\)
    (E) \(-3\sin(x/3)+C\)
  6. If \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=1+3x\) and the curve passes through \((4,10)\), find \(y\).
  7. Find \(\displaystyle\int(9x^5+3x^2)\,dx\) in positive-index form.
  8. Find \(\displaystyle\int\left(3x^3-\dfrac{4}{x}\right)dx\) with positive indices/surds as needed.
  9. Area between \(y=x-x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis from \(0\) to \(1\).
  10. Show intersection of \(y=x^2\) and \(y=2-x^2\) at \((\pm1,1)\) and find the enclosed area.
  11. Find \(\displaystyle\int(e^{2x}+4x)\,dx\).
  12. Use \(u=x^2-3\) (or suitable) to evaluate \(\displaystyle\int 2x(x^2-3)^3\,dx\).

Test 2

  1. \(\displaystyle\int x^{1/2}\,dx=\)
    (A) \(\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}+C\)
    (B) \(\tfrac{1}{2}x^{-1/2}+C\)
    (C) \(x^{3/2}+C\)
    (D) \(\tfrac{3}{2}x^{3/2}+C\)
    (E) \(2x^{1/2}+C\)
  2. \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx=\)
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(1\)
    (C) \(-1\)
    (D) \(\pi/2\)
    (E) \(2\)
  3. For \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x}{x^2+5}\,dx\) a good substitution is
    (A) \(u=2x\)
    (B) \(u=x^2+5\)
    (C) \(u=x^2\)
    (D) \(u=\ln x\)
    (E) \(u=5\)
  4. \(\displaystyle\int e^{3x}\,dx=\)
    (A) \(3e^{3x}+C\)
    (B) \(\tfrac13 e^{3x}+C\)
    (C) \(e^{3x}+C\)
    (D) \(e^{x}/3+C\)
    (E) \(-3e^{3x}+C\)
  5. Area under \(y=\sqrt{x}\) from \(0\) to \(4\) equals
    (A) \(8/3\)
    (B) \(16/3\)
    (C) \(4\)
    (D) \(8\)
    (E) \(2\)
  6. Gradient \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=2x-5\), passes through \((3,1)\). Find the curve.
  7. \(\displaystyle\int\left(x^4-\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)dx\).
  8. \(\displaystyle\int_1^{4}\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx\).
  9. Area between \(y=4-x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis (full positive region).
  10. \(\displaystyle\int(3\cos2x-2\sin x)\,dx\).
  11. \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{5+x}\,dx\).
  12. Substitution for \(\displaystyle\int\cos2x\cdot(2\sin2x)^3\,dx\): choose \(u\) and finish.

Test 3

  1. \(\displaystyle\int(2x+1)^5\,dx\) (by inspection/chain reverse) =
    (A) \(\tfrac16(2x+1)^6+C\)
    (B) \(\tfrac{1}{12}(2x+1)^6+C\)
    (C) \(5(2x+1)^4+C\)
    (D) \(\tfrac15(2x+1)^6+C\)
    (E) \((2x+1)^6+C\)
  2. \(\displaystyle\int_0^{1}(3x^2-1)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(1\)
    (C) \(2\)
    (D) \(-1\)
    (E) \(1/2\)
  3. \(\displaystyle\int\sin(3x)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(-\tfrac13\cos3x+C\)
    (B) \(\tfrac13\cos3x+C\)
    (C) \(-\cos3x+C\)
    (D) \(3\cos3x+C\)
    (E) \(\sin3x+C\)
  4. Best \(u\) for \(\displaystyle\int x e^{x^2}\,dx\)
    (A) \(u=x\)
    (B) \(u=x^2\)
    (C) \(u=e^{x}\)
    (D) \(u=xe^{x}\)
    (E) \(u=2x\)
  5. FTC: if \(F'=f\) then \(\displaystyle\int_a^b f=\)
    (A) \(F(a)-F(b)\)
    (B) \(F(b)-F(a)\)
    (C) \(F(ab)\)
    (D) \(F'(b)-F'(a)\)
    (E) \(f(b)-f(a)\)
  6. Curve with \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{1}{x}\) through \((e,2)\). Find \(y\).
  7. \(\displaystyle\int\left(6x^{1/2}-x^{-1/2}\right)dx\).
  8. \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}(1-\sin2x)\,dx\) exact value.
  9. Area between \(y=x\) and \(y=x^2\) on \([0,1]\).
  10. \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{4}{2x+1}\,dx\).
  11. Find \(\displaystyle\int 5e^{-x}\,dx\).
  12. Use substitution to find \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x}{\sqrt{x^2+9}}\,dx\).

Test 4

  1. \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{x^2}\,dx=\)
    (A) \(\ln|x|+C\)
    (B) \(-\dfrac{1}{x}+C\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{1}{x}+C\)
    (D) \(x^{-3}/(-3)+C\)
    (E) \(2/x+C\)
  2. \(\displaystyle\int_0^{2}(x^2+1)\,dx=\)
    (A) \(8/3\)
    (B) \(14/3\)
    (C) \(4\)
    (D) \(6\)
    (E) \(10/3\)
  3. \(\displaystyle\int\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)dx=\)
    (A) \(2\sin(x/2)+C\)
    (B) \(\tfrac12\sin(x/2)+C\)
    (C) \(-\sin(x/2)+C\)
    (D) \(2\cos(x/2)+C\)
    (E) \(\sin x+C\)
  4. Area under \(y=e^{x}\) from \(0\) to \(1\) is
    (A) \(e-1\)
    (B) \(e\)
    (C) \(1\)
    (D) \(e+1\)
    (E) \(1/e\)
  5. For \(\displaystyle\int(6x-2)\sqrt{3x^2-2x}\,dx\), a good substitution is
    (A) \(u=6x-2\)
    (B) \(u=3x^2-2x\)
    (C) \(u=\sqrt{x}\)
    (D) \(u=3x\)
    (E) \(u=x^2\)
  6. If \(y'=4x^3-2\) and \(y(1)=5\), find \(y\).
  7. \(\displaystyle\int\left(x^{3}-\dfrac{2}{x^{3}}\right)dx\).
  8. \(\displaystyle\int_{-2}^{1}(3x^2)\,dx\).
  9. Area between \(y=\sin x\) and the \(x\)-axis from \(0\) to \(\pi\).
  10. \(\displaystyle\int(2\sec^2 x-3\cos x)\,dx\).
  11. \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x}{x^2+1}\,dx\).
  12. Substitution: evaluate \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin x}\,dx\) (or \(\displaystyle\int\cot x\,dx\)).

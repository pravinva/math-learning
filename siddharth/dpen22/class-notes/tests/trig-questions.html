Test 1

  1. In a right triangle, adjacent side \(5\) m and angle \(13^\circ45'\). Find the hypotenuse correct to 2 d.p.
    (A) \(1.19\) m
    (B) \(5.15\) m
    (C) \(21.04\) m
    (D) \(4.86\) m
    (E) \(20.43\) m
  2. What is the reference angle for \(\theta=-135^\circ\)?
    (A) \(135^\circ\)
    (B) \(225^\circ\)
    (C) \(315^\circ\)
    (D) \(45^\circ\)
    (E) \(90^\circ\)
  3. If \(\sec\theta<0\) and \(\cot\theta>0\), which quadrant contains \(\theta\)?
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(\dfrac{11\pi}{12}\) to degrees.
    (A) \(165^\circ\)
    (B) \(195^\circ\)
    (C) \(150^\circ\)
    (D) \(135^\circ\)
    (E) \(15^\circ\)
  5. Starting from \(y=\cos x\): amplitude \(3\), period \(4\pi\), shift down \(2\). The equation is
    (A) \(3\cos(4\pi x)-2\)
    (B) \(3\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)-2\)
    (C) \(3\cos(2x)-2\)
    (D) \(2\cos(4\pi x)-3\)
    (E) \(2\cos(3x)-2\)
  6. Simplify \((1-\sin^2 x)\sec^2 x\).
  7. Hypotenuse \(15\) m, adjacent \(7\) m. Find the included angle to the nearest minute.
  8. If \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{3}{5}\) and \(\theta\) is acute, find the exact value of \(\sec\theta\).
  9. Find \(x\) if \(\sin 80^\circ=\cos(90^\circ-2x)\).
  10. Find the exact value of \(\tan\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\).
  11. Solve \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\). Exact answers in degrees.
  12. Solve \(\cot\theta=\sqrt{3}\) for \(0\le\theta\le2\pi\). Exact answers in radians.

Test 2

  1. Adjacent \(8\) m, angle \(22^\circ\). Find the opposite side to 2 d.p.
    (A) \(3.23\) m
    (B) \(7.42\) m
    (C) \(21.20\) m
    (D) \(8.64\) m
    (E) \(19.80\) m
  2. Reference angle for \(\theta=210^\circ\)?
    (A) \(30^\circ\)
    (B) \(60^\circ\)
    (C) \(150^\circ\)
    (D) \(210^\circ\)
    (E) \(45^\circ\)
  3. If \(\sin\theta>0\) and \(\tan\theta<0\), \(\theta\) lies in quadrant
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(240^\circ\) to radians (exact).
    (A) \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\)
    (E) \(\pi\)
  5. \(y=\sin x\) stretched to amplitude \(2\), period \(\pi\), up \(1\):
    (A) \(2\sin(2x)+1\)
    (B) \(2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)+1\)
    (C) \(\sin(2x)+2\)
    (D) \(2\sin(\pi x)+1\)
    (E) \(2\sin x-1\)
  6. Simplify \(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot\cos\theta\sec\theta\).
  7. Opposite \(9\) m, hypotenuse \(15\) m. Find the angle opposite the \(9\) m side to the nearest degree.
  8. If \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{5}{13}\) and \(\theta\) acute, find exact \(\tan\theta\).
  9. Find \(x\) if \(\cos 35^\circ=\sin(2x)\).
  10. Exact value of \(\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\).
  11. Solve \(2\sin\theta=-1\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  12. Sketch \(y=2\cos 3x\) on \([0,2\pi]\) and state amplitude and period.

Test 3

  1. Angle of elevation \(18^\circ\) from a point \(40\) m from a tower base. Tower height to 1 d.p.?
    (A) \(12.4\) m
    (B) \(13.0\) m
    (C) \(38.0\) m
    (D) \(123.1\) m
    (E) \(41.2\) m
  2. Reference angle for \(\theta=-\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\)?
    (A) \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)
  3. \(\csc\theta<0\) and \(\cos\theta>0\): quadrant?
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(\dfrac{7\pi}{4}\) to degrees.
    (A) \(135^\circ\)
    (B) \(225^\circ\)
    (C) \(315^\circ\)
    (D) \(405^\circ\)
    (E) \(45^\circ\)
  5. \(y=\cos x\): amplitude \(4\), period \(2\pi\), left shift \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\), up \(1\):
    (A) \(4\cos\!\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)+1\)
    (B) \(4\cos(2x)+1\)
    (C) \(4\cos\!\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)-1\)
    (D) \(\cos\!\left(4x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)+1\)
    (E) \(4\cos x+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)
  6. Simplify \(1+\tan^2\theta\) using an identity.
  7. A ship is \(2.5\) km from a lighthouse. Angle of elevation to the top is \(12^\circ\). Find the lighthouse height to 2 d.p.
  8. If \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{8}{15}\) and \(\theta\) acute, find exact \(\sin\theta\).
  9. Find exact \(\cos\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\).
  10. Solve \(\tan\theta=1\) for \(0\le\theta\le2\pi\).
  11. Solve \(2\cos\theta=\sqrt{3}\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  12. Prove \(\dfrac{1-\cos 2\theta}{\sin 2\theta}=\tan\theta\) (or show both sides equal for a chosen \(\theta\) and outline the identity steps).

Test 4

  1. In \(\triangle ABC\), right-angled at \(C\), \(AC=6\), \(BC=8\). Find \(\sin A\).
    (A) \(\dfrac{3}{4}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{4}{5}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{3}{5}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{5}{4}\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{6}{8}\)
  2. Reference angle for \(300^\circ\)?
    (A) \(30^\circ\)
    (B) \(60^\circ\)
    (C) \(120^\circ\)
    (D) \(300^\circ\)
    (E) \(45^\circ\)
  3. \(\sin\theta<0\) and \(\sec\theta<0\): quadrant?
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(75^\circ\) to radians (exact).
    (A) \(\dfrac{5\pi}{12}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{3\pi}{8}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\)
  5. \(y=\sin x\): amplitude \(5\), period \(6\pi\), down \(3\):
    (A) \(5\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)-3\)
    (B) \(5\sin(3x)-3\)
    (C) \(5\sin(6\pi x)-3\)
    (D) \(\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)-5\)
    (E) \(5\sin x-3\)
  6. Simplify \(\cos^2\theta(\sec^2\theta-1)\).
  7. A wire from the top of a \(12\) m pole makes \(35^\circ\) with the ground. Find the wire length to 2 d.p.
  8. If \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{12}{13}\) and \(\theta\) obtuse, find exact \(\cos\theta\).
  9. Exact value of \(\tan\!\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\).
  10. Solve \(\sin 2\theta=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le180^\circ\).
  11. Solve \(2\cos^2\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  12. For \(y=\dfrac12\sin 2x\), state amplitude, period, and the first positive \(x\)-intercept.

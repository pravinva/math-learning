Test 1

Total number of questions: 15
  • Questions 1–5 are multiple choice. Each question is worth 1 mark; marks are awarded for the answer only.
  • Questions 6–15 are long-answer questions. The value of each question is indicated; show all working.

Questions 1–5: Multiple choice

  1. In a right triangle, adjacent side \(5\) m and angle \(13^\circ45'\). Find the hypotenuse correct to 2 d.p.
    (A) \(1.19\) m
    (B) \(5.15\) m
    (C) \(21.04\) m
    (D) \(4.86\) m
    (E) \(20.43\) m
  2. What is the reference angle for \(\theta=-135^\circ\)?
    (A) \(135^\circ\)
    (B) \(225^\circ\)
    (C) \(315^\circ\)
    (D) \(45^\circ\)
    (E) \(90^\circ\)
  3. If \(\sec\theta<0\) and \(\cot\theta>0\), which quadrant contains \(\theta\)?
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(\dfrac{11\pi}{12}\) to degrees.
    (A) \(165^\circ\)
    (B) \(195^\circ\)
    (C) \(150^\circ\)
    (D) \(135^\circ\)
    (E) \(15^\circ\)
  5. Starting from \(y=\cos x\): amplitude \(3\), period \(4\pi\), shift down \(2\). The equation is
    (A) \(3\cos(4\pi x)-2\)
    (B) \(3\cos\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)-2\)
    (C) \(3\cos(2x)-2\)
    (D) \(2\cos(4\pi x)-3\)
    (E) \(2\cos(3x)-2\)

Questions 6–15: Long answer

  1. Simplify \((1-\sin^2 x)\sec^2 x\).[2 marks]
  2. Hypotenuse \(15\) m, adjacent \(7\) m. Find the included angle to the nearest minute.[2 marks]
  3. If \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{3}{5}\) and \(\theta\) is acute, find the exact value of \(\sec\theta\).[2 marks]
  4. Solve \(\sin 80^\circ=\cos(90^\circ-2x)\) for \(0^\circ[2 marks]
  5. Find the exact value of \(\tan\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\).[2 marks]
  6. Solve \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\). Exact answers in degrees.[3 marks]
  7. Solve \(\cot\theta=\sqrt{3}\) for \(0\le\theta\le2\pi\). Exact answers in radians.[3 marks]
  8. Given \(\sin A=\dfrac35\) and \(\cos B=\dfrac{12}{13}\), where \(A\) and \(B\) are acute, find the exact value of \(\cos(A-B)\).[3 marks]
  9. Solve \(2\sin^2\theta-3\sin\theta+1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).[4 marks]
  10. Sketch \(y=3\sin(2x)-1\) for \(0\le x\le2\pi\). State its amplitude, period and range.[4 marks]

Total number of questions: 15
  • Questions 1–5 are multiple choice. Each question is worth 1 mark; marks are awarded for the answer only.
  • Questions 6–15 are long-answer questions. The value of each question is indicated; show all working.

Questions 1–5: Multiple choice

  1. Adjacent \(8\) m, angle \(22^\circ\). Find the opposite side to 2 d.p.
    (A) \(3.23\) m
    (B) \(7.42\) m
    (C) \(21.20\) m
    (D) \(8.64\) m
    (E) \(19.80\) m
  2. Reference angle for \(\theta=210^\circ\)?
    (A) \(30^\circ\)
    (B) \(60^\circ\)
    (C) \(150^\circ\)
    (D) \(210^\circ\)
    (E) \(45^\circ\)
  3. If \(\sin\theta>0\) and \(\tan\theta<0\), \(\theta\) lies in quadrant
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(240^\circ\) to radians (exact).
    (A) \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{4\pi}{3}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\)
    (E) \(\pi\)
  5. \(y=\sin x\) stretched to amplitude \(2\), period \(\pi\), up \(1\):
    (A) \(2\sin(2x)+1\)
    (B) \(2\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{2}\right)+1\)
    (C) \(\sin(2x)+2\)
    (D) \(2\sin(\pi x)+1\)
    (E) \(2\sin x-1\)

Questions 6–15: Long answer

  1. Simplify \(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}\cdot\cos\theta\sec\theta\).[2 marks]
  2. Opposite \(9\) m, hypotenuse \(15\) m. Find the angle opposite the \(9\) m side to the nearest degree.[2 marks]
  3. If \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{5}{13}\) and \(\theta\) acute, find exact \(\tan\theta\).[2 marks]
  4. Solve \(\cos 35^\circ=\sin(2x)\) for \(0^\circ[2 marks]
  5. Exact value of \(\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\).[2 marks]
  6. Solve \(2\sin\theta=-1\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).[3 marks]
  7. Sketch \(y=2\cos 3x\) on \([0,2\pi]\) and state amplitude and period.[3 marks]
  8. Prove \((\sec\theta-\tan\theta)(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)=1\).[3 marks]
  9. Two observation points on level ground are \(20\) m apart and lie on the same straight line from a tower. Their angles of elevation to the top are \(45^\circ\) and \(30^\circ\). Find the tower height to 2 d.p.[4 marks]
  10. Sketch \(y=2\cos\!\left(3x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)+1\) for \(0\le x\le2\pi\). State its amplitude, period, phase shift and range.[4 marks]

    (C) \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)
  3. \(\csc\theta<0\) and \(\cos\theta>0\): quadrant?
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4

Questions 6–15: Long answer

  1. Simplify \(1+\tan^2\theta\) using an identity.[2 marks]
  2. A ship is \(2.5\) km from a lighthouse. Angle of elevation to the top is \(12^\circ\). Find the lighthouse height to 2 d.p.[2 marks]

  6. Solve \(2\cos\theta=\sqrt{3}\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).[3 marks]
  7. Prove \(\dfrac{1-\cos 2\theta}{\sin 2\theta}=\tan\theta\).[3 marks]
  8. If \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac35\) and \(\theta\) is in Quadrant IV, find the exact values of \(\cos 2\theta\) and \(\tan 2\theta\).[3 marks]
  9. Solve \(2\cos\theta+1=0\) for \(0\le\theta\le2\pi\).[4 marks]
  10. Given \(\sin A=\dfrac{5}{13}\) and \(\cos B=\dfrac45\), where \(A\) and \(B\) are acute, find the exact value of \(\sin(A+B)\).[4 marks]

  • Questions 1–5 are multiple choice. Each question is worth 1 mark; marks are awarded for the answer only.

Questions 1–5: Multiple choice

  1. In \(\triangle ABC\), right-angled at \(C\), \(AC=6\), \(BC=8\). Find \(\sin A\).
    (A) \(\dfrac{3}{4}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{4}{5}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{3}{5}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{5}{4}\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{6}{8}\)
  2. Reference angle for \(300^\circ\)?
    (A) \(30^\circ\)
    (B) \(60^\circ\)
    (C) \(120^\circ\)
    (D) \(300^\circ\)
    (E) \(45^\circ\)
  3. \(\sin\theta<0\) and \(\sec\theta<0\): quadrant?
    (A) 1
    (B) 2
    (C) 3
    (D) 4
    (E) none
  4. Convert \(75^\circ\) to radians (exact).
    (A) \(\dfrac{5\pi}{12}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{3\pi}{8}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\)
  5. \(y=\sin x\): amplitude \(5\), period \(6\pi\), down \(3\):
    (A) \(5\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)-3\)
    (B) \(5\sin(3x)-3\)
    (C) \(5\sin(6\pi x)-3\)
    (D) \(\sin\!\left(\dfrac{x}{3}\right)-5\)
    (E) \(5\sin x-3\)

Questions 6–15: Long answer

  1. Simplify \(\cos^2\theta(\sec^2\theta-1)\).[2 marks]
  2. A wire from the top of a \(12\) m pole makes \(35^\circ\) with the ground. Find the wire length to 2 d.p.[2 marks]
  3. If \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{12}{13}\) and \(\theta\) obtuse, find exact \(\cos\theta\).[2 marks]
  4. Exact value of \(\tan\!\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right)\).[2 marks]
  5. Solve \(\sin 2\theta=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le180^\circ\).[2 marks]
  6. Solve \(2\cos^2\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).[3 marks]
  7. For \(y=\dfrac12\sin 2x\), state amplitude, period, and the first positive \(x\)-intercept.[3 marks]
  8. Prove \(\dfrac{1-\cos 2\theta}{1+\cos 2\theta}=\tan^2\theta\).[3 marks]
  9. Solve \(\sin^2\theta=\dfrac34\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).[4 marks]
  10. A sinusoidal function has maximum \(5\), minimum \(-1\), period \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\), and passes through \((0,2)\) while increasing. Find an equation for the function and sketch one period.[4 marks]

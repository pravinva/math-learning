Test 1 — Worked Solutions

Q1. Substitute \(x=-2\): numerator \((-2)^3+8=0\), denominator \((-2)^2+5=9\neq0\). So \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3+8}{x^2+5}=\dfrac{0}{9}=0\). Answer: (A) \(0\).
Q2. Stationary points: \(f'(x)=2x-6\). Set \(f'(x)=0\): \(2x-6=0\Rightarrow x=3\). Answer: (D) \(x=3\).
Q3. Chain rule: \(y=(x^2-1)^{1/3}\), so \(y'=\tfrac13(x^2-1)^{-2/3}(2x)=\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{2/3}}\). Answer: (A).
Q4. Quotient rule: \(f=\dfrac{x^2+3}{x^3}\). \(f'=\dfrac{2x\cdot x^3-(x^2+3)\cdot 3x^2}{x^6}=\dfrac{-x^4-9x^2}{x^6}=-\dfrac{x^2+9}{x^4}\). Answer: (E).
Q5. Product rule: \(y'=e^x\sin(3x)+e^x(3\cos3x)=e^x(\sin3x+3\cos3x)\). Answer: (A).
Q6. Tangent: \(y'=6x\Rightarrow m=6(-1)=-6\). At \(x=-1\), \(y=3\). So \(y-3=-6(x+1)\Rightarrow y=-6x-3\). Answer: (A).
Q7. Graph limit: From both sides the curve approaches the open circle at \((1,4)\). LHL \(=\) RHL \(=4\), so \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to1}f(x)=4\) exists (even though \(f(1)=2\)). Answer: Yes; limit \(=4\).
Q8. (a) Factor: \(\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}=\dfrac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x-2}=x+2\to4\) as \(x\to2\). (b) Highest-degree terms: divide by \(x^2\): \(\dfrac{3+6/x^2}{2-7/x^2}\to\dfrac32\). Answer: (a) \(4\), (b) \(\dfrac32\).
Q9. Rewrite powers: \(y=x^{1/2}+x^{3/2}\). \(y'=\tfrac12 x^{-1/2}+\tfrac32 x^{1/2}=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}+\dfrac{3}{2}\sqrt{x}\). Answer: as above.
Q10. Expand then differentiate: \((x-1)(2x+3)=2x^2+x-3\), so \(f'(x)=4x+1\). Answer: \(f'(x)=4x+1\).
Q11. First derivative: \(y=(x-1)^{1/2}\Rightarrow y'=\tfrac12(x-1)^{-1/2}\). Second derivative: \(y''=-\tfrac14(x-1)^{-3/2}\). Answer: \(y''=-\dfrac14(x-1)^{-3/2}\).
Q12. Chain rule: \(f'(x)=e^{x^2+2}(2x)\). So \(f'(1)=2e^{3}\). Answer: \(2e^3\).
Q13. Quotient rule: \(y'=\dfrac{x\cdot\frac{1}{x}-\ln(2x)}{x^2}=\dfrac{1-\ln(2x)}{x^2}\). Answer: as above.
Q14. Log chain: \(f'(x)=\dfrac{2}{2x-3}\). So \(f'(2)=\dfrac{2}{1}=2\). Answer: \(2\).
Q15. Trig chain: \(y'=2\pi\sec^2(2\pi x)\). At \(x=\tfrac16\): \(2\pi x=\tfrac\pi3\), \(\sec^2(\tfrac\pi3)=4\), so \(y'(\tfrac16)=8\pi\). Answer: \(8\pi\).
Q16. Product rule: \(y'=-e^{-x}(x^2-1)+e^{-x}(2x)=e^{-x}(-x^2+2x+1)\). Answer: as above.
Q17. Tangent: \(y'=3x^2-3\Rightarrow m=9\) at \(x=2\); \(y(2)=2\). So \(y-2=9(x-2)\Rightarrow y=9x-16\). Answer: \(y=9x-16\).
Q18. Parallel slope: target slope \(=3\). \(y'=\tfrac12 x+9=3\Rightarrow x=-6\). \(y(-6)=\tfrac12(36)-54+4=-23\). Answer: \(x=-6\), point \((-6,-23)\).

Test 2 — Worked Solutions

Q1. Factor: \(\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}=\dfrac{(x-3)(x+3)}{x-3}=x+3\to6\) as \(x\to3\). Answer: (C) \(6\).
Q2. Stationary points: \(f'=12x^2-12=12(x^2-1)\). \(f'=0\Rightarrow x=\pm1\). Answer: (B) \(x=\pm1\).
Q3. Chain rule: \(y'=\tfrac32(x^2+4)^{1/2}(2x)=3x\sqrt{x^2+4}\). Answer: (A)/(C).
Q4. Log chain: \(y'=\dfrac{10x}{5x^2+1}\). Answer: (B).
Q5. Quotient rule: \(f'=\dfrac{xe^x-e^x}{x^2}=\dfrac{e^x(x-1)}{x^2}\). Answer: (B).
Q6. Tangent: \(y'=2e^{2x}\Rightarrow m=2\) at \(x=0\); \(y(0)=1\). So \(y=2x+1\). Answer: (A).
Q7. Jump at \(x=0\): from the left \(y\to-1\), from the right \(y\to1\). LHL \(\neq\) RHL, so the limit does not exist. Answer: No.
Q8. (a) \(\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+1}=\dfrac{(x+1)(x-1)}{x+1}=x-1\to-2\). (b) degree of denominator greater: limit \(=0\). Answer: (a) \(-2\), (b) \(0\).
Q9. Differentiate: \(y'=4x^3-16x=4x(x^2-4)\). Set \(y'=0\): \(x=0,\pm2\). \(y(0)=3\), \(y(\pm2)=-13\). Answer: stationary points \((-2,-13),(0,3),(2,-13)\).
Q10. Quotient rule: \(y'=\dfrac{3(x-4)-(3x+2)}{(x-4)^2}=\dfrac{-14}{(x-4)^2}\). Answer: as above.
Q11. Second derivative: \(f''=12x^2-24x\). Set \(=0\): \(12x(x-2)=0\Rightarrow x=0,2\). Answer: \(f''=12x^2-24x\); \(x=0,2\).
Q12. Chain rule: \(f'=-\sin(5x^2)(10x)\). So \(f'(0)=0\). Answer: \(0\).
Q13. Product rule: \(y'=1\cdot\ln x+x\cdot\tfrac1x=\ln x+1\). Answer: \(1+\ln x\).
Q14. Quotient rule: \(f'=\dfrac{1\cdot x-\ln x}{x^2}=\dfrac{1-\ln x}{x^2}\). At \(x=e\): \(f'(e)=\dfrac{1-1}{e^2}=0\). Answer: \(0\).
Q15. Chain rule on \(\sin^2(4x)\): \(y'=2\sin4x\cdot4\cos4x=8\sin4x\cos4x=4\sin8x\). Answer: \(8\sin4x\cos4x\) (or \(4\sin8x\)).
Q16. Product rule: \(y'=(2x)e^x+(x^2+1)e^x=e^x(x^2+2x+1)\). Answer: as above.
Q17. Tangent to \(y=1/x\) at \(x=2\): \(m=-1/4\), \(y(2)=\tfrac12\). So \(y-\tfrac12=-\tfrac14(x-2)\Rightarrow y=-\tfrac14 x+1\). Answer: \(y=-\tfrac14 x+1\).
Q18. Velocity: \(v=s'=3t^2-12t+9=3(t^2-4t+3)=3(t-1)(t-3)\). Zero on \([0,5]\) at \(t=1,3\). Answer: \(t=1,3\).

Test 3 — Worked Solutions

Q1. Standard limit: \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin x}{x}=1\). Answer: (B).
Q2. Second derivative: \(f''=12x^2-24x=12x(x-2)\). \(f''=0\Rightarrow x=0,2\). Answer: (A).
Q3. Chain rule: \(y=(3x-1)^{-4}\Rightarrow y'=-4(3x-1)^{-5}(3)=\dfrac{-12}{(3x-1)^5}\). Answer: (A).
Q4. Trig chain: \(y'=2\pi\sec^2(2\pi x)\). At \(x=\tfrac16\): \(2\pi\sec^2(\tfrac\pi3)=2\pi\cdot4=8\pi\). Answer: (A) \(8\pi\) (equivalently \(2\pi\sec^2(\pi/3)\)).
Q5. Quotient then solve: \(y'=\dfrac{1-\ln x}{x^2}=0\Rightarrow\ln x=1\Rightarrow x=e\). Answer: (A) \(x=e\).
Q6. Tangent to \(1/x\) at \(x=2\): \(m=-\tfrac14\), point \((2,\tfrac12)\Rightarrow y=-\tfrac14 x+1\). Answer: (A).
Q7. One-sided limit: as \(x\to0^+\), \(\ln x\to-\infty\), not a finite real number. Answer: No (diverges to \(-\infty\)).
Q8. (a) Rationalise: \(\dfrac{\sqrt{x}-2}{x-4}\cdot\dfrac{\sqrt{x}+2}{\sqrt{x}+2}=\dfrac1{\sqrt{x}+2}\to\dfrac14\). (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\Bigl(3+\dfrac2x\Bigr)=3\). Answer: (a) \(\tfrac14\), (b) \(3\).
Q9. Power rule: \(y'=4\cdot\tfrac32 x^{1/2}-2(-\tfrac12)x^{-3/2}=6x^{1/2}+x^{-3/2}\). Answer: as above.
Q10. Chain rule: \(y'=5(2x-1)^4(2)=10(2x-1)^4\). Answer: \(10(2x-1)^4\).
Q11. Differentiate twice: \(y'=2e^{2x}+2x\), \(y''=4e^{2x}+2\). Answer: \(4e^{2x}+2\).
Q12. Chain rule: \(f'=5(2x+1)^4(2)=10(2x+1)^4\). \(f'(1)=10\cdot3^4=810\). Answer: \(810\).
Q13. Quotient rule: \(y'=\dfrac{2x(x^2-2)-2x(x^2+2)}{(x^2-2)^2}=\dfrac{-8x}{(x^2-2)^2}\). Answer: as above.
Q14. Log chain: \(f'(x)=\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}\). \(f'(1)=\dfrac{2}{2}=1\). Answer: \(1\).
Q15. Product rule: \(y'=3\cos3x\cdot e^x+\sin3x\cdot e^x=e^x(3\cos3x+\sin3x)\). Answer: as above.
Q16. Product rule: \(y'=2x\ln(3x)+x^2\cdot\tfrac1x=2x\ln(3x)+x\). Answer: as above.
Q17. Tangent: \(y'=e^x+1\Rightarrow m=2\) at \(x=0\); \(y(0)=1\). So \(y=2x+1\). Answer: \(y=2x+1\).
Q18. Horizontal tangent: \(y'=3x^2-12x+9=3(x-1)(x-3)=0\Rightarrow x=1,3\). \(y(1)=5\), \(y(3)=1\). Second derivative test: max at \((1,5)\), min at \((3,1)\). Answer: \((1,5)\) local max, \((3,1)\) local min.

Test 4 — Worked Solutions

Q1. Factor / L'Hôpital idea: \(\dfrac{x^3-1}{x-1}=\dfrac{(x-1)(x^2+x+1)}{x-1}=x^2+x+1\to3\). Answer: (D) \(3\).
Q2. Calculus cue: stationary points come from \(f'(x)=0\). Answer: (B).
Q3. Chain rule: \(y=(2x+5)^{1/2}\Rightarrow y'=\tfrac12(2x+5)^{-1/2}(2)=\dfrac1{\sqrt{2x+5}}\). Answer: (A).
Q4. Chain rule: \(y'=2\sin4x\cdot4\cos4x=8\sin4x\cos4x\). Note: this simplifies to \(4\sin8x\) via the double-angle identity, but that form is not listed. Answer: (B).
Q5. Parallel tangent: \(y'=\tfrac12 x+9=3\Rightarrow x=-6\). Answer: (A) \(x=-6\).
Q6. Tangent: \(y'=2x-4\Rightarrow m=2\) at \(x=3\); \(y(3)=-3\). So \(y=2x-9\). Answer: (A).
Q7. Limit vs function value: both sides approach the open circle at \((2,3)\), so \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to2}f(x)=3\) can exist even if \(f(2)\) is undefined or different (\(f(2)=1\) here). Answer: Yes; limit \(=3\).
Q8. (a) \(\dfrac{x^3-8}{x-2}=\dfrac{(x-2)(x^2+2x+4)}{x-2}=x^2+2x+4\to12\). (b) equal degrees: \(\dfrac{4x^3-1}{2x^3+5}\to\dfrac42=2\). Answer: (a) \(12\), (b) \(2\).
Q9. Power rule: \(y'=\tfrac52 x^{3/2}+3(-2)x^{-3}=\tfrac52 x^{3/2}-6x^{-3}\). Answer: as above.
Q10. Chain rule: \(y'=4(3x-2)^3(3)=12(3x-2)^3\). Answer: \(12(3x-2)^3\).
Q11. As Test 1 Q11: \(y'=\tfrac12(x-1)^{-1/2}\), \(y''=-\tfrac14(x-1)^{-3/2}\). Answer: as above.
Q12. Product rule: \(f'=3e^{3x}\cos x-e^{3x}\sin x\). \(f'(0)=3\cdot1\cdot1-0=3\). Answer: \(3\).
Q13. Quotient rule: \(y'=\dfrac{2(x+3)-(2x-1)}{(x+3)^2}=\dfrac{7}{(x+3)^2}\). Answer: as above.
Q14. Log chain: \(f'(x)=\dfrac{3}{3x+1}\). \(f'(1)=\dfrac{3}{4}\). Answer: \(\dfrac34\).
Q15. Trig chain: \(y'=3\sec^2(3x)\). Answer: as above.
Q16. Product rule: \(y'=2e^{2x}\sin x+e^{2x}\cos x=e^{2x}(2\sin x+\cos x)\). Answer: as above.
Q17. Tangent to \(\ln x\) at \(x=e\): \(m=1/e\), \(y(e)=1\). \(y-1=\tfrac1e(x-e)\Rightarrow y=\tfrac{x}{e}\). Answer: \(y=x/e\).
Q18. Rest: \(3t^2-12t+9=3(t-1)(t-3)=0\Rightarrow t=1,3\). Acceleration: \(a=v'=6t-12\), so \(a(1)=-6\). Answer: rest at \(t=1,3\); \(a(1)=-6\).

Test 5 — Worked Solutions

Q1. Factor: \(\dfrac{x^2-25}{x-5}=\dfrac{(x-5)(x+5)}{x-5}=x+5\to10\). Answer: (C) \(10\).
Q2. Solve \(f'=0\): \(3x^2-3=0\Rightarrow x^2=1\Rightarrow x=\pm1\). Answer: (B).
Q3. Chain rule: \(y'=-2(4x-1)^{-3}(4)=-8(4x-1)^{-3}\). Answer: (A).
Q4. Log chain: \(y'=\dfrac{3}{3x+2}\). Answer: (B).
Q5. Product rule: \(y'=2xe^{3x}+x^2(3e^{3x})=e^{3x}(2x+3x^2)\). Answer: (A).
Q6. Tangent to \(x^3\) at \(x=1\): \(m=3\), \(y(1)=1\Rightarrow y=3x-2\). Answer: (A).
Q7. Jump discontinuity: left limit \(=2\), right limit \(=-1\). Limit does not exist. Answer: No.
Q8. (a) \(\dfrac{x^2+x-2}{x-1}=\dfrac{(x-1)(x+2)}{x-1}=x+2\to3\). (b) \(\dfrac{7x-1}{2x+5}\to\dfrac72\). Answer: (a) \(3\), (b) \(\tfrac72\).
Q9. Power rule: \(y'=\tfrac32 x^{1/2}+4x^{-2}\). Answer: as above.
Q10. Chain rule: \(y'=6(5-2x)^5(-2)=-12(5-2x)^5\). Answer: as above.
Q11. Product rule twice: \(y'=3x^2e^x+x^3e^x=e^x(x^3+3x^2)\). \(y''=e^x(x^3+6x^2+6x)\). Answer: as above.
Q12. Chain rule: \(f'=4(x^2+1)^3(2x)\). \(f'(1)=4\cdot2^3\cdot2=32\). Answer: \(32\).
Q13. Quotient rule: \(y'=\dfrac{x\cos x-\sin x}{x^2}\). Answer: as above.
Q14. Log chain: \(f'(x)=\dfrac{2x}{x^2-3}\). \(f'(2)=\dfrac{4}{1}=4\). Answer: \(4\).
Q15. Trig chain: \(f'(x)=-2\sin(2x)\). \(f'(\tfrac\pi6)=-2\sin(\tfrac\pi3)=-\sqrt3\). Answer: \(-\sqrt3\).
Q16. Product rule: \(y'=e^x\ln x+e^x\cdot\tfrac1x=e^x(\ln x+\tfrac1x)\). Answer: as above.
Q17. Tangent to \(\sqrt{x}\) at \(x=4\): \(m=\tfrac1{2\sqrt4}=\tfrac14\), \(y(4)=2\). So \(y=\tfrac14 x+1\). Answer: \(y=\tfrac14 x+1\).
Q18. Parallel slope \(-2\): \(y'=2x-6=-2\Rightarrow x=2\). \(y(2)=-3\). Answer: \(x=2\), point \((2,-3)\).

Test 6 — Worked Solutions

Q1. Factor: \(\dfrac{x^2-9}{x+3}=\dfrac{(x-3)(x+3)}{x+3}=x-3\to-6\). Answer: (B) \(-6\).
Q2. Solve \(f'=0\): \(6x-12=0\Rightarrow x=2\). Answer: (B) \(2\).
Q3. Chain rule: \(y'=5(x^3-1)^4(3x^2)=15x^2(x^3-1)^4\). Answer: (B).
Q4. Quotient rule: \(f'=\dfrac{2(x-1)-(2x+1)}{(x-1)^2}=\dfrac{-3}{(x-1)^2}\). Answer: (A).
Q5. Product rule: \(\cos x\cdot\cos x+\sin x(-\sin x)=\cos^2x-\sin^2x=\cos2x\). Answer: (A).
Q6. Tangent to \(e^x\) at \(0\): \(m=1\), \(y(0)=1\Rightarrow y=x+1\). Answer: (B).
Q7. Both sides \(\to2\): LHL \(=\) RHL \(=2\), so \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}f(x)=2\) exists (even though \(f(3)=4\)). Answer: Yes; limit \(=2\).
Q8. (a) Expand: \(\dfrac{(x+2)^2-4}{x}=\dfrac{x^2+4x}{x}=x+4\to4\). (b) \(\dfrac{x^2+1}{3x^2-4}\to\dfrac13\). Answer: (a) \(4\), (b) \(\tfrac13\).
Q9. Power rule: \(y'=6\cdot\tfrac53 x^{2/3}-(-3)x^{-4}=10x^{2/3}+3x^{-4}\). Answer: as above.
Q10. Chain rule: \(y=(4x+1)^{-1/2}\Rightarrow y'=-\tfrac12(4x+1)^{-3/2}(4)=-2(4x+1)^{-3/2}\). Answer: as above.
Q11. Log chain: \(y'=\dfrac{2}{2x+1}\). \(y''=\dfrac{-4}{(2x+1)^2}\). Answer: as above.
Q12. Chain rule: \(f'=4(3x+1)^3(3)=12(3x+1)^3\). \(f'(0)=12\). Answer: \(12\).
Q13. Product rule: \(y'=\cos x-x\sin x\). Answer: as above.
Q14. Log chain: \(f'(x)=\dfrac{5}{5x}=\dfrac1x\). \(f'(1)=1\). Answer: \(1\).
Q15. Derivative of \(\tan x\): \(f'(x)=\sec^2x\). \(f'(\tfrac\pi4)=\sec^2(\tfrac\pi4)=2\). Answer: \(2\).
Q16. Quotient rule: \(y'=\dfrac{e^x\sin x-e^x\cos x}{\sin^2x}=\dfrac{e^x(\sin x-\cos x)}{\sin^2x}\). Answer: as above.
Q17. Tangent to \(x^2+1\) at \(x=-1\): \(m=-2\), \(y(-1)=2\). \(y-2=-2(x+1)\Rightarrow y=-2x\). Answer: \(y=-2x\).
Q18. Parallel to \(y=2x\): \(y'=3x^2-1=2\Rightarrow x=\pm1\). At \(x=1\): \(y=0\), tangent \(y=2x-2\). At \(x=-1\): \(y=0\), tangent \(y=2x+2\). Answer: both tangents as above.

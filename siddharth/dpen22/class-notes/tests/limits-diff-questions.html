Test 1

  1. \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3+2}{x^2+5}\)=
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(-\infty\)
    (C) \(-2\)
    (D) \(4\)
    (E) DNE
  2. For \(f(x)=x^2-6x-1\), \(f'(x)=0\) when \(x=\)
    (A) \(-1\)
    (B) \(6\)
    (C) \(-3\)
    (D) \(3\)
    (E) \(0\)
  3. Derivative of \(y=(x^2-1)^{1/3}\) is
    (A) \(\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{2/3}}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{4/3}}\)
    (C) \(-\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{4/3}}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{x}{(x^2-1)^{2/3}}\)
    (E) other
  4. \(f(x)=\dfrac{x^2+3}{x^3}\) has \(f'(x)=\)
    (A) \(2x-3x^2\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{2x\cdot x^3-3x^2(x^2+3)}{x^6}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{-x^2-9}{x^4}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{2}{x}\)
    (E) \(-\dfrac{x^2+9}{x^4}\)
  5. Tangent to \(y=3x^2\) at \(x=-1\) (gradient-intercept) is
    (A) \(y=-6x-3\)
    (B) \(y=-6x+3\)
    (C) \(y=6x-3\)
    (D) \(y=6x+3\)
    (E) \(y=-6x-6\)
  6. From a graph that approaches \(y=4\) from both sides at \(x=1\), does \(\lim_{x\to1}f(x)\) exist? Justify.
  7. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{x^2-4}{x-2}\) if it exists.
  8. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{3x^2+6}{2x^2-7}\) if it exists.
  9. Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y=\sqrt{x}+x\sqrt{x}\), leaving surds.
  10. Expand and differentiate \(f(x)=(x-1)(2x+3)\), simplify fully.
  11. Find \(f'(1)\) for \(f(x)=e^{x^2+2}\) (chain rule, exact).
  12. Differentiate \(y=\dfrac{\ln(2x)}{x}\) and simplify.

Test 2

  1. \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}\)=
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(3\)
    (C) \(6\)
    (D) DNE
    (E) \(\infty\)
  2. \(f(x)=4x^3-12x\). Stationary points when \(f'=0\):
    (A) \(x=0\) only
    (B) \(x=\pm1\)
    (C) \(x=\pm\sqrt{3}\)
    (D) \(x=1,2\)
    (E) none
  3. Derivative of \(y=\sin(3x)e^{x}\) by product rule:
    (A) \(e^x(3\cos3x+\sin3x)\)
    (B) \(3\cos3x\,e^x\)
    (C) \(e^x\sin3x\)
    (D) \(e^x(\cos3x-\sin3x)\)
    (E) other
  4. \(y=\ln(5x^2+1)\). Then \(y'=\)
    (A) \(\dfrac{1}{5x^2+1}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{10x}{5x^2+1}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{5x}{5x^2+1}\)
    (D) \(10x\)
    (E) \(\ln(10x)\)
  5. Tangent to \(y=e^{2x}\) at \(x=0\):
    (A) \(y=2x+1\)
    (B) \(y=x+1\)
    (C) \(y=2x\)
    (D) \(y=e^{2}x\)
    (E) \(y=2e^{2}x+1\)
  6. Does \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{|x|}{x}\) exist? Justify.
  7. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+1}\).
  8. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{5x+1}{x^2+4}\).
  9. Differentiate \(y=(2x-1)^5\).
  10. Differentiate \(y=\dfrac{3x+2}{x-4}\).
  11. Differentiate \(y=x^4-8x^2+3\) and find all stationary points (classification optional).
  12. Find the equation of the tangent to \(y=x^3-3x\) at \(x=2\).

Test 3

  1. \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\sin x}{x}\)=
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(1\)
    (C) \(\infty\)
    (D) DNE
    (E) \(-1\)
  2. \(f(x)=x^4-4x^3+2\). \(f''(x)=0\) has solution(s)
    (A) \(x=0,2\)
    (B) \(x=1\) only
    (C) \(x=0\) only
    (D) \(x=3\)
    (E) none
  3. \(y=\tan(2\pi x)\). \(y'(\tfrac16)=\)
    (A) \(2\pi\sec^2(\pi/3)\)
    (B) \(\sec^2(\pi/3)\)
    (C) \(2\pi\)
    (D) \(4\)
    (E) \(4\pi\)
  4. \(f(x)=\dfrac{e^{x}}{x}\). \(f'(x)=\)
    (A) \(e^x\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{e^x(x-1)}{x^2}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{e^x}{x^2}\)
    (D) \(e^x(1-x)\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{xe^x-e^x}{x}\)
  5. Point on \(y=\dfrac12 x^2+9x+4\) where tangent is parallel to \(y=3x-1\):
    (A) \(x=-6\)
    (B) \(x=3\)
    (C) \(x=-3\)
    (D) \(x=6\)
    (E) \(x=0\)
  6. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to4}\dfrac{\sqrt{x}-2}{x-4}\) if it exists.
  7. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(3+\dfrac{2}{x}\right)\).
  8. Differentiate \(y=x\ln x\).
  9. Differentiate \(y=\cos(5x^2)\).
  10. Find \(f'(2)\) if \(f(x)=\ln(2x-3)\).
  11. Differentiate \(y=e^{-x}(x^2-1)\) and simplify.
  12. Find coordinates where the tangent to \(y=x^3-6x^2+9x+1\) is horizontal.

Test 4

  1. \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to1}\dfrac{x^3-1}{x-1}\)=
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(1\)
    (C) \(2\)
    (D) \(3\)
    (E) DNE
  2. \(f(x)=2x^3-15x^2+36x\). Local max/min classification starts from solving
    (A) \(f=0\)
    (B) \(f'=0\)
    (C) \(f''=0\)
    (D) \(f'=f\)
    (E) \(f''=f'\)
  3. \(y=\sin^2(4x)\). \(y'=\)
    (A) \(2\sin4x\)
    (B) \(8\sin4x\cos4x\)
    (C) \(\sin8x\)
    (D) \(4\sin4x\)
    (E) both B and equivalent forms
  4. \(y=\dfrac{\ln x}{x}\). Critical points from \(y'=0\) give
    (A) \(x=e\)
    (B) \(x=1\)
    (C) \(x=0\)
    (D) \(x=e^{-1}\)
    (E) none
  5. Tangent to \(y=\dfrac{1}{x}\) at \(x=2\):
    (A) \(y=-\tfrac14 x+1\)
    (B) \(y=-\tfrac14x+\tfrac34\)
    (C) \(y=-\tfrac12x+1\)
    (D) \(y=\tfrac12x\)
    (E) \(y=-\tfrac14 x+\tfrac12\)
  6. State whether \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0^+} \ln x\) exists as a real number.
  7. Evaluate \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to2}\dfrac{x^3-8}{x-2}\).
  8. Differentiate \(y=(x^2+1)e^{x}\).
  9. Differentiate \(y=\dfrac{x^2+2}{x^2-2}\).
  10. Find \(y''\) for \(y=\sqrt{x-1}\).
  11. Equation of tangent to \(y=e^{x}+x\) at \(x=0\).
  12. A particle has \(s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t\). Find times when velocity is zero on \([0,5]\).

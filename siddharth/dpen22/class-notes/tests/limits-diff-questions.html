Questions 1–6: Multiple choice

  1. [Limits] \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-2}\dfrac{x^3+2}{x^2+5}=\)
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(-\infty\)
    (C) \(-2\)
    (D) \(4\)
    (E) DNE
  2. [Powers of \(x\)] For \(f(x)=x^2-6x-1\), \(f'(x)=0\) when \(x=\)
    (A) \(-1\)
    (B) \(6\)
    (C) \(-3\)
    (D) \(3\)
    (E) \(0\)
  3. [Rules 1–5] Derivative of \(y=(x^2-1)^{1/3}\) is
    (A) \(\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{2/3}}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{4/3}}\)
    (C) \(-\dfrac{2x}{3(x^2-1)^{4/3}}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{x}{(x^2-1)^{2/3}}\)
    (E) other
  4. [Any function] \(f(x)=\dfrac{x^2+3}{x^3}\) has \(f'(x)=\)
    (A) \(2x-3x^2\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{2x\cdot x^3-3x^2(x^2+3)}{x^6}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{-x^2-9}{x^4}\)
    (D) \(\dfrac{2}{x}\)
    (E) \(-\dfrac{x^2+9}{x^4}\)
  5. [Mixed] Derivative of \(y=e^{x}\sin(3x)\) is
    (A) \(e^x(3\cos3x+\sin3x)\)
    (B) \(3\cos3x\,e^x\)
    (C) \(e^x\sin3x\)
    (D) \(e^x(\cos3x-\sin3x)\)
    (E) other
  6. [Tangent] Tangent to \(y=3x^2\) at \(x=-1\) (gradient-intercept) is
    (A) \(y=-6x-3\)
    (B) \(y=-6x+3\)
    (C) \(y=6x-3\)
    (D) \(y=6x+3\)
    (E) \(y=-6x-6\)

  3. [Powers of \(x\)] Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) for \(y=\sqrt{x}+x\sqrt{x}\), leaving surds.[2 marks]

Questions 1–6: Multiple choice

  1. [Limits] \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{x^2-9}{x-3}=\)
    (A) \(0\)
    (B) \(3\)
    (C) \(6\)
    (D) DNE
    (E) \(\infty\)
  2. [Powers of \(x\)] \(f(x)=4x^3-12x\). Stationary points when \(f'=0\):
    (A) \(x=0\) only
    (B) \(x=\pm1\)
    (C) \(x=\pm\sqrt{3}\)
    (D) \(x=1,2\)
    (E) none
  3. [Rules 1–5] Derivative of \(y=(x^2+4)^{3/2}\) is
    (A) \(3x(x^2+4)^{1/2}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{3}{2}(x^2+4)^{1/2}\)
    (C) \(3x\sqrt{x^2+4}\)
    (D) \(2x(x^2+4)^{3/2}\)
    (E) other
  4. [Any function] \(y=\ln(5x^2+1)\). Then \(y'=\)
    (A) \(\dfrac{1}{5x^2+1}\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{10x}{5x^2+1}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{5x}{5x^2+1}\)
    (D) \(10x\)
    (E) \(\ln(10x)\)
  5. [Mixed] \(f(x)=\dfrac{e^{x}}{x}\). \(f'(x)=\)
    (A) \(e^x\)
    (B) \(\dfrac{e^x(x-1)}{x^2}\)
    (C) \(\dfrac{e^x}{x^2}\)
    (D) \(e^x(1-x)\)
    (E) \(\dfrac{xe^x-e^x}{x}\)
  6. [Tangent] Tangent to \(y=e^{2x}\) at \(x=0\):
    (A) \(y=2x+1\)
    (B) \(y=x+1\)
    (C) \(y=2x\)
    (D) \(y=e^{2}x\)
    (E) \(y=2e^{2}x+1\)

Questions 7–18: Long answer

  1. [Limits] The graph below shows \(y=f(x)\). Does \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}f(x)\) exist? Justify. (This is the graph of \(y=\dfrac{|x|}{x}\) with the usual jump.)
    [1 mark]
  2. [Limits] Evaluate, if they exist: (a) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to-1}\dfrac{x^2-1}{x+1}\) (b) \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{5x+1}{x^2+4}\).[4 marks]
  3. [Powers of \(x\)] Differentiate \(y=x^4-8x^2+3\) and find all stationary points.[2 marks]
  4. [Rules 1–5] Differentiate \(y=\dfrac{3x+2}{x-4}\) and simplify.[3 marks]
  5. [2nd derivative] Find \(f''(x)\) for \(f(x)=x^4-4x^3+2\), then solve \(f''(x)=0\).[3 marks]
  6. [Derivative at a point] Find \(f'(0)\) for \(f(x)=\cos(5x^2)\).[3 marks]
  7. [Any function] Differentiate \(y=x\ln x\).[3 marks]
  8. [Logarithms] Find \(f'(e)\) for \(f(x)=\dfrac{\ln x}{x}\).[3 marks]
  9. [Trigonometric] Find \(y'\) for \(y=\sin^2(4x)\), then evaluate if required in exact form.[3 marks]
  10. [Mixed] Differentiate \(y=(x^2+1)e^{x}\).[3 marks]
  11. [Tangent] Find the equation of the tangent to \(y=\dfrac{1}{x}\) at \(x=2\).[3 marks]
  12. [Application of tangent] A particle has \(s(t)=t^3-6t^2+9t\). Find the times when velocity is zero on \([0,5]\).[3 marks]

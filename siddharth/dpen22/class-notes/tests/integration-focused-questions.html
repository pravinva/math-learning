Focused Integration Practice

DPEN022 practice organised by skill. Give exact answers. Include \(+C\) on indefinite integrals. Use radians for trigonometric questions.

Structure: four groups, three sets of 16 in each group (192 questions). Show working and name the main integration move used.

Group 1 — Integration Techniques

Each set contains four questions from each of four types: rewrite then integrate; powers of \(x\); exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric reverse-chain integrals; and simple substitution.

Set 1

16 questions · 4 from each technique family
  1. Find \(\displaystyle\int(x+2)^2\,dx\).Expand, then integrate term by term
  2. Find \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^2+3x}{x}\,dx\).Split the fraction
  3. Find \(\displaystyle\int\left(1+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)^2 dx\).Expand the square
  4. Find \(\displaystyle\int\sqrt{x}\,(x+1)\,dx\).Distribute, rewrite as powers
  5. Find \(\displaystyle\int(4x^3-6x)\,dx\).Power rule
  6. Find \(\displaystyle\int\left(\sqrt{x}+\dfrac{1}{x}\right)\,dx\).Root as a power; \(\int 1/x=\ln|x|\)
  7. Find \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{5}{x^2}\,dx\).Negative power
  8. Find \(\displaystyle\int\left(3x^{1/3}-2x^{-1/2}\right)\,dx\).Fractional powers
  9. Find \(\displaystyle\int e^{2x}\,dx\).Exponential with linear inside
  10. Find \(\displaystyle\int\sin(3x)\,dx\).Sine with linear inside
  11. Find \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{x+4}\,dx\).Log form
  12. Find \(\displaystyle\int 4\cos x\,dx\).Basic cosine
  13. Use \(u=x^2+1\) to find \(\displaystyle\int 2x(x^2+1)^3\,dx\).Substitution; \(du\) is already present
  14. Find \(\displaystyle\int x e^{x^2}\,dx\).Inside the exponential appears as a factor
  15. Find \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x}{x^2+9}\,dx\).Denominator is a good \(u\)
  16. Find \(\displaystyle\int 4x\sqrt{x^2+1}\,dx\).Inside the root appears as a factor

Group 3 — Area

Each set contains eight areas relative to the \(x\)-axis and eight areas between curves. Find zeros or intersections, split where the sign changes, and give an exact area.

Set 1

16 questions · 8 to the axis and 8 between curves
  1. Find the area between \(y=x\) and the \(x\)-axis from \(x=0\) to \(x=3\).Above the axis: integrate \(y\)
  2. Find the area between \(y=x-x^2\) and the \(x\)-axis from \(x=0\) to \(x=1\).Sketch; the curve is above the axis
  3. Find the area of the region where \(y=4-x^2\) lies above the \(x\)-axis.First find the intercepts
  4. Find the area under \(y=\sqrt{x}\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=4\).
  5. Find the area between \(y=\sin x\) and the \(x\)-axis from \(0\) to \(\pi\).Radians; the sine arch is above the axis
  6. Find the area under \(y=x^2\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=2\).
  7. Find the area under \(y=2x+1\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=2\).
  8. Find the area under \(y=e^x\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=1\).
  9. Find the area between \(y=x\) and \(y=x^2\) on \([0,1]\).Find which curve is on top
  10. Show that \(y=x^2\) and \(y=2-x^2\) meet at \(x=\pm 1\), then find the enclosed area.Top minus bottom
  11. Find the area between \(y=x\) and \(y=x^3\) on \([0,1]\).
  12. Find the area between \(y=4x\) and \(y=x^2\).Intersections at \(0\) and \(4\)
  13. Find the area between \(y=2x\) and \(y=x^2+x\) from their first-quadrant intersection points.
  14. Find the area between \(y=\sqrt{x}\) and \(y=x\) on \([0,1]\).
  15. Find the area between \(y=1\) and \(y=x^2\) from \(x=-1\) to \(x=1\).
  16. Find the area enclosed by \(y=x^2\) and \(y=4x-x^2\).

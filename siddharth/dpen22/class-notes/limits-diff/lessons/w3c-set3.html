Combining differentiation rules

From Week 3C: problems mixing product, quotient, and chain rules.

1. Short lesson

Longer expressions need a plan: identify composition (chain), products, and quotients.

Often apply chain rule inside product or quotient terms. Differentiate step by step.

Simplify algebraically only after differentiating, unless simplification makes the derivative easier.

Worked example. \(\dfrac{d}{dx}\!\left[x^2(2x+1)^3\right]=2x(2x+1)^3+x^2\cdot 3(2x+1)^2\cdot 2\) (product + chain).

2. Key points summary

  • Break into inside/outside for chain.
  • Product/quotient on factors.
  • Differentiate once per rule application.
  • Factor common terms at the end.
  • Check with a quick estimate if unsure.

3. Formulas & identities

  • Chain inside product: \((uv)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}v+uv^{\prime}\)
  • Quotient + chain on \(u/v\)
  • Simplify factors like \((2x+1)^2\) when factoring

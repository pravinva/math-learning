Gradient function & meaning of the derivative

From Week 3A: average rate of change, gradient function \(f^{\prime}(x)\), and \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) notation.

1. Short lesson

The derivative measures instantaneous rate of change. The gradient of \(y=f(x)\) at \(x=a\) is the slope of the tangent at that point.

The gradient function \(f^{\prime}(x)\) (or \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)) gives the slope at each \(x\). Notation: \(f^{\prime}(x)=\dfrac{d}{dx}f(x)\).

Average gradient on \([a,a+h]\) is \(\dfrac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}\). The derivative is the limit as \(h\to 0\) (first principles — rules come next).

Worked example. For \(f(x)=x^2\), the gradient at \(x=3\) is \(f^{\prime}(3)=6\) (tangent slope \(6\)).

2. Key points summary

  • Derivative = instantaneous rate of change.
  • \(f^{\prime}(x)\) is the gradient function.
  • Notation: \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\), \(f^{\prime}(x)\), \(\dfrac{d}{dx}f(x)\).
  • Tangent slope equals \(f^{\prime}(a)\) at \(x=a\).
  • Average gradient uses a chord slope.

3. Formulas & identities

  • Average gradient: \(\dfrac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}\)
  • \(f^{\prime}(a)=\displaystyle\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}\)
  • Tangent at \(x=a\) has slope \(f^{\prime}(a)\)

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. If \(f(x)=x^2\), find \(f^{\prime}(3)\).
  2. If \(y=5x+2\), what is \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\)?
  3. State the gradient of \(y=3\) (constant).
  4. If \(f^{\prime}(2)=-4\), describe the tangent at \(x=2\).
  5. Find average gradient of \(f(x)=x^2\) on \([1,3]\).
  6. If \(f(x)=2x+1\), find \(f^{\prime}(x)\) by recognising linear slope.
  7. What does \(f^{\prime}(a)>0\) tell you about \(f\) near \(a\)?
  8. What does \(f^{\prime}(a)=0\) suggest at a turning point?
  9. If \(y=x^2\), find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\big|_{x=-1}\).
  10. Write the gradient of the tangent to \(y=x^2\) at \(x=4\).
  11. If \(f(x)=x^2+3x\), use \(f^{\prime}(x)=2x+3\) to find \(f^{\prime}(1)\).
  12. Does \(f^{\prime}(a)\) depend on the value \(f(a)\)?
  13. If chord from \((2,4)\) to \((5,25)\) on \(y=x^2\), find chord slope.
  14. Sketch idea: \(f^{\prime}(x)>0\) where \(y=x^2\) for \(x>0\).
  15. Units: if \(s(t)\) is metres, what are units of \(s^{\prime}(t)\)?

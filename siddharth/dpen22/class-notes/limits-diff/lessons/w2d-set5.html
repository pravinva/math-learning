1. Short lesson

One-sided limits \(x\to a^\pm\) matter near jumps and vertical asymptotes.

For limits at infinity, dominant terms (or divide by the highest power) decide the value.

Use \(\displaystyle\lim_{\theta\to 0}\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\theta}=1\) after rewriting composite arguments.

Worked example. \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to 0}\dfrac{\sin(3x)}{x}=3\cdot\lim\dfrac{\sin(3x)}{3x}=3\).

2. Key points summary

  • Check left and right separately when needed.
  • Divide by highest power for \(x\to\infty\).
  • Rewrite \(\sin(kx)/x\) using \(\sin u/u\).
  • Squeeze: sandwich between known bounds.
  • State \(\pm\infty\) when unbounded.

3. Key limit results

  • \(\displaystyle\lim_{\theta\to 0}\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\theta}=1\)
  • \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to\infty}\dfrac{\sin x}{x}=0\)
  • One-sided: \(x\to a^+\) and \(x\to a^-\)

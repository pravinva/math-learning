Q1. Form the difference quotient and take \(h\to 0\).\[f^{\prime}(2)=\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{(2+h)^2-4}{h}=\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{4h+h^2}{h}=\lim_{h\to 0}(4+h)=4.\]\[\boxed{4}\]
Q2. \[\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{3(1+h)-3}{h}=\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{3h}{h}=3.\]\[\boxed{3}\]
Q3. \[\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{h^3-0}{h}=\lim_{h\to 0}h^2=0.\]\[\boxed{0}\]
Q4. \(f(3)=-3\), \(f^{\prime}(x)=2x-4\), so \(m=2\).\[y+3=2(x-3)\Rightarrow y=2x-9.\]\[\boxed{y=2x-9}\]
Q5. \(f(-1)=-1\), \(f^{\prime}(x)=3x^2\), \(m=3\).\[y+1=3(x+1)\Rightarrow y=3x+2.\]\[\boxed{y=3x+2}\]
Q6. \(f^{\prime}(x)=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\), so \(f^{\prime}(4)=\dfrac{1}{4}>0\): \(f\) is increasing at \(x=4\).\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}\ \text{(increasing)}}\]
Q7. Average: \(\dfrac{f(3)-f(1)}{2}=\dfrac{10-2}{2}=4\). Also \(f^{\prime}(2)=4\).\[\boxed{\text{both equal }4}\]
Q8. \(v(t)=s^{\prime}(t)=2t-6\), so \(v(4)=2>0\): moving in the positive direction.\[\boxed{v(4)=2\ \text{(forward)}}\]
Q9. \(f^{\prime}(x)=6x^2-3=0\Rightarrow x^2=\dfrac12\Rightarrow x=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\).\[\boxed{x=\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}}\]

1. Short lesson

The derivative definition \(f^{\prime}(a)=\displaystyle\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}\) recovers slopes without memorised rules.

Once \(f^{\prime}\) is known, interpret increase/decrease and write tangent lines \(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\).

Use the power rule for efficient tangent calculations on polynomials.

Worked example. For \(f(x)=x^2\) at \(x=3\): \(m=6\), point \((3,9)\), tangent \(y-9=6(x-3)\).

2. Key points summary

  • First principles: limit of difference quotient.
  • Tangent slope \(=f^{\prime}(a)\).
  • Point–gradient form.
  • Sign of \(f^{\prime}\) shows increase/decrease.
  • Power rule speeds polynomial work.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(f^{\prime}(a)=\displaystyle\lim_{h\to 0}\dfrac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}\)
  • \(y-y_1=m(x-x_1)\)
  • \(\dfrac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}\)

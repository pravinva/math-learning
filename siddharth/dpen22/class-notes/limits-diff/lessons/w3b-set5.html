Second and third derivatives with products

From Week 3B: exam-style practice finding \(f^{\prime\prime}\) and \(f^{\prime\prime\prime}\) for products and polynomials.

1. Short lesson

Differentiate once with the product rule, then differentiate again for \(f^{\prime\prime}\).

Third derivatives appear in Taylor ideas and in motion (\(jerk=s^{\prime\prime\prime}\)).

Simplify after each differentiation when possible.

Worked example. For \(f(x)=x e^x\): \(f^{\prime}=e^x+xe^x\), \(f^{\prime\prime}=2e^x+xe^x\).

2. Key points summary

  • Differentiate step by step.
  • Re-apply product rule each time.
  • Acceleration \(=s^{\prime\prime}\).
  • Factor \(e^x\) when it appears.
  • Check by expanding polynomials.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(f^{\prime\prime}=(f^{\prime})^{\prime}\)
  • \(f^{\prime\prime\prime}=(f^{\prime\prime})^{\prime}\)
  • \((uv)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}v+uv^{\prime}\)

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. \(f(x)=x e^x\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\).
  2. \(f(x)=x^2 e^x\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\).
  3. \(f(x)=x^4\); find \(f^{\prime\prime\prime}(x)\).
  4. \(f(x)=x(x+1)\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\).
  5. \(s(t)=t^2 e^{t}\); find \(s^{\prime\prime}(0)\).
  6. \(f(x)=(2x+1)(x-3)\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\).
  7. \(f(x)=x^3\sin x\) at \(x=0\): find \(f^{\prime\prime}(0)\) (product twice).
  8. \(f(x)=x^5-2x^3\); find \(f^{\prime\prime\prime}(1)\).
  9. \(f(x)=e^{2x}\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\) and \(f^{\prime\prime\prime}(x)\).
  10. \(f(x)=x\ln x\) (\(x>0\)); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\).
  11. \(y=x^2(x+1)\); find \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\).
  12. \(f(x)=(x+1)^3\); find \(f^{\prime\prime\prime}(x)\).
  13. \(s(t)=t^3-3t^2\); find acceleration at \(t=2\).
  14. \(f(x)=x\cos x\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(x)\).
  15. \(f(x)=x^2+xe^x\); find \(f^{\prime\prime}(0)\).

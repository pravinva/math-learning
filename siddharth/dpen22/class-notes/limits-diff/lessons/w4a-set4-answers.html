Q1. Product: \(u=x\), \(v=\ln(x^2+1)\).\[\ln(x^2+1)+x\cdot\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}=\ln(x^2+1)+\dfrac{2x^2}{x^2+1}.\]\[\boxed{\ln(x^2+1)+\dfrac{2x^2}{x^2+1}}\]
Q2. \[\dfrac{1}{x}\ln(x+1)+\ln x\cdot\dfrac{1}{x+1}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{\ln(x+1)}{x}+\dfrac{\ln x}{x+1}}\]

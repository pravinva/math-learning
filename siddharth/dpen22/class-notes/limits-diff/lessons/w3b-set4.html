Products of three factors & product with powers

From Week 3B: multi-step product rule with three factors or a power factor needing the chain rule.

1. Short lesson

For three factors \(uvw\), \((uvw)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}vw+uv^{\prime}w+uvw^{\prime}\).

Often group as \((uv)w\) and apply the product rule twice.

When a factor is \((ax+b)^n\), combine product with chain rule.

Worked example. \(\dfrac{d}{dx}[x(x+1)(x+2)]=(x+1)(x+2)+x(x+2)+x(x+1)\).

2. Key points summary

  • Three-factor product expands to three terms.
  • Group factors if helpful.
  • Chain rule on powered factors.
  • Factor common terms at the end.
  • Check by expanding then differentiating.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \((uvw)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}vw+uv^{\prime}w+uvw^{\prime}\)
  • \((uv)^{\prime}=u^{\prime}v+uv^{\prime}\)
  • \(\dfrac{d}{dx}(ax+b)^n=na(ax+b)^{n-1}\)

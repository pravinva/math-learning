Exponential growth rates & tangent links

From Week 3D: exam-style related-rates-lite and growth problems linking \(e^{kt}\) derivatives to tangents.

1. Short lesson

If \(P=P_0 e^{kt}\), then \(\dfrac{dP}{dt}=kP\): rate proportional to current size.

Related-rates-lite: if \(y=e^{u(t)}\), then \(\dfrac{dy}{dt}=e^{u}u^{\prime}(t)\).

Tangent slopes on growth curves equal \(k\) times the height.

Worked example. If \(P=100e^{0.2t}\), then \(P'(t)=20e^{0.2t}=0.2P\).

2. Key points summary

  • Growth: \(P^{\prime}=kP\).
  • Chain on \(e^{u(t)}\).
  • Evaluate rates at a given time.
  • Link slope to tangent line.
  • Units matter in context.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(\dfrac{d}{dt}P_0 e^{kt}=kP_0 e^{kt}\)
  • \(\dfrac{d}{dt}e^{u}=e^{u}u^{\prime}\)
  • Tangent slope \(=f^{\prime}(a)\)

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. If \(P=50e^{0.1t}\), find \(P'(0)\) and interpret.
  2. If \(N=N_0 e^{-0.2t}\), find the relative decay rate \(\dfrac{N'}{N}\).
  3. \(y=e^{3t}\); find the tangent to \(y\) vs \(t\) at \(t=0\).
  4. A quantity \(Q=4e^{2t}\). When \(Q=4e^2\), find \(\dfrac{dQ}{dt}\).
  5. If \(x=e^{t}\) and \(y=e^{2t}\), find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) at \(t=0\).
  6. \(P=100e^{kt}\) doubles when \(t=5\). Find \(k\), then \(P'(0)\).
  7. \(y=e^{x}+x\); rate of change of \(y\) when \(x=0\).
  8. If the tangent to \(y=e^{kx}\) at \(x=0\) has slope \(4\), find \(k\).
  9. \(s(t)=e^{t}-t\); find velocity and acceleration at \(t=0\).
  10. \(y=2e^{-t}\); equation of the tangent when \(y=1\).
  11. If \(u=2t\) and \(y=e^{u}\), find \(\dfrac{dy}{dt}\) in terms of \(y\).
  12. \(P=P_0 e^{0.05t}\); percentage rate of change.
  13. \(y=e^{x^2}\); find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) at \(x=0\) and the tangent line.
  14. A bacteria count \(B=1000e^{0.3t}\). Find \(B^{\prime}(2)\) exactly.
  15. \(y=e^{t}+e^{-t}\); show \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dt^2}=y\), and find the tangent at \(t=0\).

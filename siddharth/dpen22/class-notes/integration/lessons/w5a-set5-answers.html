Q1. Zeros: \(x(6-x)=0\Rightarrow x=0,6\).\[\int_0^6(6x-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[3x^2-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_0^6=108-72=36.\]\[\boxed{36}\]
Q2. Zeros \(x=\pm 2\).\[\int_{-2}^2(4-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[4x-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_{-2}^2=\dfrac{32}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{32}{3}}\]
Q3. Other intercept \(x=2\).\[\int_0^2(2x-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[x^2-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_0^2=\dfrac{4}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}\]
Q4. Next zero at \(x=\pi\).\[\int_0^{\pi}\sin x\,dx=2.\]\[\boxed{2}\]
Q5. Zeros \(\pm 3\).\[\int_{-3}^3(9-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[9x-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_{-3}^3=36.\]\[\boxed{36}\]
Q6. Intercept \(x=3\). On \([0,3]\), \(y\ge 0\).\[\int_0^3(3-x)\,dx=\bigl[3x-\tfrac{x^2}{2}\bigr]_0^3=\dfrac{9}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}}\]
Q7. \[\int_0^9 x^{1/2}\,dx=\bigl[\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}\bigr]_0^9=18.\]\[\boxed{18}\]
Q8. At \(0\), \(y=0\); on the interval \(y\ge 0\).\[\int_0^{\ln 2}(e^x-1)\,dx=\bigl[e^x-x\bigr]_0^{\ln 2}=(2-\ln 2)-1=1-\ln 2.\]\[\boxed{1-\ln 2}\]
Q9. Zeros \(0,2\).\[\int_0^2(2x-x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{4}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}\]
Q10. \[\int_1^4 5\,dx=15.\]\[\boxed{15}\]
Q11. \[\int_0^2 x^3\,dx=4.\]\[\boxed{4}\]
Q12. \(x(4-x^2)=0\Rightarrow x=0,2\) (since \(x\ge 0\)).\[\int_0^2(4x-x^3)\,dx=\bigl[2x^2-\tfrac{x^4}{4}\bigr]_0^2=8-4=4.\]\[\boxed{4}\]
Q13. \[\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx=1.\]\[\boxed{1}\]
Q14. Intercept \(x=4\).\[\int_0^4(8-2x)\,dx=\bigl[8x-x^2\bigr]_0^4=32-16=16.\]\[\boxed{16}\]
Q15. Zeros \(\pm 4\).\[\int_{-4}^4(16-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[16x-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_{-4}^4=\dfrac{256}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{256}{3}}\]

Signed area traps

From Week 5B: multi-step problems where the signed integral differs from geometric area.

1. Short lesson

The definite integral \(\displaystyle\int_a^b f\) is signed; geometric area uses \(\displaystyle\int_a^b|f|\).

Split at zeros when the curve crosses the axis inside the interval.

Compare the signed value with the total area to catch sign traps.

Worked example. \(\displaystyle\int_{-1}^2 x\,dx=\dfrac32\), but area \(=\displaystyle\int_{-1}^0(-x)\,dx+\int_0^2 x\,dx=\dfrac52\).

2. Key points summary

  • Signed integral can be smaller than area.
  • Split at roots.
  • Negate below-axis pieces for area.
  • Sketch signs.
  • State which quantity is asked.

3. Formulas & identities

  • Area \(=\displaystyle\int_a^b|f|\,dx\)
  • Signed: \(\displaystyle\int_a^b f\,dx\)

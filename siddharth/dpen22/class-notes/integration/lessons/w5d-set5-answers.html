Answers (15)

Q1. \(u=x^2+1\): \(1\to 5\), \(du=2x\,dx\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_1^5\sqrt{u}\,du=\dfrac{1}{3}\bigl[u^{3/2}\bigr]_1^5=\dfrac{5\sqrt{5}-1}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{5\sqrt{5}-1}{3}}\]
Q2. \(u=\cos x\): \(1\to 0\).\[\int_1^0 u^2(-du)=\int_0^1 u^2\,du=\dfrac{1}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{3}}\]
Q3. \(u=\ln x\): \(0\to 1\).\[\int_0^1 u\,du=\dfrac{1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q4. \(u=x^2+1\): \(1\to 2\).\[\int_1^2 u^{-2}\,du=\bigl[-u^{-1}\bigr]_1^2=\dfrac{1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q5. \(u=2x\): \(0\to 2\ln 3\), or directly.\[\bigl[\tfrac12 e^{2x}\bigr]_0^{\ln 3}=\tfrac12(9-1)=4.\]\[\boxed{4}\]
Q6. \(u=\tan x\): \(0\to 1\).\[\int_0^1 u\,du=\dfrac{1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q7. \(u=x^2+1\): \(2\to 5\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_2^5\dfrac{du}{u}=\dfrac{1}{2}\ln\dfrac{5}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\ln\dfrac{5}{2}}\]
Q8. \(u=x^2\): \(0\to 1\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^1 e^{u}\,du=\dfrac{e-1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{e-1}{2}}\]
Q9. \(u=2x\): \(0\to\pi/3\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^{\pi/3}\sin u\,du=\dfrac{1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q10. \(u=x^2+16\): \(16\to 25\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_{16}^{25}u^{-1/2}\,du=\bigl[\sqrt{u}\bigr]_{16}^{25}=5-4=1.\]\[\boxed{1}\]
Q11. \(u=2x+1\): \(1\to 3\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_1^3 u^3\,du=\dfrac{1}{8}\bigl[u^4\bigr]_1^3=\dfrac{80}{8}=10.\]\[\boxed{10}\]
Q12. Let \(u=1+\sqrt{x}\), \(du=\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\,dx\). Limits \(2\to 3\).\[2\int_2^3\dfrac{du}{u}=2\ln\dfrac{3}{2}.\]\[\boxed{2\ln\dfrac{3}{2}}\]
Q13. \(u=1+\cos x\): \(2\to 1\).\[\int_2^1\dfrac{-du}{u}=\ln 2.\]\[\boxed{\ln 2}\]
Q14. \(u=e^x+1\): \(2\to e+1\).\[\ln(e+1)-\ln 2=\ln\dfrac{e+1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\ln\dfrac{e+1}{2}}\]
Q15. \(u=x^2\): \(0\to\pi\).\[\int_0^{\pi}\cos u\,du=0.\]\[\boxed{0}\]

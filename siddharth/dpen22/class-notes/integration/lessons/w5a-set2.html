Definite integrals of powers & polynomials

From Week 5A: practice evaluating definite integrals of polynomial and power functions.

1. Short lesson

Integrate using power rule, then substitute limits.

Watch negative limits: \(\displaystyle\int_a^b = -\displaystyle\int_b^a\).

Simplify fractions in the final answer.

Worked example. \(\displaystyle\int_0^2 (x^2-2x)\,dx = \big[\tfrac{x^3}{3}-x^2\big]_0^2 = \dfrac{8}{3}-4 = -\dfrac{4}{3}\).

2. Key points summary

  • Antiderivative then subtract.
  • Negative area possible algebraically.
  • Simplify exact fractions.
  • Check sign of integrand.
  • Limits order matters.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(\displaystyle\int_a^b x^n\,dx = \dfrac{b^{n+1}-a^{n+1}}{n+1}\)
  • \(\displaystyle\int_a^b (f+g)=\int_a^b f+\int_a^b g\)

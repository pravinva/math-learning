Q1. \(x^2=4x-x^2\Rightarrow 2x^2-4x=0\Rightarrow x=0,2\). On \([0,2]\), \(4x-x^2\) is above.\[\int_0^2\bigl((4x-x^2)-x^2\bigr)\,dx=\int_0^2(4x-2x^2)\,dx=\bigl[2x^2-\tfrac{2x^3}{3}\bigr]_0^2=\dfrac{8}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}}\]
Q2. \(x=x^3\Rightarrow x(x^2-1)=0\Rightarrow x=-1,0,1\).\[\int_{-1}^0(x^3-x)\,dx+\int_0^1(x-x^3)\,dx=\dfrac12.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q3. \(\sqrt{x}=x/2\Rightarrow x=0\) or \(x=4\). On \([0,4]\), \(\sqrt{x}\) is above.\[\int_0^4\bigl(\sqrt{x}-\tfrac{x}{2}\bigr)\,dx=\bigl[\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}-\tfrac{x^2}{4}\bigr]_0^4=\dfrac{16}{3}-4=\dfrac{4}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}\]
Q4. They meet at \(x=0\); on \((0,1]\), \(e^x>x+1\).\[\int_0^1(e^x-x-1)\,dx=\bigl[e^x-\tfrac{x^2}{2}-x\bigr]_0^1=e-\tfrac{5}{2}.\]\[\boxed{e-\dfrac{5}{2}}\]
Q5. Intersections at \(0\) and \(2\); line above the parabola.\[\int_0^2(2x-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[x^2-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_0^2=\dfrac{4}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{4}{3}}\]
Q6. \(x^2-x-2=0\Rightarrow(x-2)(x+1)=0\Rightarrow x=-1,2\). Line above between.\[\int_{-1}^2\bigl((x+2)-x^2\bigr)\,dx=\bigl[\tfrac{x^2}{2}+2x-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_{-1}^2=\dfrac{9}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}}\]
Q7. On \([0,\pi/4]\), \(\cos x\ge\sin x\).\[\int_0^{\pi/4}(\cos x-\sin x)\,dx=\bigl[\sin x+\cos x\bigr]_0^{\pi/4}=\sqrt{2}-1.\]\[\boxed{\sqrt{2}-1}\]
Q8. \[\int_{-2}^2(4-x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{32}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{32}{3}}\]
Q9. \(x^2=2-x^2\Rightarrow x=\pm 1\). Top is \(2-x^2\).\[\int_{-1}^1(2-2x^2)\,dx=\bigl[2x-\tfrac{2x^3}{3}\bigr]_{-1}^1=\dfrac{8}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}}\]
Q10. They meet at \(x=\sqrt{2}\). On \([1,\sqrt{2}]\), \(\dfrac{2}{x}\ge x\); on \([\sqrt{2},2]\), \(x\ge\dfrac{2}{x}\).\[\int_1^{\sqrt{2}}\bigl(\tfrac{2}{x}-x\bigr)\,dx+\int_{\sqrt{2}}^2\bigl(x-\tfrac{2}{x}\bigr)\,dx=\dfrac{1}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q11. \(e^{x}\ge e^{-x}\) on \([0,1]\).\[\int_0^1(e^{x}-e^{-x})\,dx=\bigl[e^{x}+e^{-x}\bigr]_0^1=(e+e^{-1})-2.\]\[\boxed{e+e^{-1}-2}\]
Q12. \[\int_0^3(3x-x^2)\,dx=\bigl[\tfrac{3x^2}{2}-\tfrac{x^3}{3}\bigr]_0^3=\dfrac{27}{2}-9=\dfrac{9}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}}\]
Q13. \[\int_0^1(\sqrt{x}-x)\,dx=\bigl[\tfrac{2}{3}x^{3/2}-\tfrac{x^2}{2}\bigr]_0^1=\dfrac{2}{3}-\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{1}{6}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{6}}\]
Q14. Intersect: \(x^2-1=1-x^2\Rightarrow x=\pm 1\). Top \(1-x^2\).\[\int_{-1}^1\bigl((1-x^2)-(x^2-1)\bigr)\,dx=\int_{-1}^1(2-2x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{8}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}}\]
Q15. \(2\sqrt{x}=x\Rightarrow x=0\) or \(x=4\). Top \(2\sqrt{x}\) on \([0,4]\).\[\int_0^4(2\sqrt{x}-x)\,dx=\bigl[\tfrac{4}{3}x^{3/2}-\tfrac{x^2}{2}\bigr]_0^4=\dfrac{32}{3}-8=\dfrac{8}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{8}{3}}\]

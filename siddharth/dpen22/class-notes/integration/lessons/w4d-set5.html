1. Short lesson

Expand products, split fractions, or rewrite roots as powers before integrating.

Sometimes divide polynomials to simplify rational integrands.

After rewriting, use power rule / reverse chain term by term.

Worked example. \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^2+2x}{x}\,dx=\displaystyle\int(x+2)\,dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+2x+C\).

2. Key points summary

  • Algebra before integration.
  • Split fractions.
  • Expand brackets.
  • Then reverse power/chain.
  • Differentiate to check.

3. Formulas & identities

  • Simplify, then \(\int x^n\)
  • \(\dfrac{x^2+a}{x}=x+\dfrac{a}{x}\)

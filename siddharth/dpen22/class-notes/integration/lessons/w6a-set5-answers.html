Q1. \(u=1+\tan x\): \(1\to 2\).\[\int_1^2\dfrac{du}{u}=\ln 2.\]\[\boxed{\ln 2}\]
Q2. Let \(u=x^4+1\), \(du=4x^3\,dx\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{x^4+1}+C.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{x^4+1}+C}\]
Q3. \(u=1+x^2\): \(1\to 2\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_1^2 u^{-2}\,du=\dfrac{1}{4}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}\]
Q4. \[e^{\tan x}+C.\]\[\boxed{e^{\tan x}+C}\]
Q5. \[\bigl[\ln x+\tfrac{(\ln x)^2}{2}\bigr]_1^e=1+\tfrac12=\dfrac{3}{2}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{3}{2}}\]
Q6. Note \(\sin(2x)=2\sin x\cos x\). Let \(u=\cos x\), or \(u=1+\cos^2 x\), \(du=-2\cos x\sin x\,dx=-\sin(2x)\,dx\).\[-\ln(1+\cos^2 x)+C.\]\[\boxed{-\ln(1+\cos^2 x)+C}\]
Q7. \(u=e^{x}+1\): \(2\to 3\).\[\int_2^3\sqrt{u}\,du=\dfrac{2}{3}\bigl[u^{3/2}\bigr]_2^3=\dfrac{2}{3}(3\sqrt{3}-2\sqrt{2}).\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{2}{3}(3\sqrt{3}-2\sqrt{2})}\]
Q8. Let \(u=\sqrt{x}\), so \(x=u^2\), \(dx=2u\,du\).\[\int\dfrac{2u\,du}{u^2(1+u)}=2\int\dfrac{du}{u(1+u)}=2\int\bigl(\tfrac{1}{u}-\tfrac{1}{1+u}\bigr)\,du=2\ln\left|\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{1+\sqrt{x}}\right|+C.\]\[\boxed{2\ln\left|\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{1+\sqrt{x}}\right|+C}\]
Q9. \(u=\sin x\): \(0\to 1\).\[\int_0^1 u^3\,du=\dfrac{1}{4}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{4}}\]
Q10. \[\ln|x^3+x|+C.\]\[\boxed{\ln|x^3+x|+C}\]
Q11. Derivative of denominator is \(2x+1\).\[\bigl[\ln(x^2+x+1)\bigr]_0^1=\ln 3.\]\[\boxed{\ln 3}\]
Q12. Let \(u=\cos x\), \(du=-\sin x\,dx\).\[\int\sin(\cos x)\sin x\,dx=-\int\sin u\,du=\cos u+C=\cos(\cos x)+C.\]\[\boxed{\cos(\cos x)+C}\]
Q13. \[\dfrac{1}{2}\bigl[\ln(1+x^2)\bigr]_0^{\sqrt{3}}=\dfrac{1}{2}\ln 4=\ln 2.\]\[\boxed{\ln 2}\]
Q14. Let \(u=e^{2x}-e^{-2x}\), \(du=2(e^{2x}+e^{-2x})\,dx\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\ln|e^{2x}-e^{-2x}|+C.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}\ln|e^{2x}-e^{-2x}|+C}\]
Q15. \(u=x^2-1\): \(0\to 3\).\[\dfrac{1}{2}\int_0^3 u^3\,du=\dfrac{1}{8}\cdot 81=\dfrac{81}{8}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{81}{8}}\]

Q1. Signed: odd function \(\Rightarrow 0\). Area: \(2\displaystyle\int_0^2 x\,dx=4\).\[\boxed{\text{signed }0,\ \text{area }4}\]
Q2. Zeros at \(\pm 1\). Below on \([-1,1]\), above on the outer intervals.\[2\int_0^1(1-x^2)\,dx+2\int_1^2(x^2-1)\,dx=\dfrac{4}{3}+\dfrac{8}{3}=4.\]\[\boxed{4}\]
Q3. Signed: \(\bigl[-\cos x\bigr]_0^{2\pi}=0\). Area: \(4\).\[\boxed{\text{signed }0,\ \text{area }4}\]
Q4. On \([0,3]\), \(y\le 0\), so area \(=-\displaystyle\int_0^3(x^2-3x)\,dx=-\bigl[\tfrac{x^3}{3}-\tfrac{3x^2}{2}\bigr]_0^3=\dfrac{9}{2}\).\[\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}}\]
Q5. Signed: \(\bigl[\tfrac{x^2}{2}-2x\bigr]_0^3=-\tfrac{3}{2}\). Area: \(\int_0^2(2-x)+\int_2^3(x-2)=\tfrac52\).\[\boxed{\text{signed }-\tfrac{3}{2},\ \text{area }\tfrac{5}{2}}\]
Q6. Positive on \([0,\pi/2]\), negative on \([\pi/2,\pi]\); total area \(2\).\[\boxed{2}\]
Q7. Zero at \(x=2\). Below on \([0,2]\), above on \([2,3]\).\[\int_0^2(4-x^2)\,dx+\int_2^3(x^2-4)\,dx=\dfrac{16}{3}+\dfrac{7}{3}=\dfrac{23}{3}.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{23}{3}}\]
Q8. Odd integrand on symmetric interval.\[\boxed{0}\]
Q9. Zeros at \(-1,0,1\). On \([-1,0]\), \(y\ge 0\); on \([0,1]\), \(y\le 0\).\[\int_{-1}^0(x^3-x)\,dx+\int_0^1(x-x^3)\,dx=\dfrac12.\]\[\boxed{\dfrac{1}{2}}\]
Q10. Zero at \(x=2\).\[\int_0^2(2-x)\,dx+\int_2^4(x-2)\,dx=2+2=4.\]\[\boxed{4}\]
Q11. \[\bigl[2x-\tfrac{x^2}{2}\bigr]_0^4=8-8=0.\]\[\boxed{0}\]
Q12. One positive and one negative hump of equal area \(1\) each; total area \(2\).\[\boxed{2}\]
Q13. For \(x>0\), \(y=x^2\); for \(x<0\), \(y=-x^2\). Total area \(=2\displaystyle\int_0^2 x^2\,dx=\dfrac{16}{3}\).\[\boxed{\dfrac{16}{3}}\]
Q14. Factor \((x-2)(x+1)\); zeros \(-1,2\). On \((-1,2)\) the parabola is below the axis.Area \(=-\displaystyle\int_{-1}^2(x^2-x-2)\,dx=-\bigl[\tfrac{x^3}{3}-\tfrac{x^2}{2}-2x\bigr]_{-1}^2=\dfrac{9}{2}\).\[\boxed{\dfrac{9}{2}}\]
Q15. \[\bigl[\sin x\bigr]_0^{2\pi}=0.\]\[\boxed{0}\]

Equations that need identities

From Week 2A: solve trig equations by rewriting with Pythagorean identities, then solving on a stated interval.

1. Short lesson

Some equations are not solvable until rewritten. Classic pattern: replace \(\cos^2 heta\) by \(1-\sin^2 heta\) (or vice versa) to get a quadratic in one trig function.

After rewriting, solve the quadratic for \(\sin heta\) or \(\cos heta\), discard impossible roots (\(|k|>1\)), then find angles in the interval.

Always check solutions in the original equation if you squared or used reciprocal steps.

Worked example. Solve \(2\sin^2\theta-\sin\theta-1=0\) on \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\): \((2\sin\theta+1)(\sin\theta-1)=0\) → \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac12\) or \(1\) → \(\theta=90^\circ,210^\circ,330^\circ\).

2. Key points summary

  • Rewrite to one trig function when possible.
  • Quadratic in \(\sin\) or \(\cos\) is common.
  • Reject roots with absolute value \(>1\).
  • Finish with the usual quadrant method.

3. Formulas & identities

  • Replace \(\cos^2=1-\sin^2\), \(\sin^2=1-\cos^2\).
  • Replace \(\sec^2=1+\tan^2\) when the equation is in tan/sec.
  • Standard quadratic formula / factoring after substitution \(u=\sin\theta\).

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. Solve \(2\sin^2\theta-\sin\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  2. Solve \(2\cos^2\theta+\cos\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  3. Solve \(\sin^2\theta=\dfrac34\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  4. Solve \(2\cos^2\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  5. Solve \(\tan^2\theta=3\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  6. Solve \(\sec^2\theta=4\) for \(0\le\theta\le 2\pi\).
  7. Solve \(2\sin^2\theta+\sin\theta=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  8. Solve \(\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta=0\) for \(0\le\theta\le 2\pi\) (hint: \(\cos 2\theta\)).
  9. Solve \(1+\tan^2\theta=4\) for \(0\le\theta\le 2\pi\).
  10. Solve \(2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\sin\theta\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  11. Solve \(\sin^2\theta-\cos\theta-1=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\) (rewrite \(\sin^2\)).
  12. Solve \(2\cos^2\theta= \cos\theta\) for \(0\le\theta\le 2\pi\).
  13. Solve \(\cot^2\theta=1\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\), \(\theta\ne k\cdot 180^\circ\).
  14. Solve \(3\sin^2\theta-2\sin\theta=0\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).
  15. Solve \(\cos 2\theta=\sin\theta\) is beyond basic Pythagorean — instead solve \(\cos^2\theta=\dfrac12\) for \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\).

CAST, reference angles & multi-solution equations

From Week 1B: exact values beyond \(90^\circ\), CAST signs, reference angles, and solving trig equations on \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\) with several solutions.

1. Short lesson

For any angle, reduce to a reference angle (acute angle to the \(x\)-axis), read the special-triangle value, then attach the CAST sign for the quadrant.

Equations such as \(\sin\theta=k\) or \(\cos\theta=k\) generally give two solutions per full turn (when \(|k|\lt 1\)). Equations involving \(\tan\theta=k\) also give two solutions in \([0^\circ,360^\circ)\) because the period of tan is \(180^\circ\).

Forms like \(\sin 2\theta=k\) or \(2\cos\theta=\sqrt2\) are handled by first isolating a single trig expression. For \(\sin 2\theta=k\), solve for \(2\theta\) on a doubled interval, then divide by \(2\) and keep solutions in the original domain.

Negative angles and angles greater than \(360^\circ\) are first rewritten with a coterminal angle in \([0^\circ,360^\circ)\) before applying CAST.

Worked example. Solve \(2\sin\theta=-\sqrt3\) on \(0^\circ\le\theta\le360^\circ\): \(\sin\theta=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\), reference \(60^\circ\), sin negative in Q3/Q4 → \(\theta=240^\circ,300^\circ\).

2. Key points summary

  • Reference angle is always acute (or \(0^\circ/90^\circ\) on axes).
  • CAST gives the sign; the magnitude comes from the reference angle.
  • List every solution in the stated interval.
  • For \(\sin 2\theta\), work with the interval for \(2\theta\) first.
  • Axis angles \(0^\circ,90^\circ,180^\circ,270^\circ,360^\circ\) need special care (one coordinate is \(0\)).
  • Tangent repeats every \(180^\circ\).

3. Formulas & identities

  • Q2 ref: \(180^\circ-\theta\); Q3: \(\theta-180^\circ\); Q4: \(360^\circ-\theta\).
  • \(\sin(180^\circ-\theta)=\sin\theta\),\ \(\cos(180^\circ-\theta)=-\cos\theta\).
  • \(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\),\ \(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\).
  • If \(\sin\theta=k\): Q1/Q2 when \(k\gt 0\); Q3/Q4 when \(k\lt 0\).
  • If \(\cos\theta=k\): Q1/Q4 when \(k\gt 0\); Q2/Q3 when \(k\lt 0\).

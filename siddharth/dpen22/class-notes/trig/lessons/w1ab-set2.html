1. Short lesson

Exact trig values come from two special triangles: the isosceles right triangle (\(45^\circ\)-\(45^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\)) with sides \(1:1:\sqrt2\), and the half-equilateral triangle (\(30^\circ\)-\(60^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\)) with sides \(1:\sqrt3:2\).

These give exact \(\sin,\cos, an\) of \(30^\circ,45^\circ,60^\circ\) without a calculator. For other acute angles, use a calculator in degree mode and round as required.

Right-triangle applications: choose the ratio that links the known side/angle to the unknown. Elevation/depression problems are just right triangles standing upright.

Worked example. \(\sin 30^\circ=\dfrac12\), \(\cos 30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\), \(\tan 30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\); \(\sin 45^\circ=\cos 45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\), \(\tan 45^\circ=1\); \(\sin 60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\), \(\cos 60^\circ=\dfrac12\), \(\tan 60^\circ=\sqrt3\).

2. Key points summary

  • \(45^\circ\)-\(45^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\) sides: \(1:1:\sqrt2\).
  • \(30^\circ\)-\(60^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\) sides: \(1:\sqrt3:2\) (short leg opposite \(30^\circ\)).
  • Memorise exact sin/cos/tan of \(30^\circ,45^\circ,60^\circ\).
  • Check calculator is in degrees for degree problems.
  • Elevation: angle up from horizontal; depression: angle down from horizontal.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12,\ \cos30^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\ \tan30^\circ=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\)
  • \(\sin45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\ \cos45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\ \tan45^\circ=1\)
  • \(\sin60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\ \cos60^\circ=\dfrac12,\ \tan60^\circ=\sqrt3\)
  • Unknown side: rearrange SOH-CAH-TOA, e.g. \(\text{opp}=\text{hyp}\sin\theta\).

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. Find the exact value of \(\sin 60^\circ+\cos 30^\circ\).
  2. Find the exact value of \(\tan 45^\circ\cdot\sin 30^\circ\).
  3. Find the exact value of \(\dfrac{\sin 60^\circ}{\cos 60^\circ}\).
  4. Simplify \(\sin^2 45^\circ+\cos^2 45^\circ\).
  5. A right triangle has an acute angle \(30^\circ\) and hypotenuse \(10\). Find the exact opposite and adjacent sides.
  6. A right triangle has an acute angle \(45^\circ\) and adjacent side \(6\). Find the exact hypotenuse and opposite.
  7. Find \(x\) to 2 d.p. if \(\sin 38^\circ=\dfrac{x}{12}\).
  8. Find \(\theta\) to the nearest degree if \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{7}{15}\) and \(\theta\) is acute.
  9. Find \(\theta\) to the nearest minute if \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{9}{20}\) and \(\theta\) is acute.
  10. From a point \(40\) m from a tower base, the angle of elevation to the top is \(28^\circ\). Find the tower height to 1 d.p.
  11. A \(15\) m ladder leans against a wall at \(65^\circ\) to the ground. How far is the base from the wall (2 d.p.)?
  12. Exact value of \(\sec 30^\circ\).
  13. Exact value of \(\csc 45^\circ\).
  14. Exact value of \(\cot 60^\circ\).
  15. A ship is \(1.8\) km from a lighthouse. Angle of elevation to the top is \(14^\circ\). Find the lighthouse height to 2 d.p. (in km).

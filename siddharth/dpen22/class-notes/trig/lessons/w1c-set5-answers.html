Answers (15)

Q1. Reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\); sin negative in Q3 and Q4. \[ \theta=\pi+\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{7\pi}{6},\quad 2\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{11\pi}{6}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{7\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{6}}\]
Q2. Reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\); cos positive in Q1 and Q4. \[ \theta=\frac{\pi}{4},\quad 2\pi-\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{7\pi}{4}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{4}}\]
Q3. \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\) has reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\); tan positive in Q1 and Q3. \[ \theta=\frac{\pi}{6},\quad \pi+\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{7\pi}{6}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{6}}\]
Q4. \(2\sin\theta=-\sqrt3\Rightarrow\sin\theta=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\). Reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\); Q3/Q4. \[ \theta=\pi+\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac{4\pi}{3},\quad 2\pi-\frac{\pi}{3}=\frac{5\pi}{3}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{4\pi}{3},\ \dfrac{5\pi}{3}}\]
Q5. \(\sqrt2\cos\theta=1\Rightarrow\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\). Same as Q2: \[ \theta=\frac{\pi}{4},\ \frac{7\pi}{4}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{4}}\]
Q6. \(3\tan\theta=-\sqrt3\Rightarrow\tan\theta=-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\). Reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\); tan negative in Q2/Q4. \[ \theta=\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{5\pi}{6},\quad 2\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{11\pi}{6}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{5\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{6}}\]
Q7. Let \(\alpha=2\theta\). Then \(\sin\alpha=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\) with \(0\le\alpha\le 2\pi\). \[ \alpha=\frac{5\pi}{4},\ \frac{7\pi}{4} \implies\theta=\frac{5\pi}{8},\ \frac{7\pi}{8}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{5\pi}{8},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{8}}\]
Q8. \(\cos 2\theta=\dfrac12\). Let \(\alpha=2\theta\) with \(0\le\alpha\le 4\pi\). \[ \begin{aligned} \alpha&=\frac{\pi}{3},\ \frac{5\pi}{3},\ \frac{7\pi}{3},\ \frac{11\pi}{3}\\ \theta&=\frac{\pi}{6},\ \frac{5\pi}{6},\ \frac{7\pi}{6},\ \frac{11\pi}{6}. \end{aligned} \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{5\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{6}}\]
Q9. \(\csc\theta=-2\Rightarrow\sin\theta=-\dfrac12\), same as Q1. \[\boxed{\dfrac{7\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{6}}\]
Q10. \(\sec\theta=\dfrac{2}{\sqrt3}\Rightarrow\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\). Reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\); Q1/Q4. \[ \theta=\frac{\pi}{6},\quad 2\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{11\pi}{6}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{11\pi}{6}}\]
Q11. \(\sin\theta=\cos\theta\Rightarrow\tan\theta=1\) (where \(\cos\theta\neq 0\)). \[ \theta=\frac{\pi}{4},\ \frac{5\pi}{4}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{5\pi}{4}}\]
Q12. \(2\sin\theta\cos\theta=\sin 2\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\) with \(0\le\theta\le\pi\) so \(0\le 2\theta\le 2\pi\). \[ 2\theta=\frac{\pi}{3},\ \frac{2\pi}{3} \implies\theta=\frac{\pi}{6},\ \frac{\pi}{3}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{6},\ \dfrac{\pi}{3}}\]
Q13. \(\cos\theta=0\) or \(\cos\theta=1\). \[ \cos\theta=0\Rightarrow\theta=\frac{\pi}{2},\ \frac{3\pi}{2}; \quad\cos\theta=1\Rightarrow\theta=0,\ 2\pi. \] \[\boxed{0,\ \dfrac{\pi}{2},\ \dfrac{3\pi}{2},\ 2\pi}\]
Q14. \(2\sin^2\theta-1=0\Rightarrow\sin^2\theta=\dfrac12\Rightarrow\sin\theta=\pm\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}\). \[ \theta=\frac{\pi}{4},\ \frac{3\pi}{4},\ \frac{5\pi}{4},\ \frac{7\pi}{4}. \] (Equivalently \(\cos 2\theta=0\).) \[\boxed{\dfrac{\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{3\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{5\pi}{4},\ \dfrac{7\pi}{4}}\]
Q15. Factor: \(\sin\theta(\tan\theta-1)=0\). So \(\sin\theta=0\) or \(\tan\theta=1\), excluding points where \(\tan\) is undefined. \[ \sin\theta=0\Rightarrow\theta=0,\pi,2\pi; \quad\tan\theta=1\Rightarrow\theta=\frac{\pi}{4},\frac{5\pi}{4}. \] At \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{2},\dfrac{3\pi}{2}\), \(\tan\) is undefined, so those are not solutions of the original form as written. \[\boxed{0,\ \dfrac{\pi}{4},\ \pi,\ \dfrac{5\pi}{4},\ 2\pi}\]

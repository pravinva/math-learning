1. Short lesson

Longer proofs still move from the more complicated side to the simpler side. Rewrite tan/sec/cot/csc in \(\sin\) and \(\cos\), then apply \(\sin^2+\cos^2=1\) or the companion identities.

Factoring early often reveals a Pythagorean swap: e.g. \(\sin^2\theta(1+\cot^2\theta)=\sin^2\theta\csc^2\theta=1\).

Difference of squares is frequent: \((\sec\theta-\tan\theta)(\sec\theta+\tan\theta)=\sec^2\theta-\tan^2\theta=1\).

When both sides look messy, convert everything to \(\sin\) and \(\cos\) over a common denominator, expand the numerator, and cancel.

Worked example. Prove \(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{1+\cos\theta}+\dfrac{1+\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}=2\csc\theta\): common denominator \(\sin\theta(1+\cos\theta)\) gives numerator \(\sin^2\theta+(1+\cos\theta)^2=2+2\cos\theta=2(1+\cos\theta)\), which cancels to \(\dfrac{2}{\sin\theta}\).

2. Key points summary

  • Complicated side → simpler side.
  • Rewrite in \(\sin/\cos\) when stuck.
  • Factor and use difference of squares.
  • Quote each Pythagorean identity you use.
  • Check excluded values where you divide by zero.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1,\ 1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta,\ 1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta\)
  • \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta},\ \sec\theta=\dfrac{1}{\cos\theta},\ \cot\theta=\dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta},\ \csc\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta}\)
  • \(\sec^2\theta-\tan^2\theta=1,\ \csc^2\theta-\cot^2\theta=1\)
  • Common patterns: factor \(\sin^2\) or \(\cos^2\); combine fractions.

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. Simplify \(\dfrac{1-\cos^2\theta}{1-\sin^2\theta}\).
  2. Simplify \(\dfrac{\sec\theta}{\tan\theta}-\dfrac{\tan\theta}{\sec\theta}\).
  3. Simplify \(\sin\theta\cos\theta\tan\theta+\sin\theta\cos\theta\cot\theta\).
  4. Prove \(\dfrac{\cos\theta}{1-\sin\theta}-\dfrac{\cos\theta}{1+\sin\theta}=2\tan\theta\) (where defined).
  5. Prove \((\sin\theta+\cos\theta)^2+(\sin\theta-\cos\theta)^2=2\).
  6. Simplify \(\dfrac{\csc^2\theta-1}{\csc^2\theta}\).
  7. Prove \(\dfrac{1+\tan^2\theta}{1+\cot^2\theta}=\tan^2\theta\).
  8. Simplify \(\dfrac{\sin^4\theta-\cos^4\theta}{\sin^2\theta-\cos^2\theta}\).
  9. Prove \(\sec\theta-\cos\theta=\sin\theta\tan\theta\).
  10. Simplify \(\dfrac{1}{\sec\theta-1}+\dfrac{1}{\sec\theta+1}\) in terms of \(\cot\theta\) or \(\cos\theta\).
  11. Prove \(\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\csc\theta}-\dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sec\theta}=\sin^2\theta-\cos^2\theta\).
  12. Simplify \(\tan\theta+\cot\theta\) into a single fraction, then show it equals \(\dfrac{1}{\sin\theta\cos\theta}\).
  13. Prove \(\dfrac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}+\dfrac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\sec\theta\csc\theta\).
  14. Simplify \(\dfrac{\tan^2\theta}{\sec\theta+1}+\sec\theta-1\).
  15. Prove \(\dfrac{1-\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}=\dfrac{\cos\theta}{1+\sin\theta}\) (where defined).

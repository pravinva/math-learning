Answers (15)

Q1. Degrees to radians: multiply by \(\dfrac{\pi}{180}\). \[ 210^\circ=210\cdot\frac{\pi}{180}=\frac{7\pi}{6}. \] Radians to degrees: \(3.8\cdot\dfrac{180}{\pi}\approx 217.7^\circ\). \[\boxed{\dfrac{7\pi}{6};\ 217.7^\circ}\]
Q2. \[ \frac{11\pi}{6}\cdot\frac{180}{\pi}=330^\circ,\qquad 75^\circ=75\cdot\frac{\pi}{180}=\frac{5\pi}{12}. \] \[\boxed{330^\circ;\ \dfrac{5\pi}{12}}\]
Q3. Convert \(120^\circ\) to radians: \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\). Then \(s=r\theta\). \[ s=5\cdot\frac{2\pi}{3}=\frac{10\pi}{3}\text{ cm}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{10\pi}{3}\text{ cm}}\]
Q4. With \(\theta=\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\) and \(r=5\), \[ A=\frac12 r^2\theta=\frac12\cdot 25\cdot\frac{2\pi}{3}=\frac{25\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^2. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{25\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^2}\]
Q5. \[ \theta=\frac{s}{r}=\frac{10}{4}=2.5\text{ rad}. \] In degrees: \(2.5\cdot\dfrac{180}{\pi}\approx 143.2^\circ\). \[\boxed{2.5\text{ rad};\ 143.2^\circ}\]
Q6. \(A=\dfrac12 r^2\theta\Rightarrow 18\pi=\dfrac12\cdot 36\cdot\theta=18\theta\Rightarrow\theta=\pi\). Arc \(s=r\theta=6\pi\). \[\boxed{\theta=\pi,\ s=6\pi}\]
Q7. \(\dfrac{5\pi}{3}\) is Q4; reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\). Sin −, cos +. \[ \sin\frac{5\pi}{3}=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\quad\cos\frac{5\pi}{3}=\frac12, \] so the sum is \(-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}+\dfrac12=\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2}\). \[\boxed{\dfrac{1-\sqrt3}{2}}\]
Q8. \(\dfrac{7\pi}{6}\) is Q3; reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{6}\); tan positive: \(\tan\dfrac{7\pi}{6}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\). Also \(\cos\dfrac{\pi}{6}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\). \[ \tan\frac{7\pi}{6}\cdot\cos\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{1}{\sqrt3}\cdot\frac{\sqrt3}{2}=\frac12. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac12}\]
Q9. \(\sec\dfrac{3\pi}{4}=\dfrac{1}{\cos(3\pi/4)}=\dfrac{1}{-\sqrt2/2}=-\sqrt2\), and \(\csc\dfrac{\pi}{6}=2\). \[ \sec\frac{3\pi}{4}+\csc\frac{\pi}{6}=-\sqrt2+2. \] \[\boxed{2-\sqrt2}\]
Q10. \(\dfrac{19\pi}{4}-2\cdot 2\pi=\dfrac{19\pi}{4}-\dfrac{16\pi}{4}=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\) (in \([0,2\pi)\)). \[ \sin\frac{3\pi}{4}=\frac{\sqrt2}{2},\quad\cos\frac{3\pi}{4}=-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}. \] \[\boxed{\dfrac{3\pi}{4};\ \sin=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\ \cos=-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}}\]
Q11. Convert \(250^\circ\) to radians: \(\theta=250\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}=\dfrac{25\pi}{18}\). \[ s=r\theta=0.35\cdot\frac{25\pi}{18}\approx 1.53\text{ m}. \] \[\boxed{s\approx 1.53\text{ m}}\]
Q12. Convert \(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\) to degrees: \(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\cdot\dfrac{180}{\pi}=150^\circ\gt 140^\circ\). Alternatively convert \(140^\circ\) to radians: \(140\cdot\dfrac{\pi}{180}=\dfrac{7\pi}{9}\approx 2.443\), while \(\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\approx 2.618\). \[\boxed{\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\text{ is larger}}\]
Q13. Odd/even: \(\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta\), \(\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta\). \(\dfrac{2\pi}{3}\) is Q2 with reference \(\dfrac{\pi}{3}\), so \(\sin\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\), \(\cos\dfrac{2\pi}{3}=-\dfrac12\). \[ \sin\!\left(-\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\quad \cos\!\left(-\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)=-\frac12. \] \[\boxed{\sin=-\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2},\ \cos=-\dfrac12}\]
Q14. From \(s=r\theta\): \(4\pi=10\theta\Rightarrow\theta=\dfrac{2\pi}{5}\). \[ A=\frac12 r^2\theta=\frac12\cdot 100\cdot\frac{2\pi}{5}=20\pi. \] \[\boxed{A=20\pi}\]
Q15. \[ 1\text{ rad}=\frac{180}{\pi}^\circ\approx 57.2958^\circ. \] Decimal part \(0.2958^\circ\times 60\approx 17.75'\), which rounds to \(18'\). \[\boxed{57^\circ 18'}\]

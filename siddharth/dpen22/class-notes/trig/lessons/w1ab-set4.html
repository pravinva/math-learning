Mixed right-triangle applications & exact values

1. Short lesson

Application problems often need more than one right triangle, or a mix of exact special angles and calculator work. Sketch first, mark known sides and angles, then choose SOH-CAH-TOA for each unknown.

When two observers (or two positions) look at the same object, draw both triangles sharing a height or a common side. Solve one triangle, then feed that length into the second.

Exact and approximate answers can appear in the same question: leave special-angle work in surds, and use a calculator (degree mode) only where the angle is not \(30^\circ\), \(45^\circ\) or \(60^\circ\).

Co-function identities \(\sin\theta=\cos(90^\circ-\theta)\) remain useful for rewriting and for checking complementary angles in a right triangle.

Worked example. From a point \(A\), the angle of elevation to the top of a tower is \(30^\circ\). From a point \(B\) a further \(20\) m toward the tower, the elevation is \(45^\circ\). Height \(h\) satisfies \(\dfrac{h}{d+20}=\tan30^\circ\) and \(\dfrac{h}{d}=\tan45^\circ\), so \(h=d\) and \(d+20=h\sqrt3\), giving \(h=20(\sqrt3+1)\).

2. Key points summary

  • Sketch and label every triangle before writing ratios.
  • Shared heights or shared bases link multi-triangle problems.
  • Exact values for \(30^\circ,45^\circ,60^\circ\); calculator for other acute angles.
  • Co-functions: \(\sin\theta=\cos(90^\circ-\theta)\).
  • State units and round only at the end when a decimal is required.
  • Bearings: measure clockwise from north unless the question says otherwise.

3. Formulas & identities

  • \(\sin\theta=\dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}},\ \cos\theta=\dfrac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}},\ \tan\theta=\dfrac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}\)
  • \(\sin30^\circ=\dfrac12,\ \sin45^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2},\ \sin60^\circ=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{2}\) (and matching cos/tan)
  • \(\sin\theta=\cos(90^\circ-\theta),\ \cos\theta=\sin(90^\circ-\theta)\)
  • Two-triangle elevation: eliminate the unknown base distance between stations.
  • Bearing: direction measured clockwise from north.

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. From point \(A\) on level ground, the angle of elevation to the top of a tower is \(30^\circ\). From point \(B\), \(40\) m closer to the tower on the same straight line, the elevation is \(45^\circ\). Find the exact height of the tower.
  2. A surveyor at \(P\) measures the angle of elevation to the top of a cliff as \(28^\circ\). Walking \(50\) m toward the cliff to \(Q\), the elevation becomes \(41^\circ\). Find the cliff height to 1 d.p.
  3. Exact value of \(\sin 60^\circ\cos 30^\circ+\cos 60^\circ\sin 30^\circ\).
  4. Exact value of \(\dfrac{\tan 60^\circ-\tan 45^\circ}{1+\tan 60^\circ\tan 45^\circ}\).
  5. Write \(\cos 18^\circ\) as a sine, and \(\sin 72^\circ\) as a cosine, using co-functions.
  6. In right \(\triangle ABC\) with right angle at \(C\), \(AC=5\sqrt3\) and \(\angle A=30^\circ\). Find exact \(BC\) and \(AB\).
  7. A \(12\) m ladder leans against a wall so that the angle with the ground is \(60^\circ\). Find the exact height reached on the wall and the exact distance of the foot from the wall.
  8. A ship sails from harbour \(H\) on a bearing of \(035^\circ\) for \(4.5\) km to \(A\). Find, to 2 d.p., how far east and how far north of \(H\) the ship is at \(A\).
  9. If \(\tan\theta=\dfrac{5}{12}\) (acute) and \(\phi=90^\circ-\theta\), find exact \(\sin\phi\) and \(\cos\phi\).
  10. A kite string is \(85\) m long and makes a \(52^\circ\) angle with the ground. Find the kite height to the nearest metre, and the horizontal distance from flyer to a point directly below the kite (1 d.p.).
  11. Exact value of \(\sec 45^\circ+\csc 30^\circ-\cot 60^\circ\).
  12. An observer on a cliff \(60\) m above sea level sees a boat at an angle of depression of \(18^\circ\). Find the boat distance from the cliff base to 1 d.p.
  13. In a right triangle, one acute angle is \(x\) and the opposite side is \(7\). The adjacent side is \(7\sqrt3\). Find \(x\) exactly and the hypotenuse.
  14. Simplify \(\dfrac{\sin(90^\circ-\theta)}{\cos(90^\circ-\theta)}\) in terms of \(\tan\theta\).
  15. From \(A\), elevation to a tower top is \(35^\circ\); from \(B\), \(25\) m farther from the tower along the line of sight on level ground, elevation is \(22^\circ\). Find the tower height to 1 d.p.

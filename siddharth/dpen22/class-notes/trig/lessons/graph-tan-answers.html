Tan graphs

Answers (5)

Q1. Asymptotes \(x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}\); period \(\pi\); points \(\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4},-1\right),\ (0,0),\ \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4},1\right)\); increasing.
Q2. Asymptotes still \(x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}\); period \(\pi\); points \(\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4},-3\right),\ (0,0),\ \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4},3\right)\) — steeper vertical stretch.
Q3. Period \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\); asymptotes \(x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{4}\); points \(\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{8},-1\right),\ (0,0),\ \left(\dfrac{\pi}{8},1\right)\).
Q4. Asymptotes \(x=-\dfrac{\pi}{4}\) and \(x=\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\); period \(\pi\); points \((0,-1),\ \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4},0\right),\ \left(\dfrac{\pi}{2},1\right)\).
Q5. Asymptotes \(x=\pm\dfrac{\pi}{2}\); midline \(y=1\); decreasing through \((0,1)\); points \(\left(-\dfrac{\pi}{4},2\right),\ (0,1),\ \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4},0\right)\).

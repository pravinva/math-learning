Graphs of sin, cos and tan

From Week 1D: basic graphs in radians, period, amplitude, and key features of \(y=\sin x\), \(y=\cos x\), \(y= an x\).

1. Short lesson

Plotting unit-circle \(y\)-coordinates as \(x\) varies produces \(y=\sin x\). Plotting \(x\)-coordinates produces \(y=\cos x\). Both are continuous, amplitude \(1\), period \(2\pi\).

The cosine graph is a horizontal shift of the sine graph (by \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\)).

\(y= an x\) has period \(\pi\), vertical asymptotes at \(x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+k\pi\), and no amplitude (range all real \(y\)).

Worked example. For \(y=\sin x\): at \(x=0,\dfrac{\pi}{2},\pi,\dfrac{3\pi}{2},2\pi\) the values are \(0,1,0,-1,0\).

2. Key points summary

  • \(\sin\) and \(\cos\): period \(2\pi\), amplitude \(1\), range \([-1,1]\).
  • \(\tan\): period \(\pi\), range \(\mathbb{R}\), asymptotes odd multiples of \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\).
  • \(\sin(x+2k\pi)=\sin x\); \(\tan(x+k\pi)=\tan x\).
  • Cosine is sine shifted left by \(\dfrac{\pi}{2}\): \(\cos x=\sin\!\left(x+\dfrac{\pi}{2} ight)\).

3. Formulas & identities

  • Amplitude of \(a\sin x\) or \(a\cos x\): \(|a|\)
  • Period of \(\sin x\) / \(\cos x\): \(2\pi\); of \(\tan x\): \(\pi\)
  • Asymptotes of \(\tan x\): \(x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}+k\pi\)

4. Practice problems (15)

  1. State amplitude and period of \(y=\sin x\).
  2. State amplitude and period of \(y=\cos x\).
  3. State period of \(y=\tan x\). Does it have an amplitude?
  4. Fill: \(\sin 0=\ldots,\ \sin\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\ldots,\ \sin\pi=\ldots\).
  5. Fill: \(\cos 0=\ldots,\ \cos\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\ldots,\ \cos\pi=\ldots\).
  6. Where are the first positive asymptotes of \(y=\tan x\)?
  7. Sketch one cycle of \(y=\sin x\) for \(0\le x\le 2\pi\) (describe key points).
  8. Sketch one cycle of \(y=\cos x\) for \(0\le x\le 2\pi\) (describe key points).
  9. What is the range of \(y=\sin x\)?
  10. What is the range of \(y=\tan x\)?
  11. True/false: \(\cos x=\sin\!\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)\).
  12. Find \(x\in[0,2\pi]\) where \(\sin x=0\).
  13. Find \(x\in[0,2\pi]\) where \(\cos x=-1\).
  14. Explain why \(y=\tan x\) repeats every \(\pi\), not \(2\pi\).
  15. Compare \(y=\sin x\) and \(y=\cos x\): what horizontal shift maps one to the other?

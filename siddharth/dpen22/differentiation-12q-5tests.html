DPEN22 Differentiation Exam Pack

Five exam-style tests with 12 questions each. Focus: core rules, stationary points, concavity, full curve-analysis contexts, and optimisation word problems.
Scope rule: no implicit differentiation, no parametric differentiation, and no higher-order derivative drills beyond the second derivative needed for concavity/stationary classification.
Recommended timing: 55-60 minutes per test.

Test 1 (12 Questions) Rules + Stationary + Concavity + Optimisation

  1. Differentiate \(f(x)=3x^4-5x^2+7x-9\).
  2. Differentiate \(y=(2x-1)^5\).
  3. Differentiate \(y=(x^2+1)e^x\).
  4. Differentiate \(y=\dfrac{3x+2}{x-4}\).
  5. Differentiate \(y=\ln(5x^2+1)\).
  6. Differentiate \(y=\sin(3x)e^{-x}\).
  7. For \(f(x)=x^3-6x^2+9x+1\), find stationary points and classify them.
  8. For \(g(x)=x^4-4x^3+2\), find intervals of concavity and all inflection points.
  9. A production cost model is \(C(q)=q^3-9q^2+24q+40\), \(q\ge 0\). Determine where cost is increasing/decreasing and find the local turning points with their coordinates.
  10. The vertical position of an automated lift is \(h(t)=-t^3+6t^2-9t+5\), \(0\le t\le 5\). Determine when the lift is rising/falling, find stationary points, and identify where concavity changes.
  11. A rectangular enclosure is built against a straight wall, so only three sides need fencing. If 200 m of fencing is available, find dimensions that maximize area and state the maximum area.
  12. An open-top box is formed by cutting squares of side \(x\) from each corner of a \(30\text{ cm}\times20\text{ cm}\) sheet and folding up the sides. Find the value of \(x\) that maximizes volume and state the maximum volume.

Answer Key — Test 1

  1. \(f'(x)=12x^3-10x+7\)
  2. \(y'=10(2x-1)^4\)
  3. \(y'=e^x(x+1)^2\)
  4. \(y'=-\dfrac{14}{(x-4)^2}\)
  5. \(y'=\dfrac{10x}{5x^2+1}\)
  6. \(y'=e^{-x}(3\cos 3x-\sin 3x)\)
  7. At \(x=1\), local max \((1,5)\); at \(x=3\), local min \((3,-17)\)
  8. Concave up: \((-\infty,0)\cup(2,\infty)\); concave down: \((0,2)\); inflection points \((0,2)\), \((2,-14)\)
  9. Increasing on \((0,2)\cup(4,\infty)\), decreasing on \((2,4)\); local max \((2,60)\), local min \((4,56)\)
  10. Rising on \((1,3)\); falling on \((0,1)\cup(3,5)\); local min \((1,1)\), local max \((3,5)\); inflection at \((2,3)\)
  11. Width \(50\text{ m}\), length \(100\text{ m}\), max area \(5000\text{ m}^2\)
  12. \(x=\dfrac{25-5\sqrt7}{3}\approx3.92\text{ cm}\), \(V_{\max}\approx1056\text{ cm}^3\)

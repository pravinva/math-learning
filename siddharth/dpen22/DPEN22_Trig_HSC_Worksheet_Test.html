Section A — Multiple Choice Style (Q1-Q8)

Q1 (1 mark)

A sector has radius \(12\) cm and arc length \(10\pi\) cm. Find its area.

  1. \(30\pi\)
  2. \(60\pi\)
  3. \(90\pi\)
  4. \(120\pi\)
Q2 (1 mark)

What is the period of \(y=\sin(4x-\pi)\)?

  1. \(\frac{\pi}{2}\)
  2. \(\pi\)
  3. \(2\pi\)
  4. \(4\pi\)
Q3 (1 mark)

Find the exact value of \(\sin\left(\frac{7\pi}{6}\right)\).

  1. \(\frac{1}{2}\)
  2. \(-\frac{1}{2}\)
  3. \(\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\)
  4. \(-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\)
Q4 (1 mark)

If \(\theta=\frac{\pi}{3}\) and \(r=9\), the exact arc length is:

  1. \(3\pi\)
  2. \(\frac{9\pi}{2}\)
  3. \(6\pi\)
  4. \(9\pi\)
Q5 (1 mark)

For \(y=2\cos\left(x+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)-1\), the amplitude is:

  1. \(1\)
  2. \(2\)
  3. \(\frac{\pi}{4}\)
  4. \(3\)
Q6 (1 mark)

Given \(y=\tan(3x)\), the period is:

  1. \(\pi\)
  2. \(\frac{\pi}{2}\)
  3. \(\frac{\pi}{3}\)
  4. \(3\pi\)
Q7 (1 mark)

A pendulum of length \(5\) m swings through angle \(0.8\) radians. Find arc length between extreme positions.

  1. \(2\)
  2. \(4\)
  3. \(5.8\)
  4. \(8\)
Q8 (1 mark)

If \(\cos x=-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\) and \(x\in[0,2\pi)\), one valid value is:

  1. \(\frac{\pi}{6}\)
  2. \(\frac{5\pi}{6}\)
  3. \(\frac{4\pi}{3}\)
  4. \(\frac{11\pi}{6}\)

Section B — Short Response (Q9-Q16)

Q9 (2 marks)

A sector has radius \(r\) and angle \(\theta=\frac{5\pi}{6}\). If perimeter is \(35\), find \(r\).

Q10 (2 marks)

Sketch \(y=\sin x\) and \(y=\sin x+1\) on same axes over \(0\le x\le2\pi\). State vertical shift and new range.

Q11 (2 marks)

Find all solutions of \(2\sin x=\sqrt3\) for \(0\le x<2\pi\).

Q12 (2 marks)

For \(y=3\cos(2x-\pi)+4\), state amplitude, period, and phase shift.

Q13 (2 marks)

Find area of a sector if \(r=14\) cm and \(\theta=0.9\) rad. Give exact formula and decimal.

Q14 (2 marks)

The area of a sector is \(54\) cm\(^2\), radius is \(6\) cm. Find \(\theta\) in radians.

Q15 (2 marks)

Show that for a sector, \(A=\frac12rs\), where \(s\) is arc length.

Q16 (2 marks)

A circle has radius \(6\) cm and central angle \(30^\circ\). Find exact area of the triangle formed by the two radii and the chord.

Section C — Extended Response (Q17-Q24)

Q17 (3 marks)

A tide is modelled by \(h(t)=4+3\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi}{6}(t-1)\right)\), with \(t\) in hours. Find all \(t\in[0,24]\) when \(h(t)=4\).

Q18 (3 marks)

For the same tide model, find times in \(0\le t\le24\) when \(h(t)\ge5.5\).

Q19 (3 marks)

A pendulum of length \(2.4\) m swings through angle \(1.1\) radians. Find: (i) arc length, (ii) sector area swept, (iii) straight-line distance between extreme positions.

Q20 (3 marks)

The graph of \(y=a\sin(bx+c)\) has amplitude \(2\), period \(\pi\), and crosses the origin increasing. Determine one valid equation.

Q21 (4 marks)

A logo is formed by intersection of two circles of equal radius \(5\) m with centres \(6\) m apart. Find the area of overlap (lens) exactly in terms of \(\pi\) and decimal approximation.

Q22 (3 marks)

Solve \(2\cos\!\left(2x-\frac{\pi}{3}\right)=1\) for \(0\le x<2\pi\).

Q23 (3 marks)

In a circle of radius \(10\) cm, a sector has angle \(1.2\) radians. Find the exact area of the corresponding minor segment.

Q24 (3 marks)

For \(y=4\sin\!\left(x-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)+3\): (i) state max/min values, (ii) find the first positive \(x\) for which \(y=3\).

Section D — Challenge Set (Q25-Q30)

Q25 (4 marks)

A tide is modelled by \(H(t)=6+2\cos\!\left(\frac{\pi t}{6}\right)\), \(0\le t\le12\). Find: (i) low-tide height and first low-tide time, (ii) time intervals when \(H(t)\ge7\).

Q26 (3 marks)

Find the area under \(y=2\sin x\) from \(x=0\) to \(x=\pi\). Then find the average value of the function on this interval.

Q27 (3 marks)

Find the exact values of: (i) \(\arcsin\!\left(\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)\), (ii) \(\arccos\!\left(-\frac12\right)\), (iii) \(\arctan(\sqrt3)\) using principal value ranges.

Q28 (4 marks)

An annular sector has outer radius \(9\), inner radius \(5\), and angle \(\frac{2\pi}{5}\). Find: (i) area of the annular sector, (ii) total boundary length (outer arc + inner arc + two radial edges).

Q29 (3 marks)

A Ferris wheel seat path has radius \(14\) m and subtends angle \(0.9\) radians between positions \(A\) and \(B\). Find: (i) arc distance traveled, (ii) straight-line displacement \(\overline{AB}\).

Q30 (4 marks)

Given \(\sin\theta=\frac{3}{5}\) and \(\theta\) in Quadrant II, find exact values of \(\cos\theta\), \(\tan\theta\), and \(\sin(2\theta)\). Then find the exact area of a triangle with sides \(8\), \(11\), included angle \(\theta\).

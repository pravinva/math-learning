MATH141 Revision — Differential Equations Set 1

12 exam-style questions · Separable DEs, IVPs, models, and Euler via an IVP
Scope: Direct integration, separation of variables, initial value problems, growth/decay/mixing-style models. Not integrating-factor / exact / 2nd-order methods (those are MATH142).
  1. Solve the IVP $y'=6x^2-2x+1$, $y(0)=3$.
  2. Solve $y'=e^{-x}$, $y(0)=0$.
  3. Solve $y'=5y$, $y(0)=2$.
  4. Solve $y'=-2y$, $y(0)=10$.
  5. Solve $y'=xy$ (general solution). Then apply $y(0)=4$.
  6. Solve $y'=\dfrac{x}{y}$ for $y>0$, with $y(0)=2$.
  7. Solve $y'=x(1+y^2)$, $y(0)=0$.
  8. Explain why $y'=x+y$ is not separable by the methods of this set (no full solution required).
  9. A bacterial culture doubles every $3$ hours and starts with $200$ cells. How many cells are there after $9$ hours? After $4$ hours (exact form is fine)?
  10. $\$8000$ is invested with continuous compounding at $6\%$ per year. How long until the investment doubles? Leave the answer in exact form.
  11. A tank contains $100\,\mathrm{L}$ of water with $5\,\mathrm{kg}$ of dissolved salt. Brine of concentration $0.1\,\mathrm{kg/L}$ flows in at $2\,\mathrm{L/min}$, and the well-mixed solution flows out at the same rate. The salt amount $S(t)$ satisfies $\dfrac{dS}{dt}=\dfrac{10-S}{50}$. Solve for $S(t)$ with $S(0)=5$, and find $S(50)$.
  12. Show that $y=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ satisfies $\dfrac{dy}{d\theta}=iy$ with $y(0)=1$. Solve that IVP, and hence prove $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.

CP Set 3 — Answers

  1. \(6(\cos330^\circ+i\sin330^\circ)=3\sqrt3-3i\).
  2. \(64=64\cis0\Rightarrow z=2\cis\left(\frac{k\pi}{3}\right),\ k=0,\dots,5\).
  3. \(\frac{2-i}{-1+3i}=\frac{-5-5i}{10}=-\frac12-\frac12 i\). Modulus \(=1/\sqrt2\), argument \(-3\pi/4\).
  4. \((x-1)^2+y^2=4[(x+1)^2+y^2]\Rightarrow (x+\frac53)^2+y^2=(\frac43)^2\): circle.
  5. \(1-i=\sqrt2\cis(-\pi/4)\Rightarrow (1-i)^5=4\sqrt2\cis(-5\pi/4)\), principal arg \(=3\pi/4\).
  6. Roots \(1\pm2i\), conjugates. Product \(=|1+2i|^2=5\).
  7. De Moivre: \((\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^7=\cos7\theta+i\sin7\theta\). For \(\theta=\pi/7\): \(-1\).
  8. \(z+\frac4z=4\Rightarrow z^2-4z+4=0\Rightarrow (z-2)^2=0\Rightarrow z=2\).
  9. \(\frac{3-4i}{1+2i}=\frac{-1-10i}{5}=-\frac15-2i\). Polar: \(r=\sqrt{101}/5\), \(\theta=\pi+\tan^{-1}(10)\) principal.
  10. \(w=i(2-3i)+1=4+2i\).
  11. \(27i=27\cis(\pi/2+2k\pi)\Rightarrow z=3\cis\left(\frac{\pi/2+2k\pi}{3}\right)\), \(k=0,1,2\).
  12. \(z=3\cis\alpha\Rightarrow z^2=9\cis(2\alpha)\), so \(\Re(z^2)=9\cos2\alpha\). At \(40^\circ\): \(9\cos80^\circ\).

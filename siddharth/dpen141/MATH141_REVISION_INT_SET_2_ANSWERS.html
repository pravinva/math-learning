MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 2 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $[x^3-x]_0^2=8-2=6$. $6$
  2. $[\ln x]_1^e=1$. $1$
  3. $[-\cos x]_0^{\pi/2}=1$. $1$
  4. Odd integrand on symmetric interval $\Rightarrow0$. $0$
  5. FTC: $F'(x)=x^2+1$, so $F'(2)=5$. $5$
  6. Chain: $G'(x)=\sqrt{x^2}\cdot2x=2x^2$ for $x>0$. $2x^2$
  7. $\tfrac12[e^{2x}]_0^1=\tfrac12(e^2-1)$. $(e^2-1)/2$
  8. $[\tan x]_0^{\pi/4}=1$. $1$
  9. $\dfrac{1}{3}\int_0^3 x^2\,dx=\dfrac{1}{3}\cdot9=3$. $3$
  10. $[\sqrt{x}]_1^4=2-1=1$. $1$
  11. $\int_b^c=\int_a^c-\int_a^b=2-7=-5$. $-5$
  12. $\int_0^1(1-x)\,dx+\int_1^2(x-1)\,dx=\tfrac12+\tfrac12=1$. $1$

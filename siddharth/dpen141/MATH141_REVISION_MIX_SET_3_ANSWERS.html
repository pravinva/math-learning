Strand 1 — Q1–10

  1. $\sin\theta(2\sin\theta-1)=0\Rightarrow\theta=0,\pi,\dfrac{\pi}{6},\dfrac{5\pi}{6}$. $0,\pi/6,5\pi/6,\pi$
  2. $-4i=4e^{-i\pi/2}$; roots $2e^{-i\pi/4}=\sqrt2-i\sqrt2$ and $2e^{i3\pi/4}=-\sqrt2+i\sqrt2$. $\sqrt2(1-i)$, $\sqrt2(-1+i)$
  3. $\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}+\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}=\dfrac{2x+1}{x^2+1}$. $\dfrac{2x+1}{x^2+1}$
  4. $\ln y=\sin x\ln x\Rightarrow y'/y=\cos x\ln x+\sin x/x$, so $y'=x^{\sin x}\bigl(\cos x\ln x+\dfrac{\sin x}{x}\bigr)$. as stated
  5. $[\ln x]_1^e=1$. $1$
  6. $\dfrac{A}{x-1}+\dfrac{B}{x+2}$: $A=\dfrac43$, $B=\dfrac53$? Check: $A(x+2)+B(x-1)=3x+1$. $x=1$: $3A=4\Rightarrow A=4/3$. $x=-2$: $-3B=-5\Rightarrow B=5/3$. Integral $\dfrac43\ln|x-1|+\dfrac53\ln|x+2|+C$. $\frac43\ln|x-1|+\frac53\ln|x+2|+C$
  7. $y\,dy=x\,dx\Rightarrow y^2=x^2+9$, $y=\sqrt{x^2+9}$. $y=\sqrt{x^2+9}$
  8. $T-20=(90-20)e^{-kt}=70e^{-kt}$. $60-20=70e^{-10k}\Rightarrow e^{-10k}=\dfrac47\Rightarrow k=\dfrac{1}{10}\ln\dfrac74$. $\frac1{10}\ln(7/4)$
  9. $y'=2Ce^{2x}=2y$. Verified
  10. $A=\pi r^2\Rightarrow dA/dt=2\pi r\,dr/dt=2\pi\cdot5\cdot3=30\pi$. $30\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2\mathrm{s}^{-1}$

MATH141 Revision — Differentiation Set 3

12 exam-style questions · Week 8: parametric + implicit differentiation
Scope: Find $dy/dx$ and $d^2y/dx^2$ for parametric/implicit curves; tangents and normals.
  1. For $x=t^2+1$, $y=t^3-t$, find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ in terms of $t$.
  2. For $x=2\cos t$, $y=3\sin t$, find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ and the tangent gradient when $t=\pi/4$.
  3. Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ implicitly for $x^2+y^2=25$, then the tangent at $(3,4)$.
  4. Differentiate $x^3+y^3=6xy$ implicitly and find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ at $(1,2)$ (first verify the point lies on the curve).
  5. For $x=t-\sin t$, $y=1-\cos t$ (cycloid), find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ for $0<t<2\pi$.
  6. Find $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}$ for $x=t^2$, $y=t^3$ at $t=1$.
  7. Implicitly differentiate $e^y=x+y$ and solve for $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$.
  8. Find the equation of the normal to $xy=4$ at $(2,2)$.
  9. For $x=\ln t$, $y=t^2$, $t>0$, find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ and $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}$.
  10. Show that every radius of the circle $x^2+y^2=a^2$ ($a>0$) meets the circle orthogonally. (Hint: compare the slope of the radius with the tangent slope from implicit differentiation.)
  11. Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ for $\sin y=x\cos y$.
  12. A curve is given by $x=t^2-2t$, $y=t^3-3t$. Find every point where the tangent is horizontal.

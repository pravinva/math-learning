MATH141 Revision — AP / GP / Harmonic Set 1 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check formula choice ($S_n$ vs $S_\infty$) and the $|r|<1$ condition.
  1. $a=7$, $d=3$: $T_{12}=7+11\cdot3=40$. $40$
  2. $T_4=a+3d=11$, $T_8=a+7d=23$. Subtract: $4d=12\Rightarrow d=3$, $a=2$. $T_{20}=2+19\cdot3=59$. $a=2$, $d=3$, $T_{20}=59$
  3. $(x+5)-(3x-1)=(2x+4)-(x+5)\Rightarrow6-2x=x-1\Rightarrow7=3x\Rightarrow x=\dfrac73$. $x=7/3$
  4. $a=4$, $d=5$, $n=25$: $S_{25}=\dfrac{25}{2}(2\cdot4+24\cdot5)=\dfrac{25}{2}(8+120)=1600$. $1600$
  5. AP $a=5$, $d=5$, $l=100$: $n=20$. $S_{20}=\dfrac{20}{2}(5+100)=1050$. $1050$
  6. $S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}(2a+(n-1)d)$. $S_6=3(2a+5d)=45\Rightarrow2a+5d=15$. $S_{12}=6(2a+11d)=186\Rightarrow2a+11d=31$. Subtract: $6d=16\Rightarrow d=\dfrac83$, $2a+5\cdot\dfrac83=15\Rightarrow2a=15-\dfrac{40}{3}=\dfrac{5}{3}\Rightarrow a=\dfrac{5}{6}$. $a=5/6$, $d=8/3$
  7. $T_8=5\cdot2^{7}=5\cdot128=640$. $640$
  8. $3r^{3}=24\Rightarrow r^{3}=8\Rightarrow r=2$. $r=2$
  9. $a=2$, $r=3$: $S_{10}=\dfrac{2(3^{10}-1)}{3-1}=3^{10}-1=59049-1=59048$. $59048$
  10. $a=16$, $r=1/2$, $|r|<1$: $S_\infty=\dfrac{16}{1-1/2}=32$. $32$
  11. Reciprocals: $\dfrac12,\dfrac15,\dfrac18,\dfrac{1}{11}$. $1/2,\,1/5,\,1/8,\,1/11$
  12. AM $=10$, GM $=\sqrt{64}=8$, HM $=\dfrac{2\cdot4\cdot16}{4+16}=\dfrac{128}{20}=6.4$. $10\ge8\ge6.4$. AM $10$, GM $8$, HM $6.4$

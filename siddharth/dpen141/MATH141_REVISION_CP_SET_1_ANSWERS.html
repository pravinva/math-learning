CP Set 1 — Answers

  1. \(r=6,\ \theta=2\pi/3\). So \(z=6e^{i2\pi/3}\).
  2. \((2-3i)(1+4i)=14+5i\), divide by \(3-2i\): \(\frac{14+5i}{3-2i}=\frac{32+43i}{13}\).
  3. Roots: \(z=2-5i\) and \(z=2+3i\).
  4. \(z^5=2^5\cis(\pi)= -32\).
  5. \(2\cis\left(\frac{\pi+2k\pi}{3}\right)\), \(k=0,1,2\): angles \(\pi/3,\pi,5\pi/3\).
  6. Circle centered at \((1,1)\), radius \(3\).
  7. \(5(\cos210^\circ+i\sin210^\circ)=-\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}-\frac{5}{2}i\).
  8. \(z_1z_2=-1+7i\), principal argument \(=\pi-\tan^{-1}(7)\).
  9. \(|w|=\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\), \(\arg w=\frac{\pi}{4}-(-\frac{\pi}{3})=\frac{7\pi}{12}\).
  10. \(4(-1/2+\sqrt3 i/2)+3(-\sqrt3/2+i/2)=(-2-\frac{3\sqrt3}{2})+(\,2\sqrt3+\frac32\,)i\).
  11. \(z=2\cis\left(\frac{\frac{2\pi}{3}+2k\pi}{4}\right)\), \(k=0,1,2,3\): angles \(\pi/6,2\pi/3,7\pi/6,5\pi/3\).
  12. \(z=x+iy\), \(2x=6\Rightarrow x=3\), \(x^2+y^2=25\Rightarrow y=\pm4\). So \(z=3\pm4i\).

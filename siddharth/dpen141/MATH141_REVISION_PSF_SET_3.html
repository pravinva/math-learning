MATH141 Revision — Parametric + Special Functions Set 3

12 exam-style questions · Advanced synthesis
Focus: non-routine elimination, omitted points and singularities, smoothness constraints, proof with special functions, and higher parametric reasoning.
  1. For $x=\cos^3t$, $y=\sin^3t$, $0\le t\le\pi/2$, eliminate $t$. State the exact Cartesian restrictions and find the tangent gradient when $t=\pi/4$.
  2. The unit circle is parametrised rationally by $$x=\frac{1-t^2}{1+t^2},\qquad y=\frac{2t}{1+t^2},\qquad -\infty<t<\infty.$$ Prove that the points lie on the unit circle, identify the one point not attained, and describe the direction of travel.
  3. The curve $x=t^3$, $y=t^2$ has a singular point at $t=0$. Eliminate $t$, determine the tangent line at the singular point by a limiting argument, and describe how the two branches meet.
  4. For $x=t^2$, $y=t^4-2t^2$, $-2\le t\le2$, find the Cartesian equation and exact restriction. Explain why almost every point is traced twice and give the direction on each pass.
  5. Find $a,b,c$ so that $$f(x)=\begin{cases}x^2+x,&x<0,\\ax^2+bx+c,&0\le x<1,\\2x^2-3x+2,&x\ge1\end{cases}$$ is differentiable at both joining points.
  6. Do there exist constants $A,B$ for which $f(x)=|x|+Ax+B$ is differentiable at $x=0$? Give a proof, not a graph-based assertion.
  7. Solve exactly $|x^2-1|=x+1$, with interval checks that exclude extraneous algebraic roots.
  8. Solve the inequality $|2x-1|<x+2$. Explain why the sign of the right-hand side matters before manipulating the inequality.
  9. Let $q(x)=\operatorname{sgn}((x-1)(x+2))$. Write $q$ piecewise, including its values at the breakpoints, and solve $q(x)=-1$.
  10. Use the limit definition, rather than differentiation rules, to find the derivative of $f(x)=x^3-2x$.
  11. Prove that $f(x)=x|x|$ is differentiable at $0$. Then find a single formula for $f'(x)$ valid for every real $x$.
  12. A logarithmic spiral is parametrised by $x=e^t\cos t$, $y=e^t\sin t$. At $t=0$, find the tangent, the normal, and $\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}$.

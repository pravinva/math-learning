MATH141 Revision — Differentiation Set 6

12 exam-style questions · Week 10: related rates, optimisation, curve sketching
Scope: Apply derivatives to rates, max/min modelling, and shape analysis.
  1. A circular oil slick expands so that its radius increases at $2\,\mathrm{cm\,s}^{-1}$. How fast is the area increasing when $r=10\,\mathrm{cm}$?
  2. A $5\,\mathrm{m}$ ladder leans against a wall. The foot slides away at $0.2\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}$. How fast is the top sliding down when the foot is $3\,\mathrm{m}$ from the wall?
  3. Find the local maximum and minimum values of $f(x)=x^3-3x^2-9x+5$ on $\mathbb{R}$.
  4. A rectangle has perimeter $40$. Find the maximum possible area.
  5. Find the critical points of $f(x)=x+\dfrac{4}{x}$ for $x>0$, and determine their nature.
  6. For $f(x)=x^3-3x$, find intervals of increase/decrease and inflection points.
  7. A particle moves with $s(t)=t^3-6t^2+8t$. Find when it is instantaneously at rest and the displacement then.
  8. An open-top box is made from a $12\times12$ square sheet by cutting equal squares of side $x$ from each corner and folding. Find $x$ that maximises volume.
  9. Show that $f(x)=x^3+x+1$ is strictly increasing, hence has exactly one real root.
  10. Find the absolute max and min of $f(x)=x^2-4x+3$ on $[0,5]$.
  11. Water drains from a conical tank (vertex down) of height $8\,\mathrm{m}$ and top radius $4\,\mathrm{m}$ at $2\,\mathrm{m}^3/\mathrm{min}$. How fast is the water level falling when depth is $4\,\mathrm{m}$?
  12. Sketch the key features of $y=xe^{-x}$: intercepts, critical points, limits as $x\to\pm\infty$.

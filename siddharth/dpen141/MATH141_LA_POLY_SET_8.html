  11. Test \(x-1\) as factor of \(Q(x)=x^4-5x^3+5x^2+5x-6\), then divide to quotient.
  12. Find \(a,b\) such that \(f(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx+2\) has factors \((x-2)\), \((x+1)\).

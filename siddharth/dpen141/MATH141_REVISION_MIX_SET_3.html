MATH141 Mixed Revision — Set 3

20 exam-style questions · 10 Strand 1 + 10 Strand 2 · suggested 100–120 minutes
Layout: Q1–10 Strand 1 (calculus / complex / DE). Q11–20 Strand 2 (matrices / polynomials / vectors / 3D). Mark each half separately if you want strand scores.

Strand 1 — Q1–10

  1. Solve $2\sin^2\theta-\sin\theta=0$ for $\theta\in[0,2\pi)$.
  2. Find all square roots of $-4i$ in $a+bi$ form (principal args fine).
  3. Differentiate $y=\ln(x^2+1)+\arctan x$.
  4. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\bigl(x^{\sin x}\bigr)$ using logarithmic differentiation.
  5. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_1^e\dfrac{1}{x}\,dx$.
  6. Decompose $\dfrac{3x+1}{(x-1)(x+2)}$ into partial fractions, then integrate.
  7. Solve $y'=\dfrac{x}{y}$ for $y>0$ with $y(0)=3$.
  8. Newton cooling: $\dfrac{dT}{dt}=-k(T-20)$, $T(0)=90$, and $T(10)=60$. Find $k$ (exact form).
  9. Verify that $y=Ce^{2x}$ solves $y'=2y$ for every constant $C$.
  10. A circular oil slick has $r$ increasing at $3\,\mathrm{cm/s}$. How fast is area increasing when $r=5\,\mathrm{cm}$? (related rates — differentiation, not DE solve)

Strand 2 — Q11–20

  1. Compute $AB$ for $A=\begin{bmatrix}1&2\\0&1\end{bmatrix}$, $B=\begin{bmatrix}3&0\\1&4\end{bmatrix}$.
  2. If one row of a $3\times3$ matrix is doubled, what happens to its determinant?
  3. Given $f(x)=x^3+ax^2+bx-6$ has roots $1$ and $-2$, find $a,b$ and the third root.
  4. Find eigenvalues and a basis of eigenvectors for $M=\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&4\end{bmatrix}$.
  5. Find a unit vector in the direction of $\langle2,-1,2\rangle$.
  6. Compute $\langle1,2,3\rangle\times\langle-1,0,2\rangle$.
  7. Find the equation of the plane through $(0,0,0)$ with normal $\langle2,-1,3\rangle$.
  8. Find the point of intersection of the line $\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac{y}{1}=\dfrac{z+1}{-1}$ with the plane $x+y+z=2$.
  9. Find the distance between parallel planes $x+2y-2z=1$ and $x+2y-2z=7$.
  10. For vectors $\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}$ with $|\mathbf{a}|=3$, $|\mathbf{b}|=4$, $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=6$, find $|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|$.

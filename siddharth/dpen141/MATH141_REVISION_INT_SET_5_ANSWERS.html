MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 5 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $\sin^2x=\dfrac{1-\cos2x}{2}$, so $\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{\sin2x}{4}+C$. $x/2-\sin(2x)/4+C$
  2. $\int\bigl(x+x^{-1}\bigr)\,dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+\ln|x|+C$. $x^2/2+\ln|x|+C$
  3. $\dfrac{2x+3}{(x+1)(x+2)}=\dfrac{1}{x+1}+\dfrac{1}{x+2}$, integral $\ln|(x+1)(x+2)|+C$. $\ln|(x+1)(x+2)|+C$
  4. $\cos^2x=\dfrac{1+\cos2x}{2}$: $[\tfrac{x}{2}+\tfrac{\sin2x}{4}]_0^{\pi/4}=\dfrac{\pi}{8}+\dfrac14$. $\pi/8+1/4$
  5. $\int(x^{1/2}+x^{-1/2})\,dx=\dfrac23 x^{3/2}+2x^{1/2}+C$. $\tfrac23 x^{3/2}+2\sqrt{x}+C$
  6. Parts twice: $\int e^x\sin x\,dx=\dfrac{e^x}{2}(\sin x-\cos x)+C$. $\frac{e^x}{2}(\sin x-\cos x)+C$
  7. $\dfrac12\tan^{-1}(x/2)+C$. $\tfrac12\arctan(x/2)+C$
  8. $[\tan^{-1}x]_0^1=\pi/4$. $\pi/4$
  9. $x\sin x-\int\sin x\,dx=x\sin x+\cos x+C$. $x\sin x+\cos x+C$
  10. $\int(x^2+2)\,dx=\dfrac{x^3}{3}+2x+C$. $x^3/3+2x+C$
  11. $u=\sin x$: $-\dfrac{1}{\sin x}+C=-\csc x+C$. $-\csc x+C$
  12. Numerator is derivative of denominator: $\ln|x^3+2x+5|+C$. $\ln|x^3+2x+5|+C$

MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 4

12 exam-style questions · Week 11: integration by substitution
Scope: Choose $u$-substitutions; change limits for definite integrals.
  1. Find $\displaystyle\int 2x(x^2+1)^5\,dx$.
  2. Find $\displaystyle\int\cos x\cdot e^{\sin x}\,dx$.
  3. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1\dfrac{2x}{x^2+1}\,dx$.
  4. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{3x^2}{\sqrt{x^3+4}}\,dx$.
  5. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^3 x\cos x\,dx$.
  6. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{e^{2x}}{1+e^{2x}}\,dx$.
  7. Find $\displaystyle\int x\sqrt{1+x}\,dx$ by a suitable substitution.
  8. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_1^e\dfrac{\ln x}{x}\,dx$.
  9. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{x\ln x}\,dx$ for $x>1$.
  10. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1\dfrac{x}{(1+x^2)^2}\,dx$.
  11. Find $\displaystyle\int\tan x\,dx$ by writing $\tan x=\dfrac{\sin x}{\cos x}$.
  12. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\sqrt3}\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}\,dx$.

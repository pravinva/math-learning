Implicit Differentiation Revision Test

Test-focused revision built from the implicit differentiation study theme. Questions are arranged in HSC-style progression from routine derivatives to tangent/normal geometry and second derivative analysis. Answers are in a separate section at the end.

Time guide: 70-85 minutes.

Calculator: Not required for most questions.

Method marks: show chain-rule steps explicitly whenever differentiating powers/functions of \(y\).

Answer Key (Separate Section)

A1-A8

A1: A, A2: A, A3: A (equivalent to B), A4: C, A5: B, A6: B, A7: Horizontal, A8: C.

B9

\(2x+2yy'=0\Rightarrow y'=-x/y\).

B10

\(2x+(xy'+y)+2yy'=0\Rightarrow y'=-\dfrac{2x+y}{x+2y}\).

B11

\(3x^2+3y^2y'=6(y+xy')\Rightarrow y'=\dfrac{2y-x^2}{y^2-2x}\).

B12

\(\cos y\cdot y'=2x\Rightarrow y'=\dfrac{2x}{\cos y}\).

B13

\(\frac{y+xy'}{xy}=1+y'\Rightarrow y'=\dfrac{x-y}{x(y-1)}\).

B14

\(e^{xy}(xy'+y)=1-y'\Rightarrow y'=\dfrac{1-ye^{xy}}{xe^{xy}+1}\).

B15

From B10, at \((3,2)\): \(y'=-\frac{8}{7}\).

B16

\(2x+2yy'=10\Rightarrow y'=\frac{5-x}{y}\). At \((1,3)\): \(\frac43\).

B17

For \(x^2+y^2=13\), \(y'=-x/y\). Horizontal means \(x=0\), so \(y=\pm\sqrt{13}\). Points \((0,\pm\sqrt{13})\).

B18

Vertical means denominator \(y=0\), so \(x=\pm\sqrt{13}\). Points \((\pm\sqrt{13},0)\).

C19

Using \(y'=-\frac{2x+y}{x+2y}\), at \((3,3)\): \(y'=-1\). Tangent: \(y-3=-1(x-3)\Rightarrow y=-x+6\). Normal slope \(=1\): \(y-3=x-3\Rightarrow y=x\).

C20

\(3x^2+3y^2y'=0\Rightarrow y'=-x^2/y^2\). At \((2,2)\): \(y'=-1\). Tangent: \(y-2=-(x-2)\Rightarrow y=-x+4\).

C21

\(-x/y=-3/4\Rightarrow 4x=3y\Rightarrow y=\frac{4x}{3}\). With circle: \(x^2+\frac{16x^2}{9}=25\Rightarrow \frac{25x^2}{9}=25\Rightarrow x=\pm3,\ y=\pm4\) (matching signs). Points \((3,4),(-3,-4)\).

C22

\(\cos(x+y)(1+y')=y'\Rightarrow y'=\dfrac{\cos(x+y)}{1-\cos(x+y)}\).

C23

First derivative: \(y'=-\frac{2x+y}{x+2y}\). At \((0,3)\), \(y'=-\frac12\).

Differentiate \(2x+y+(x+2y)y'=0\): \(2+y'+(1+2y')y'+(x+2y)y''=0\).

At \((0,3)\): \(2-\frac12+(1-1)\left(-\frac12\right)+6y''=0\Rightarrow \frac32+6y''=0\Rightarrow y''=-\frac14\).

C24

\(2x+(xy'+y)+2yy'=0\Rightarrow (x+2y)y'=-(2x+y)\Rightarrow y'=-\frac{2x+y}{x+2y}\).

C25

\(\frac{2}{3}x^{-1/3}+\frac{2}{3}y^{-1/3}y'=0\Rightarrow y'=-\frac{x^{-1/3}}{y^{-1/3}}=-\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^{1/3}\).

At \((1,3\sqrt3)\), \(y^{1/3}=\sqrt3\), so slope \(m=-\sqrt3\).

Tangent: \(y-3\sqrt3=-\sqrt3(x-1)\).

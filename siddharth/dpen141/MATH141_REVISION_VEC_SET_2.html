MATH141 Revision — Vectors Set 2

12 exam-style questions · vectors only
  1. Find \(|2\mathbf{a}-3\mathbf{b}|\) for \(\mathbf{a}=(1,-1,2)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(0,2,-1)\).
  2. Find the angle that \(\mathbf{u}=(-2,1,2)\) makes with the positive \(y\)-axis.
  3. Find the vector rejection of \(\mathbf{a}=(4,0,-2)\) from \(\mathbf{b}=(1,1,1)\).
  4. Find the area of the parallelogram spanned by \((3,1,0)\) and \((1,2,-2)\).
  5. Points \(A(1,0,0)\), \(B(2,1,1)\), \(C(0,2,1)\). Find the area of \(\triangle ABC\).
  6. Show that \(\mathbf{a}=(1,2,3)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(4,5,6)\), \(\mathbf{c}=(7,8,9)\) are coplanar, and find \(\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})\).
  7. Find a unit vector orthogonal to both \(\mathbf{a}=(1,0,1)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(0,1,-1)\).
  8. Given \(\mathbf{u}=(2,3,-1)\) and \(\mathbf{v}=(1,-1,1)\), decompose \(\mathbf{u}\) into components parallel and perpendicular to \(\mathbf{v}\).
  9. Find the volume of the parallelepiped with edges \(\mathbf{a}=(1,1,0)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(0,1,1)\), \(\mathbf{c}=(1,0,1)\).
  10. Find all \(t\) such that \(\mathbf{a}=(t,1,2)\) is parallel to \(\mathbf{b}=(2,t,-4)\).
  11. If \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\) are unit vectors with \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=\dfrac12\), find \(|\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}|\).
  12. Tetrahedron edges from one vertex: \(\mathbf{p}=(1,1,0)\), \(\mathbf{q}=(1,0,1)\), \(\mathbf{r}=(0,1,1)\). Find the volume.

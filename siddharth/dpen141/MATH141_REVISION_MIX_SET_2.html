MATH141 Mixed Revision — Set 2

20 exam-style questions · 10 Strand 1 + 10 Strand 2 · suggested 100–120 minutes
Layout: Q1–10 Strand 1 (calculus / complex / DE). Q11–20 Strand 2 (matrices / polynomials / vectors / 3D). Mark each half separately if you want strand scores.

Strand 1 — Q1–10

  1. A log–log plot of $y$ vs $x$ is a straight line of slope $2$ through $(1,3)$ on log–log axes (natural logs). Find $y$ in terms of $x$.
  2. Compute $(1+i)^4$ using De Moivre (or polar form).
  3. Differentiate $y=\dfrac{x}{1+x^2}$.
  4. For $x=t^2$, $y=t^3-1$, find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ at $t=2$.
  5. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin x\,dx$.
  6. Find $\displaystyle\int x e^{x}\,dx$ (integration by parts).
  7. Solve $y'=xy^2$, $y(0)=1$.
  8. Explain briefly why $y'=x+y$ is not separable (no solution required).
  9. Solve $y'=-\dfrac{y}{2}$, $y(0)=8$, and find $y(2\ln2)$.
  10. If $z=2\cis\dfrac{\pi}{6}$, find $z^6$.

Strand 2 — Q11–20

  1. Find RREF of $\begin{bmatrix}1&2&3\\2&4&7\end{bmatrix}$.
  2. Use Cramer's rule to solve $2x+y=5$, $x-y=1$.
  3. Factor $Q(x)=x^3-6x^2+11x-6$ given $(x-1)$ is a factor.
  4. State $\delta_{12}$ and $\delta_{33}$ (Kronecker delta).
  5. Find an eigenvector for $\lambda=2$ of $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$.
  6. Project $\mathbf{u}=\langle3,4\rangle$ onto $\mathbf{v}=\langle1,0\rangle$.
  7. Find the volume of the parallelepiped spanned by $\langle1,0,0\rangle$, $\langle1,1,0\rangle$, $\langle1,1,1\rangle$.
  8. Does the line $\mathbf{r}=\langle1,1,1\rangle+t\langle1,0,-1\rangle$ meet the plane $x+y+z=3$? If so, where?
  9. Find the angle between planes $x=0$ and $x+y=0$ (acute angle).
  10. Show that lines $\mathbf{r}_1=\langle0,0,0\rangle+s\langle1,0,0\rangle$ and $\mathbf{r}_2=\langle0,1,0\rangle+t\langle0,0,1\rangle$ are skew, and find the distance between them.

MATH141 Revision — Differentiation Set 6 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $A=\pi r^2\Rightarrow dA/dt=2\pi r\,dr/dt=2\pi\cdot10\cdot2=40\pi\,\mathrm{cm}^2\mathrm{s}^{-1}$. $40\pi$
  2. $x^2+y^2=25$. Diff: $2x x'+2y y'=0$. When $x=3$, $y=4$, $x'=0.2$: $y'=-0.15\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}$. $0.15\,\mathrm{m\,s}^{-1}$ downward
  3. $f'=3(x-3)(x+1)$. Critical points $x=-1,3$. $f(-1)=10$ (local max), $f(3)=-22$ (local min). max $10$, min $-22$
  4. $2(l+w)=40\Rightarrow w=20-l$, $A=l(20-l)$. Max at $l=10$, $A=100$. $100$
  5. $f'=1-4/x^2=0\Rightarrow x=2$ (since $x>0$). $f''>0$ so local (global) min, value $4$. min at $x=2$, value $4$
  6. $f'=3(x^2-1)$; increasing on $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$, decreasing on $[-1,1]$. Inflection at $x=0$. as stated
  7. $v=3t^2-12t+8=0\Rightarrow t=\dfrac{12\pm\sqrt{48}}{6}=2\pm\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}$. Substitute for $s$. $t=2\pm\frac{2\sqrt3}{3}$
  8. $V=x(12-2x)^2$. $V'=0\Rightarrow x=2$ (valid in $(0,6)$). $x=2$
  9. $f'=3x^2+1>0$ always, so strictly increasing; $f(0)=1>0$, $f(-1)=-1<0$, one real root. Shown
  10. $f'=2x-4=0$ at $x=2$, $f(2)=-1$. Endpoints $f(0)=3$, $f(5)=8$. Abs max $8$, abs min $-1$. max $8$, min $-1$
  11. Similar triangles: $r=h/2$, $V=\tfrac13\pi r^2 h=\tfrac13\pi(h^2/4)h=\pi h^3/12$. $dV/dt=(\pi h^2/4)dh/dt=-2$. At $h=4$: $dh/dt=-2/ (4\pi)=-1/(2\pi)\,\mathrm{m/min}$. $-1/(2\pi)\,\mathrm{m/min}$
  12. Intercept $(0,0)$. $y'=e^{-x}(1-x)=0\Rightarrow x=1$, local max $1/e$. As $x\to\infty$, $y\to0$; as $x\to-\infty$, $y\to-\infty$. max at $(1,1/e)$

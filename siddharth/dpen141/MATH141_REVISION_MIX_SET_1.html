MATH141 Mixed Revision — Set 1

20 exam-style questions · 10 Strand 1 + 10 Strand 2 · suggested 100–120 minutes
Layout: Q1–10 Strand 1 (calculus / complex / DE). Q11–20 Strand 2 (matrices / polynomials / vectors / 3D). Mark each half separately if you want strand scores.

Strand 1 — Q1–10

  1. Fit a power model $y=ax^b$ to the points $(1,2)$ and $(4,8)$ (exact $a,b$).
  2. Write $z=-1+\sqrt3\,i$ in polar form $re^{i\theta}$ with $-\pi<\theta\le\pi$.
  3. Differentiate $y=x^2e^{3x}$ (product + chain).
  4. Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ if $x^2+xy+y^2=7$ (implicit).
  5. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1 (4x^3-2x)\,dx$.
  6. Use substitution to find $\displaystyle\int 2x\cos(x^2)\,dx$.
  7. Solve the IVP $y'=4x^3$, $y(1)=2$.
  8. Solve $y'=3y$, $y(0)=5$.
  9. A radioactive sample decays continuously with half-life $10$ days and starts at $80\,\mathrm{mg}$. Find the mass after $30$ days.
  10. Show that $y=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ satisfies $\dfrac{dy}{d\theta}=iy$ with $y(0)=1$. Solve that IVP, and hence prove $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$.

Strand 2 — Q11–20

  1. Solve by row operations: $x+y=5$, $2x-y=4$.
  2. Compute $\det\begin{bmatrix}1&2&0\\3&1&1\\0&-1&2\end{bmatrix}$.
  3. Find $A^{-1}$ for $A=\begin{bmatrix}2&1\\5&3\end{bmatrix}$.
  4. Find the remainder when $P(x)=x^3-4x+1$ is divided by $x-1$.
  5. Find eigenvalues of $B=\begin{bmatrix}3&1\\0&2\end{bmatrix}$.
  6. For $\mathbf{a}=\langle2,1,-2\rangle$ and $\mathbf{b}=\langle1,-1,1\rangle$, compute $\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}$ and the angle cosine.
  7. Compute $\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j}$.
  8. Find a parametric equation of the line through $A(1,0,2)$ and $B(3,1,0)$.
  9. Find an equation of the plane through $(1,0,0)$, $(0,1,0)$, $(0,0,1)$.
  10. Find the distance from the point $(1,1,1)$ to the plane $x+y+z=0$.

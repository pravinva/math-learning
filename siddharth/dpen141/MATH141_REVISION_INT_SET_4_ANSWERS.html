MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 4 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $u=x^2+1$, $du=2x\,dx$: $\dfrac{(x^2+1)^6}{6}+C$. $(x^2+1)^6/6+C$
  2. $u=\sin x$: $e^{\sin x}+C$. $e^{\sin x}+C$
  3. $u=x^2+1$: $[\ln(x^2+1)]_0^1=\ln2$. $\ln2$
  4. $u=x^3+4$: $2\sqrt{x^3+4}+C$. $2\sqrt{x^3+4}+C$
  5. $u=\sin x$: $[\tfrac14\sin^4 x]_0^{\pi/2}=\tfrac14$. $1/4$
  6. $u=1+e^{2x}$: $\tfrac12\ln|1+e^{2x}|+C$. $\tfrac12\ln(1+e^{2x})+C$
  7. $u=1+x$, $x=u-1$: $\int(u-1)u^{1/2}\,du=\tfrac25(1+x)^{5/2}-\tfrac23(1+x)^{3/2}+C$. $\tfrac25(1+x)^{5/2}-\tfrac23(1+x)^{3/2}+C$
  8. $u=\ln x$: $[\tfrac12(\ln x)^2]_1^e=\tfrac12$. $1/2$
  9. $u=\ln x$: $\ln|\ln x|+C$. $\ln(\ln x)+C$
  10. $u=1+x^2$, $du=2x\,dx$: $\int_1^2 u^{-2}\tfrac12 du=\bigl[-\dfrac{1}{2u}\bigr]_1^2=-\dfrac14+\dfrac12=\dfrac14$. $1/4$
  11. $-\ln|\cos x|+C=\ln|\sec x|+C$. $\ln|\sec x|+C$
  12. $u=1+x^2$: $[\sqrt{1+x^2}]_0^{\sqrt3}=2-1=1$. $1$

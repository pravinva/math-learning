MATH141 Revision — AP / GP / Harmonic Set 1

12 exam-style questions · Arithmetic, geometric, harmonic sequences & series

Aligned with Lesson: Arithmetic / Geometric / Harmonic Series.

Scope: AP/GP terms and sums, infinite GP ($|r|<1$), harmonic sequences, harmonic-series divergence, AM–GM–HM. Not power series / Taylor.
  1. Find the $12$th term of the arithmetic sequence $7,10,13,16,\dots$.
  2. An arithmetic sequence has $T_4=11$ and $T_8=23$. Find $a$ and $d$, then $T_{20}$.
  3. The expressions $3x-1$, $x+5$, $2x+4$ are three consecutive terms of an AP. Find $x$.
  4. Find the sum of the first $25$ terms of $4,9,14,19,\dots$.
  5. Find the sum of all multiples of $5$ from $5$ to $100$ inclusive.
  6. An arithmetic series has $S_{12}=186$ and $S_6=45$. Find $a$ and $d$.
  7. A geometric sequence has first term $5$ and common ratio $2$. Find $T_8$.
  8. A geometric sequence has $T_1=3$ and $T_4=24$. Find the real common ratio $r$.
  9. Find $S_{10}$ for the geometric series $2+6+18+54+\cdots$.
  10. Find $S_\infty$ for $16+8+4+2+\cdots$, or explain why it does not exist.
  11. Write the first four terms of the harmonic sequence whose corresponding AP is $2,5,8,11,\dots$.
  12. For positive numbers $4$ and $16$, compute AM, GM, and HM, and verify $\mathrm{AM}\ge\mathrm{GM}\ge\mathrm{HM}$.

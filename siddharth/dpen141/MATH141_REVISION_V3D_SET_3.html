MATH141 Revision — Vectors & 3D Set 3

12 exam-style questions
  1. Find \(\lambda\) such that vectors \((1,\lambda,2)\) and \((2,-1,4)\) are perpendicular.
  2. If \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=12\), \(|\mathbf{a}|=4\), \(|\mathbf{b}|=5\), find \(\theta\) and \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|\).
  3. Find the equation of the sphere with diameter endpoints \(A(1,2,3)\), \(B(5,-2,1)\).
  4. Find plane through line \(\mathbf{r}=(1,1,0)+t(2,-1,3)\) and parallel to \((1,2,1)\).
  5. Find angle between lines \((x-1)/2=(y+2)/1=(z-3)/(-2)\) and \((x+1)/1=(y-4)/2=(z+2)/2\).
  6. Find perpendicular distance from \(P(2,3,-1)\) to line \(\mathbf{r}=(1,0,2)+t(2,2,-1)\).
  7. Determine if point \(Q(3,1,4)\) lies on plane through \(A(1,0,2)\), \(B(2,3,1)\), \(C(4,-1,0)\).
  8. Find equation of plane equidistant from points \(U(1,2,3)\), \(V(5,4,-1)\).
  9. Find intersection of line \(\mathbf{r}=(2,-1,0)+t(1,3,2)\) with planes \(x+y+z=4\) and \(2x-y+z=3\). Comment.
  10. Find acute angle between vector \((3,-2,6)\) and plane \(2x+z=0\).
  11. Find coordinates of foot of perpendicular from \(P(4,1,2)\) to plane \(x-2y+2z-3=0\).
  12. Show lines \(L_1:\mathbf{r}=(0,0,0)+s(1,2,3)\), \(L_2:\mathbf{r}=(1,1,1)+t(2,4,6)\) are parallel and find distance between them.

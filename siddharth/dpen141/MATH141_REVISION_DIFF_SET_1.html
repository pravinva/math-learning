MATH141 Revision — Differentiation Set 1

12 exam-style questions · Week 8: power, product, quotient
Scope: Differentiate combinations using the power, product and quotient rules. Simplify carefully.
  1. Differentiate $f(x)=x^5-3x^2+\dfrac{2}{x}$.
  2. Differentiate $f(x)=(3x^2-1)(x^4+2x)$ using the product rule, and expand the derivative fully.
  3. Find $f'(x)$ for $f(x)=\dfrac{2x+1}{x-3}$. Simplify fully.
  4. Differentiate $y=\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+1}$ and show that $y'=\dfrac{2x}{(x^2+1)^2}$.
  5. If $f(x)=(x^2+1)(2x-5)$, find the equation of the tangent at $x=1$.
  6. Differentiate $f(x)=\dfrac{x^3-1}{x}$. Rewrite first if helpful.
  7. Find all $x$ where $f(x)=\dfrac{x}{x^2+4}$ has a horizontal tangent.
  8. Differentiate $y=(2x-1)^3$ by expanding, then confirm with the chain rule.
  9. For $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4}{x+2}$, $x\neq-2$, simplify and differentiate.
  10. Find $f'(2)$ for $f(x)=(x^3-x)(x+1)$.
  11. Differentiate $y=\dfrac{\sqrt{x}}{x+1}$ for $x>0$.
  12. Show that if $y=\dfrac{ax+b}{cx+d}$ with $ad-bc\neq0$, then $y'=\dfrac{ad-bc}{(cx+d)^2}$.

MATH141 Revision — Complex + Polar Set 1

12 exam-style questions
  1. Write \(z=-3+3\sqrt3\,i\) in polar form \(re^{i\theta}\) with \(0\le\theta<2\pi\).
  2. Compute \(\frac{(2-3i)(1+4i)}{3-2i}\) in \(a+bi\) form.
  3. Solve \(z^2-(4-2i)z+(5-10i)=0\).
  4. If \(z=2(\cos\frac{\pi}{5}+i\sin\frac{\pi}{5})\), find \(z^5\).
  5. Find all cube roots of \(8(\cos\pi+i\sin\pi)\).
  6. Show locus \(|z-(1+i)|=3\) is a circle; give center and radius.
  7. Convert \(z=5(\cos210^\circ+i\sin210^\circ)\) to Cartesian form.
  8. For \(z_1=1-2i\), \(z_2=-3+i\), compute \(\arg(z_1z_2)\) principal value.
  9. Find modulus and argument of \(w=\frac{1+i}{1-\sqrt3\,i}\).
  10. Express \(4\cis\frac{2\pi}{3}+3\cis\frac{5\pi}{6}\) in Cartesian form.
  11. Solve \(z^4=16\left(\cos\frac{2\pi}{3}+i\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)\).
  12. If \(z+\bar z=6\) and \(|z|=5\), find all possible \(z\).

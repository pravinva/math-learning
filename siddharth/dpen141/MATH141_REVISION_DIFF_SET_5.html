MATH141 Revision — Differentiation Set 5

12 exam-style questions · Week 9: hyperbolic functions & derivatives
Scope: Definitions, identities, and derivatives of hyperbolic functions.
  1. Using $\cosh x=\dfrac{e^x+e^{-x}}{2}$ and $\sinh x=\dfrac{e^x-e^{-x}}{2}$, prove $\cosh^2x-\sinh^2x=1$.
  2. Differentiate $y=\sinh(3x)$ from first definitions or known rules.
  3. Differentiate $y=\cosh(x^2)$.
  4. Find $\dfrac{d}{dx}\tanh x$ and express it in terms of $\operatorname{sech}x$.
  5. Show that $\dfrac{d}{dx}\sinh^{-1}x=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}$.
  6. Differentiate $y=\ln(\cosh x)$.
  7. Solve $\sinh x=0$ and $\cosh x=1$.
  8. Find the tangent to $y=\cosh x$ at $x=0$.
  9. Differentiate $y=x\sinh x$ and simplify.
  10. Prove that $\sinh(2x)=2\sinh x\cosh x$ from the exponential definitions.
  11. Find $y''$ if $y=\sinh x+\cosh x$, and relate $y''$ to $y$.
  12. Differentiate $y=\tanh^{-1}(x/2)$ for $|x|<2$.

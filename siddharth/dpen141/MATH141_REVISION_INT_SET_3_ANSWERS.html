MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 3 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $2\int_0^2(4-x^2)\,dx=2\bigl[4x-x^3/3\bigr]_0^2=\dfrac{32}{3}$. $32/3$
  2. $\int_0^1(x-x^2)\,dx=\tfrac16$. $1/6$
  3. $\int_0^\pi\sin x\,dx=2$. $2$
  4. $\int_0^1(e^x-e^{-x})\,dx=[e^x+e^{-x}]_0^1=e+e^{-1}-2$. $e+1/e-2$
  5. $\int_0^4\sqrt{x}\,dx=\dfrac{16}{3}$. $16/3$
  6. Intersections: $y^2+y-2=0\Rightarrow y=-2,1$. Area $\int_{-2}^1\bigl((2-y)-y^2\bigr)\,dy=\dfrac{9}{2}$. $9/2$
  7. $2\int_0^1(x-x^3)\,dx=\tfrac12$. $1/2$
  8. $2\int_0^2(2-x)\,dx=4$. $4$
  9. Solve $4-x^2=x+2\Rightarrow x^2+x-2=0\Rightarrow x=-2,1$. $\int_{-2}^1\bigl((4-x^2)-(x+2)\bigr)\,dx=\dfrac{9}{2}$. $9/2$
  10. $\int_1^2\bigl(x^{-1}-x^{-2}\bigr)\,dx=[\ln x+1/x]_1^2=\ln2-\tfrac12$. $\ln2-1/2$
  11. Intersect: $\sqrt{x}=x-2\Rightarrow x=4$ ($y=2$) after checking. Also need domain; for $y$-form: $x=y^2$ and $x=y+2$, from $y=0$ to $2$: $\int_0^2\bigl((y+2)-y^2\bigr)\,dy$. $\int_0^2((y+2)-y^2)\,dy$
  12. Write $\cos x-\sin x=\sqrt{2}\cos(x+\pi/4)$. Then $\int_{\pi/4}^{5\pi/4}|\sqrt{2}\cos(x+\pi/4)|\,dx=2\sqrt{2}$. $2\sqrt{2}$

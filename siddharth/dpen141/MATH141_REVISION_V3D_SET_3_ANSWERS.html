V3D Set 3 — Answers

  1. \((1,\lambda,2)\cdot(2,-1,4)=0\Rightarrow 2-\lambda+8=0\Rightarrow \lambda=10\).
  2. \(\cos\theta=12/20=3/5\Rightarrow \theta=\cos^{-1}(3/5)\). \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|=|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta=20\cdot4/5=16\).
  3. Center \((3,0,2)\), radius \(\frac12|AB|=\frac12\sqrt{(4)^2+(-4)^2+(-2)^2}=3\). Sphere: \((x-3)^2+y^2+(z-2)^2=9\).
  4. Two direction vectors in plane: \((2,-1,3)\), \((1,2,1)\). Normal \(=(2,-1,3)\times(1,2,1)=(-7,1,5)\). Plane through \((1,1,0)\): \(-7(x-1)+(y-1)+5z=0\Rightarrow 7x-y-5z-6=0\).
  5. Direction vectors \((2,1,-2)\), \((1,2,2)\). \(\cos\theta=\frac{|2+2-4|}{3\cdot3}=0\Rightarrow 90^\circ\).
  6. \(d=\frac{|(P-A)\times \mathbf{d}|}{|\mathbf{d}|}\), \(A=(1,0,2)\), \(\mathbf{d}=(2,2,-1)\), \(P-A=(1,3,-3)\). Cross \(=(3,-5,-4)\), magnitude \(=5\sqrt2\), \(|\mathbf{d}|=3\). So \(d=5\sqrt2/3\).
  7. Plane from \(AB=(1,3,-1)\), \(AC=(3,-1,-2)\): normal \(=(-7,-1,-10)\). Equation \(7x+y+10z-27=0\). At \(Q\): \(21+1+40-27=35\neq0\). Not on plane.
  8. Midpoint \(M=(3,3,1)\), normal \(UV=(4,2,-4)\sim(2,1,-2)\). Plane \(2(x-3)+(y-3)-2(z-1)=0\Rightarrow 2x+y-2z-7=0\).
  9. On line, plane1 gives \(1+6t=4\Rightarrow t=1/2\). plane2 gives \(5+t=3\Rightarrow t=-2\). Different \(t\): line meets each plane at different points, not their intersection line.
  10. Normal of plane \((2,0,1)\). Angle with normal: \(\cos\phi=\frac{|(3,-2,6)\cdot(2,0,1)|}{7\sqrt5}=\frac{12}{7\sqrt5}\). Line-plane angle \(=90^\circ-\phi=\sin^{-1}\!\left(\frac{12}{7\sqrt5}\right)\).
  11. Foot \(F=P-\frac{ax_0+by_0+cz_0+d}{a^2+b^2+c^2}(a,b,c)\). Numerator \(=4-2+4-3=3\), denom \(=9\). So \(F=(4,1,2)-\frac13(1,-2,2)=\left(\frac{11}{3},\frac{5}{3},\frac{4}{3}\right)\).
  12. Directions are proportional, so parallel. Distance \(=\frac{|(1,1,1)\times(1,2,3)|}{|(1,2,3)|}=\frac{|(1,-2,1)|}{\sqrt{14}}=\frac{\sqrt6}{\sqrt{14}}=\sqrt{\frac{3}{7}}.\)

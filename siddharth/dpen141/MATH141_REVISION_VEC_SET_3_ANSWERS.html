Full workings for each question

  1. \(3\mathbf{a}+2\mathbf{b}=(12,-9,0)+(2,4,-4)=(14,-5,-4)\).

    Magnitude \(\sqrt{196+25+16}=\sqrt{237}\).

    \((14,-5,-4)\), magnitude \(\sqrt{237}\)

  2. \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=2-2+0=0\), so \(\cos\theta=0\).

    \(\cos\theta=0\) (right angle)

  3. \(\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=6\), \(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{v}=3\).

    \(\operatorname{proj}=2(1,1,1)=(2,2,2)\).

    Length of projection \(=|\operatorname{proj}|=2\sqrt{3}\) (also \(=|\mathbf{u}|\cos\theta=\dfrac{|\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}|}{|\mathbf{v}|}=\dfrac{6}{\sqrt3}=2\sqrt3\)).

    \((2,2,2)\); length \(2\sqrt3\)

  4. Standard right-handed basis: \(\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j}=\mathbf{k}\), \(\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{k}=\mathbf{i}\), \(\mathbf{k}\times\mathbf{i}=\mathbf{j}\).

    Anticommutative: \(\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{i}=-\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j}=-\mathbf{k}\).

    \(\mathbf{k}\), \(\mathbf{i}\), \(\mathbf{j}\), \(-\mathbf{k}\)

  5. \(\overrightarrow{OA}\times\overrightarrow{OB}=(2,0,0)\times(1,3,0)=(0,0,6)\).

    Area \(=\dfrac12\cdot6=3\) (also base \(2\), height \(3\), area \(\tfrac12\cdot2\cdot3=3\)).

    \(3\)

  6. \(\det\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&1\end{bmatrix}=1(0-1)-1(1-0)+0=-1-1=-2\neq0\).

    Nonzero triple product \(\Rightarrow\) linearly independent (span a parallelepiped of volume \(2\)).

    independent (triple product \(-2\))

  7. Any vector \(\mathbf{v}\) with \(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{n}=0\): \(x+2y+2z=0\).

    Example 1: take \(y=1,z=-1\Rightarrow x=0\), so \(\mathbf{v}_1=(0,1,-1)\), \(|\mathbf{v}_1|=\sqrt2\). Scale to length \(5\): \(\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}(0,1,-1)\).

    Example 2: take \(y=1,z=0\Rightarrow x=-2\), \(\mathbf{v}_2=(-2,1,0)\), \(|\mathbf{v}_2|=\sqrt5\). Scale: \(\dfrac{5}{\sqrt5}(-2,1,0)=\sqrt5(-2,1,0)\).

    e.g. \(\dfrac{5}{\sqrt2}(0,1,-1)\) and \(\sqrt5(-2,1,0)\)

  8. \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|=|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\sin\theta=\sqrt{4+1+4}=3\).

    So \(2\cdot3\cdot\sin\theta=3\Rightarrow\sin\theta=\dfrac12\).

    Also \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=|\mathbf{a}||\mathbf{b}|\cos\theta=3\Rightarrow 6\cos\theta=3\Rightarrow\cos\theta=\dfrac12\).

    Thus \(\theta=60^\circ=\dfrac{\pi}{3}\) (acute, consistent with both).

    \(\theta=\dfrac{\pi}{3}\)

  9. \(\det\begin{bmatrix}2&0&1\\1&2&0\\0&1&3\end{bmatrix}=2(6-0)-0+1(1-0)=12+1=13\).

    Volume \(=13\).

    \(V=13\)

  10. \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=k(1)+1(k)+1(1)=2k+1=0\Rightarrow k=-\dfrac12\).

    \(k=-\dfrac12\)

  11. Parallel part: \(\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf{d}}\mathbf{r}=\dfrac{\mathbf{r}\cdot\mathbf{d}}{\mathbf{d}\cdot\mathbf{d}}\mathbf{d}=\dfrac{3+5}{2}(1,0,1)=4(1,0,1)=(4,0,4)\).

    Perpendicular: \(\mathbf{r}-\operatorname{proj}=(3-4,4,5-4)=(-1,4,1)\).

    Check: \((-1,4,1)\cdot(1,0,1)=-1+1=0\) ✓.

    \((4,0,4)+(-1,4,1)\)

  12. \(\mathbf{q}\times\mathbf{r}=(8-0,\ -(4-0),\ 1-2)=(8,-4,-1)\).

    \(\mathbf{p}\cdot(\mathbf{q}\times\mathbf{r})=3(8)+0+0=24\).

    \(V=\dfrac16\cdot24=4\).

    \(V=4\)

MATH141 Revision — Complex + Polar Set 3

12 exam-style questions
  1. Convert \(z=6\cis\frac{11\pi}{6}\) to Cartesian form.
  2. Find all \(z\) such that \(z^6=64\).
  3. Given \(z_1=2-i,\ z_2=-1+3i\), compute \(\frac{z_1}{z_2}\) and give modulus-argument.
  4. Solve \(|z-1|=2|z+1|\) and identify locus type.
  5. Find principal value of \(\arg\left((1-i)^5\right)\).
  6. Show roots of \(z^2-2z+5=0\) are conjugates and find their product geometrically on Argand plane.
  7. Find \((\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^7\) expansion form and apply for \(\theta=\pi/7\).
  8. Find all values of \(z\) satisfying \(z+\frac{4}{z}=4\), \(z\neq0\).
  9. Express \(z=\frac{3-4i}{1+2i}\) in \(a+bi\), then polar.
  10. Find image of point \(z=2-3i\) under transformation \(w=iz+1\).
  11. Solve \(z^3=27i\) in trigonometric form.
  12. If \(|z|=3\) and \(\arg z=\alpha\), express \(\Re(z^2)\) in terms of \(\alpha\), then evaluate for \(\alpha=40^\circ\).

MATH141 Revision — Vectors & 3D Set 1

12 exam-style questions
  1. Given \(\mathbf{a}=(2,-1,4)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(-3,5,1)\), find \(|2\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}|\) and the angle between \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\).
  2. Find the projection of \(\mathbf{u}=(4,2,-2)\) onto \(\mathbf{v}=(1,-2,2)\), and hence the perpendicular component.
  3. Compute \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}\) for \(\mathbf{a}=(1,3,-2)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(2,-1,4)\), then find area of triangle formed by \(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\).
  4. Determine if points \(A(1,2,0)\), \(B(3,-1,4)\), \(C(5,-4,8)\) are collinear.
  5. Write vector and symmetric equations of line through \(P(2,-1,3)\), \(Q(5,3,-3)\).
  6. Find equation of plane through \(A(1,0,2)\), \(B(3,-2,1)\), \(C(0,4,-1)\).
  7. Find angle between planes \(2x-y+2z=3\) and \(x+2y-2z=1\).
  8. Find intersection point of line \(\mathbf{r}=(1,2,3)+t(2,-1,1)\) with plane \(x+y+z=7\).
  9. Find shortest distance from \(R(4,-1,2)\) to plane \(3x-2y+6z-5=0\).
  10. Determine if lines \(\mathbf{r}=(1,0,2)+s(2,1,-1)\) and \(\mathbf{r}=(3,-1,1)+t(4,2,-2)\) intersect, are parallel, or skew.
  11. Find equation of plane containing line \(\frac{x-1}{2}=\frac{y+2}{-1}=\frac{z}{3}\) and point \(S(0,1,2)\).
  12. For tetrahedron with adjacent edges \(\mathbf{p}=(1,2,0)\), \(\mathbf{q}=(2,-1,3)\), \(\mathbf{r}=(0,4,1)\), find volume.

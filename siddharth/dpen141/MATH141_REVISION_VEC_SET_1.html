MATH141 Revision — Vectors Set 1

12 exam-style questions · vectors only
  1. Given \(\mathbf{a}=(3,-4,12)\), find \(|\mathbf{a}|\) and the unit vector in the direction of \(\mathbf{a}\).
  2. Find the angle between \(\mathbf{a}=(2,1,-2)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(1,-2,2)\).
  3. Find the projection of \(\mathbf{u}=(3,1,2)\) onto \(\mathbf{v}=(1,0,-1)\), and the perpendicular component.
  4. Compute \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}\) for \(\mathbf{a}=(2,0,-1)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(1,3,2)\), and find the area of the parallelogram they span.
  5. Triangle \(ABC\) has \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(1,2,2)\) and \(\overrightarrow{AC}=(2,-1,1)\). Find its area.
  6. Determine whether \(\mathbf{a}=(1,2,-1)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(2,3,4)\) are orthogonal, and whether \(\mathbf{c}=(2,-4,2)\) is parallel to \(\mathbf{a}\).
  7. Find a vector of magnitude \(6\) that is parallel to \(\mathbf{d}=(2,-1,2)\) and points in the same direction.
  8. Find \(\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})\) for \(\mathbf{a}=(1,0,2)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(2,1,0)\), \(\mathbf{c}=(0,1,3)\), and interpret the result.
  9. Find the volume of the tetrahedron with adjacent edges \(\mathbf{p}=(2,0,0)\), \(\mathbf{q}=(0,3,0)\), \(\mathbf{r}=(0,0,4)\).
  10. Express \(\mathbf{w}=(5,1,-1)\) as a linear combination of \(\mathbf{u}=(1,0,1)\) and \(\mathbf{v}=(2,1,0)\), if possible.
  11. Find all scalars \(k\) such that \(\mathbf{a}=(1,k,2)\) is orthogonal to \(\mathbf{b}=(2,-1,k)\).
  12. Given \(\mathbf{a}=(1,2,2)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(2,0,-1)\), find \(|\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}|\) and the angle between \(\mathbf{a}+\mathbf{b}\) and \(\mathbf{a}\).

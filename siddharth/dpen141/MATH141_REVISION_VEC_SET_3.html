MATH141 Revision — Vectors Set 3

12 exam-style questions · vectors only
  1. Given \(\mathbf{a}=(4,-3,0)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(1,2,-2)\), find \(3\mathbf{a}+2\mathbf{b}\) and its magnitude.
  2. Find \(\cos\theta\) for the angle between \(\mathbf{a}=(1,-1,1)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(2,2,0)\).
  3. Project \(\mathbf{u}=(6,0,0)\) onto \(\mathbf{v}=(1,1,1)\). Also find the length of this projection.
  4. Compute \(\mathbf{i}\times\mathbf{j}\), \(\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{k}\), \(\mathbf{k}\times\mathbf{i}\), and \(\mathbf{j}\times\mathbf{i}\).
  5. Find the area of the triangle with vertices \(O(0,0,0)\), \(A(2,0,0)\), \(B(1,3,0)\).
  6. Determine whether \(\{(1,1,0),(1,0,1),(0,1,1)\}\) are linearly independent by computing the scalar triple product.
  7. Find two different vectors of length \(5\) orthogonal to \(\mathbf{n}=(1,2,2)\).
  8. If \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}=(2,-1,2)\) and \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=3\), find the angle between \(\mathbf{a}\) and \(\mathbf{b}\) given \(|\mathbf{a}|=2\), \(|\mathbf{b}|=3\).
  9. Volume of parallelepiped with edges \((2,0,1)\), \((1,2,0)\), \((0,1,3)\).
  10. Find all scalars \(k\) such that \(\mathbf{a}=(k,1,1)\) and \(\mathbf{b}=(1,k,1)\) are orthogonal.
  11. Write \(\mathbf{r}=(3,4,5)\) as the sum of a vector parallel to \(\mathbf{d}=(1,0,1)\) and a vector perpendicular to \(\mathbf{d}\).
  12. Tetrahedron with edges \(\mathbf{p}=(3,0,0)\), \(\mathbf{q}=(1,2,0)\), \(\mathbf{r}=(1,1,4)\). Find the volume.

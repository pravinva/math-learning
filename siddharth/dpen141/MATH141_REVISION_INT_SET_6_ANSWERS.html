MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 6 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $\dfrac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\pi\sin x\,dx=\dfrac{2}{\pi}$. $2/\pi$
  2. $e-1$. $e-1$
  3. $V=\pi\int_0^4 x\,dx=8\pi$. $8\pi$
  4. $V=\pi\int_0^2 x^4\,dx=\dfrac{32\pi}{5}$. $32\pi/5$
  5. $V=\pi\int_0^1\bigl(x^2-x^4\bigr)\,dx=\pi\bigl[\tfrac13-\tfrac15\bigr]=\dfrac{2\pi}{15}$. $2\pi/15$
  6. $V=\pi\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos^2 x\,dx=\pi\int_0^{\pi/2}\dfrac{1+\cos2x}{2}\,dx=\dfrac{\pi^2}{4}$. $\pi^2/4$
  7. $\dfrac{1}{4}\cdot2\int_0^2 x\,dx=1$. $1$
  8. $V=\pi\int_{-2}^2(4-x^2)^2\,dx=2\pi\int_0^2(16-8x^2+x^4)\,dx=\dfrac{512\pi}{15}$. $512\pi/15$
  9. $V=2\pi\int_0^1 x\cdot\sqrt{x}\,dx=2\pi\int_0^1 x^{3/2}\,dx=\dfrac{4\pi}{5}$. $4\pi/5$
  10. Intersections at $0$ and $1$. Outer radius $\sqrt{x}$, inner radius $x$: $V=\pi\int_0^1(x-x^2)\,dx=\pi/6$. $\pi/6$
  11. Side length $2\sqrt{1-x^2}$, area $4(1-x^2)$, $V=\int_{-1}^1 4(1-x^2)\,dx=\dfrac{16}{3}$. $16/3$
  12. Average $=4/3$, so $c^2=4/3$, $c=2/\sqrt3$. $2/\sqrt{3}$

MATH141 Revision — Differentiation Set 1 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check method and restrictions, not only the final line.
  1. $f'(x)=5x^4-6x-\dfrac{2}{x^2}$. $5x^4-6x-2/x^2$
  2. Expand: $f(x)=3x^6+6x^3-x^4-2x$, so $f'=18x^5-4x^3+18x^2-2$. Product rule gives the same. $18x^5-4x^3+18x^2-2$
  3. Quotient: $\dfrac{2(x-3)-(2x+1)}{(x-3)^2}=\dfrac{-7}{(x-3)^2}$. $-7/(x-3)^2$
  4. $\dfrac{2x(x^2+1)-x^2\cdot2x}{(x^2+1)^2}=\dfrac{2x}{(x^2+1)^2}$. Shown
  5. $f(1)=-6$. $f'=(2x)(2x-5)+(x^2+1)\cdot2$; at $1$: $2(-3)+2(2)=-2$. Tangent $y+6=-2(x-1)$, so $y=-2x-4$. $y=-2x-4$
  6. $f(x)=x^2-x^{-1}$, so $f'=2x+x^{-2}$. $2x+1/x^2$
  7. $f'=\dfrac{(x^2+4)-x\cdot2x}{(x^2+4)^2}=\dfrac{4-x^2}{(x^2+4)^2}=0\Rightarrow x=\pm2$. $x=\pm2$
  8. Expand: $y=8x^3-12x^2+6x-1$, $y'=24x^2-24x+6$. Chain: $y'=3(2x-1)^2\cdot2=6(2x-1)^2=6(4x^2-4x+1)=24x^2-24x+6$. $6(2x-1)^2$
  9. $f(x)=x-2$ for $x\neq-2$, so $f'(x)=1$. $1$ ($x\neq-2$)
  10. $f'=(3x^2-1)(x+1)+(x^3-x)$; at $2$: $11\cdot3+(8-2)=39$. $39$
  11. $\dfrac{\tfrac12 x^{-1/2}(x+1)-x^{1/2}}{(x+1)^2}=\dfrac{1-x}{2\sqrt{x}\,(x+1)^2}$. $(1-x)/(2\sqrt{x}(x+1)^2)$
  12. $\dfrac{a(cx+d)-c(ax+b)}{(cx+d)^2}=\dfrac{ad-bc}{(cx+d)^2}$. Shown

MATH141 Revision — Trig + Log-Log + Power Models Set 3

12 exam-style questions
  1. Solve \(\tan(2x-\pi/6)=\sqrt3\) for \(0\le x<2\pi\).
  2. Find period and phase shift of \(y=4\sin(5x-2)+7\).
  3. Solve \(\ln x+\ln(x-4)=\ln21\) with validity check.
  4. Given \(y=18x^{1.2}\), compute \(y\) at \(x=3\) and \(x=6\); verify scaling ratio.
  5. Linearised model: \(\log_{10} y=1.9+0.55\log_{10}x\). Find \(y\) when \(x=40\).
  6. For data \((x,y)\) with power law relation, explain why plotting \((\log x,\log y)\) helps detect model form.
  7. Solve \(7e^{-0.4t}=1.5\) for \(t\).
  8. Differentiate \(y=x^x\) for \(x>0\).
  9. Find all \(x\in[0,2\pi)\) satisfying \(2\sin^2x-3\sin x+1=0\).
  10. If \(y=kx^n\) and a log-log best-fit line has slope \(1.82\), interpret physically in one sentence.
  11. A drag law is \(F=cv^{1.6}\). If speed doubles, by what factor does drag change?
  12. Evaluate \(\int_{\pi/6}^{\pi/2}\sin(2x)\,dx\).

V3D Set 2 — Answers

  1. \(\hat{\mathbf{u}}=\frac{1}{3}(-2,1,2)\). With \(z\)-axis vector \(\mathbf{k}\): \(\cos\theta=\frac{2}{3}\).
  2. \(\text{rej}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{a}-\frac{\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}}{\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{b}=(3,0,-1)-\frac{1}{21}(1,4,2)\).
  3. Area \(=|(2,1,3)\times(-1,4,0)|=|(-12,-3,9)|=3\sqrt{26}\).
  4. \(\frac{x-1}{3}=\frac{y+2}{2}=\frac{z-4}{-5}\).
  5. \(4(x-2)-2(y-1)+(z+3)=0\Rightarrow 4x-2y+z-3=0\).
  6. \(\sin\alpha=\frac{|(2,1,2)\cdot(1,-2,2)|}{|(2,1,2)|\sqrt{9}}=\frac{4}{9}\), where \(\alpha\) is line-plane angle.
  7. Direction \(\mathbf{d}=(1,1,1)\times(2,-1,1)=(2,1,-3)\). Solve with \(z=\lambda\): \(x=\frac{4-2\lambda}{3},y=\frac{5-\lambda}{3}\).
  8. \(d=\frac{|(-4)-17|}{\sqrt{3^2+(-2)^2+6^2}}=\frac{21}{7}=3\).
  9. Same direction opposite sign, and point \((2,5,4)\) lies on \(L_1\) when \(s=2\). Same line (coincident).
  10. Use reflection formula with signed distance \(D=\frac{3+2(-2)-2(1)+5}{\sqrt{1+4+4}}=\frac{2}{3}\). Reflected point \(A'=A-\frac{2(2)}{9}(1,2,-2)=(\frac{23}{9},-\frac{26}{9},\frac{17}{9})\).
  11. Side lengths: \(|AB|=\sqrt6, |BC|=\sqrt{14}, |CA|=\sqrt{14}\) (isosceles, not scalene). Area \(=\frac12|AB\times AC|=\frac12|(1,2,-1)\times(3,1,2)|=\frac12|(5,-5,-5)|=\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}\).
  12. \(V=|\det\begin{bmatrix}2&0&1\\1&3&-1\\0&2&4\end{bmatrix}|=30\).

MATH141 Revision — 3D Analytic Geometry Set 2

12 exam-style questions · 3D analytic geometry only
  1. Find parametric equations of the line through \(A(2,3,-1)\) with direction \(\mathbf{d}=(1,-2,4)\).
  2. Find the plane through \(P(0,1,2)\) perpendicular to the vector \((3,-1,2)\).
  3. Find the angle between the line \(\mathbf{r}=(0,0,0)+t(1,1,1)\) and the plane \(x-y=0\).
  4. Does the point \(Q(1,1,1)\) lie on the line \(\dfrac{x-2}{1}=\dfrac{y-3}{2}=\dfrac{z}{-1}\)?
  5. Find the shortest distance from \(P(1,2,3)\) to the line through \(A(0,0,0)\) and \(B(1,1,0)\).
  6. Find the intersection line of planes \(2x-y+z=3\) and \(x+y-z=1\).
  7. Classify the lines \(L_1:\mathbf{r}=(1,2,3)+s(2,1,0)\) and \(L_2:\mathbf{r}=(3,3,3)+t(4,2,0)\).
  8. Find the equation of the plane through the three points \(A(1,1,1)\), \(B(2,0,1)\), \(C(1,0,-1)\).
  9. Find the reflection of the point \(B(2,0,1)\) in the plane \(x=0\) (the \(yz\)-plane).
  10. Find all points on the line \(\mathbf{r}=(0,0,0)+t(1,2,2)\) that are at distance \(3\) from the origin.
  11. Find the distance from the origin to the plane \(3x-6y+6z-9=0\), and a unit normal to the plane.
  12. Skew lines: \(L_1:\mathbf{r}=(1,0,0)+s(1,1,0)\), \(L_2:\mathbf{r}=(0,1,1)+t(0,1,1)\). Confirm they are skew and find the shortest distance between them.

MATH141 Revision — Vectors Set 2 — Worked Answers

Full workings for each question

  1. \(2\mathbf{a}-3\mathbf{b}=(2,-2,4)-(0,6,-3)=(2,-8,7)\).

    \(|2\mathbf{a}-3\mathbf{b}|=\sqrt{4+64+49}=\sqrt{117}=3\sqrt{13}\).

    \(3\sqrt{13}\)

  2. \(|\mathbf{u}|=3\). Positive \(y\)-axis direction \(\mathbf{j}=(0,1,0)\).

    \(\cos\theta=\dfrac{\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{j}}{|\mathbf{u}|}=\dfrac{1}{3}\), so \(\theta=\arccos\dfrac13\).

    \(\theta=\arccos\dfrac13\)

  3. \(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=4+0-2=2\), \(\mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{b}=3\).

    \(\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf{b}}\mathbf{a}=\dfrac{2}{3}(1,1,1)\).

    Rejection \(=\mathbf{a}-\operatorname{proj}=\left(4-\dfrac23,-\dfrac23,-2-\dfrac23\right)=\left(\dfrac{10}{3},-\dfrac{2}{3},-\dfrac{8}{3}\right)\).

    \(\left(\dfrac{10}{3},-\dfrac{2}{3},-\dfrac{8}{3}\right)\)

  4. \((3,1,0)\times(1,2,-2)=(-2,6,5)\).

    Area \(=\sqrt{4+36+25}=\sqrt{65}\).

    \(\sqrt{65}\)

  5. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(1,1,1)\), \(\overrightarrow{AC}=(-1,2,1)\).

    \(\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}=(-1,-2,3)\), area \(=\dfrac12\sqrt{1+4+9}=\dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2}\).

    \(\dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2}\)

  6. \(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c}=\begin{vmatrix}\mathbf{i}&\mathbf{j}&\mathbf{k}\\4&5&6\\7&8&9\end{vmatrix}=(-3,6,-3)\).

    \(\mathbf{a}\cdot(\mathbf{b}\times\mathbf{c})=1(-3)+2(6)+3(-3)=-3+12-9=0\).

    Triple product \(0\Rightarrow\) the three vectors are coplanar (lie in one plane through the origin).

    \(0\); coplanar

  7. \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}=(-1,1,1)\), \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}|=\sqrt{3}\).

    Unit vectors: \(\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(-1,1,1)\). Either is acceptable.

    \(\pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(-1,1,1)\)

  8. \(\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=2-3-1=-2\), \(\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{v}=3\).

    Parallel: \(\operatorname{proj}=-\dfrac{2}{3}(1,-1,1)=\left(-\dfrac23,\dfrac23,-\dfrac23\right)\).

    Perpendicular: \(\mathbf{u}-\operatorname{proj}=\left(2+\dfrac23,3-\dfrac23,-1+\dfrac23\right)=\left(\dfrac{8}{3},\dfrac{7}{3},-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\).

    parallel \(\left(-\dfrac23,\dfrac23,-\dfrac23\right)\); perp \(\left(\dfrac{8}{3},\dfrac{7}{3},-\dfrac{1}{3}\right)\)

  9. \(\det\begin{bmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&1\\1&0&1\end{bmatrix}=1(1)-1(0-1)+0=1+1=2\).

    Volume \(=|2|=2\).

    \(V=2\)

  10. Parallel \(\Rightarrow\mathbf{a}=k\mathbf{b}\): \(t=2k\), \(1=kt\), \(2=-4k\).

    From third: \(k=-\dfrac12\). Then \(t=2(-\tfrac12)=-1\), and \(1=(-\tfrac12)(-1)=\tfrac12\) — contradiction.

    Alternatively: \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}=\mathbf{0}\). Components give \(t^2+2=0\) from one pairing with inconsistency on others → no real \(t\).

    Check: directions \((t,1,2)\) and \((2,t,-4)\). For parallel need \(t/2=1/t=2/(-4)\). From \(2/(-4)=-1/2\), so \(1/t=-1/2\Rightarrow t=-2\), but then \(t/2=-1\neq-1/2\). Impossible.

    no such \(t\)

  11. \(|\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}|^2=|\mathbf{a}|^2+|\mathbf{b}|^2-2\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=1+1-2(\tfrac12)=1\).

    So \(|\mathbf{a}-\mathbf{b}|=1\).

    \(1\)

  12. \(\mathbf{q}\times\mathbf{r}=(1\cdot1-1\cdot1,\ 1\cdot0-1\cdot1,\ 1\cdot1-0\cdot1)=(0,-1,1)\).

    \(\mathbf{p}\cdot(\mathbf{q}\times\mathbf{r})=1(0)+1(-1)+0(1)=-1\).

    \(V=\dfrac16|-1|=\dfrac16\).

    \(V=\dfrac16\)

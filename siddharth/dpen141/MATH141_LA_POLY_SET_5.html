  3. Use Cramer's rule for \(3x+2y=11,\ 4x-y=5\).

  10. Remainder of \(P(x)=x^5+3x^2-2x+9\) on division by \(x-3\).
  11. Check if \(x+3\) is a factor of \(R(x)=x^3-3x^2-9x+27\), then factorise.
  12. Find \(a,b\) so that \(x^3+ax^2+bx+4\) has factors \((x+2)\) and \((x-1)\).

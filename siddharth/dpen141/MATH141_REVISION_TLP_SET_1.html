MATH141 Revision — Trig + Log-Log + Power Models Set 1

12 exam-style questions
  1. Solve \(2\sin(2x-\pi/3)=1\) for \(0\le x<2\pi\).
  2. For \(y=3\cos(4x+\pi/6)-2\), state amplitude, period, phase shift, range.
  3. Solve \(3^{2x-1}=7\) exactly and to 3 d.p.
  4. If \(y=ax^b\), show \(\log y=\log a+b\log x\).
  5. Given log-log data points \((\log x,\log y)=(0.3,1.1)\), \((1.0,2.5)\), estimate power law \(y=ax^b\).
  6. For model \(P=kt^n\), explain meaning of \(n>1\), \(0<n<1\), \(n<0\).
  7. Solve \(\log_2(x-1)+\log_2(x+3)=4\) with domain check.
  8. Differentiate \(y=x^2\sin(3x)\).
  9. A signal has \(V(t)=5\sin(\pi t/6)+12\). Find all \(t\in[0,24]\) where \(V(t)\ge15\).
  10. Linearised fit gives \(\log_{10}y=0.72+1.35\log_{10}x\). Write \(y\) as \(Cx^m\).
  11. Given \(y=40x^{-0.8}\), find elasticity \(E=\frac{d\ln y}{d\ln x}\) and interpret.
  12. Find least positive period of \(f(x)=\sin(3x)+\cos(2x)\).

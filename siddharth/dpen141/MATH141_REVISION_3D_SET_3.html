MATH141 Revision — 3D Analytic Geometry Set 3

12 exam-style questions · 3D analytic geometry only
  1. Find symmetric equations of the line of intersection of \(x+y+z=0\) and \(x-y=1\).
  2. Find the plane through \(A(2,0,0)\), \(B(0,2,0)\), \(C(0,0,2)\).
  3. Find the angle between planes \(x=0\) and \(x+y+z=1\).
  4. Find where the line \(\dfrac{x}{1}=\dfrac{y-1}{2}=\dfrac{z+1}{-1}\) meets the plane \(z=0\).
  5. Find the distance from \(P(4,0,-1)\) to the plane \(2x+y-2z=5\).
  6. A line passes through \(A(1,1,1)\) and is parallel to the intersection of planes \(x+y=0\) and \(y+z=0\). Find its vector equation.
  7. Determine if planes \(2x-y+z=1\) and \(-4x+2y-2z=3\) are parallel, coincident, or intersecting.
  8. Find the point on the plane \(x+y+z=3\) closest to the origin.
  9. Find parametric equations of the line through the midpoints of segments \(AB\) and \(CD\), where \(A(0,0,0)\), \(B(2,0,0)\), \(C(0,2,0)\), \(D(0,0,2)\).
  10. Find the equation of the plane containing the point \(P(1,2,3)\) and perpendicular to both planes \(x+y=1\) and \(y+z=2\).
  11. Find the acute angle between the line \(\mathbf{r}=t(1,0,1)\) and the line \(\mathbf{r}=(0,1,0)+s(1,1,0)\).
  12. Lines \(L_1:\mathbf{r}=(0,0,1)+s(1,0,0)\) and \(L_2:\mathbf{r}=(0,1,0)+t(0,0,1)\). Show they are skew and find the shortest distance.

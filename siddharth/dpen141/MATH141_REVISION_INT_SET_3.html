MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 3

12 exam-style questions · Week 11: area between curves
Scope: Find areas by integrating with respect to $x$ or $y$; identify intersections carefully.
  1. Find the area between $y=x^2$ and $y=4$.
  2. Find the area enclosed by $y=x$ and $y=x^2$.
  3. Find the area between $y=\sin x$ and the $x$-axis from $0$ to $\pi$.
  4. Find the area between $y=e^x$ and $y=e^{-x}$ from $x=0$ to $x=1$.
  5. Find the area of the region bounded by $y=\sqrt{x}$, $y=0$ and $x=4$.
  6. Find the area between $x=y^2$ and $x=2-y$.
  7. Find the total area between $y=x^3-x$ and the $x$-axis from $x=-1$ to $x=1$.
  8. Find the area between $y=|x|$ and $y=2$ from $x=-2$ to $x=2$.
  9. Find the area enclosed by $y=4-x^2$ and $y=x+2$.
  10. Find the area between $y=\dfrac{1}{x}$ and $y=\dfrac{1}{x^2}$ from $x=1$ to $x=2$.
  11. Set up (but do not evaluate a more complicated form) the area between $y=\sqrt{x}$ and $y=x-2$ by integrating with respect to $y$, after finding intersection points.
  12. Find the area between $y=\cos x$ and $y=\sin x$ from $x=\pi/4$ to $x=5\pi/4$.

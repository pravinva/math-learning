MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 5

12 exam-style questions · Week 12: choosing the right integration technique
Scope: Diagnose the method (table, identity, split, substitution) then integrate.
  1. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\sin^2 x\,dx$ using a double-angle identity.
  2. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^2+1}{x}\,dx$.
  3. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x+3}{x^2+3x+2}\,dx$ by factoring the denominator and splitting.
  4. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4}\cos^2 x\,dx$.
  5. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x+1}{\sqrt{x}}\,dx$.
  6. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int e^x\sin x\,dx$ by parts twice (or state the cyclic method).
  7. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{x^2+4}\,dx$.
  8. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int_0^1\dfrac{1}{1+x^2}\,dx$.
  9. Find $\displaystyle\int x\cos x\,dx$ by parts.
  10. Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^3+2x}{x}\,dx$ after simplifying.
  11. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{\cos x}{\sin^2 x}\,dx$.
  12. Decide the method and evaluate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{3x^2+2}{x^3+2x+5}\,dx$.

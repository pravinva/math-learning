MATH141 Revision — Parametric + Special Functions Set 1

12 exam-style questions · Foundation to intermediate
Scope: eliminating parameters, identifying the traced portion and direction of a curve, piecewise/absolute-value/signum functions, and derivative reasoning from first principles.
  1. A curve is given by $x=2t-1$ and $y=t^2+3$, where $-1\le t\le2$. Eliminate $t$, state the Cartesian equation, and give the exact range of $x$ traced.
  2. For $x=3\cos t$, $y=2\sin t$, $0\le t\le\pi$, identify the Cartesian curve, specify the portion traced, and state the direction of travel.
  3. Let $x=t+\dfrac1t$ and $y=t-\dfrac1t$, where $t>0$. Find a Cartesian equation and determine which branch is traced. Justify the restriction on $x$.
  4. The curve $x=t^2-1$, $y=t^3-t$ passes through the origin for more than one parameter value. Find those values and the corresponding tangent gradients using $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$.
  5. Write $f(x)=|2x-6|-x$ as a piecewise function. Hence solve $f(x)=3$ and identify the corner of its graph.
  6. Express $g(x)=\begin{cases}-2,&x<-1\\0,&-1\le x\le2\\3,&x>2\end{cases}$ using signum functions only, together with constants.
  7. Find the value of $k$ for which $f(x)=\begin{cases}kx+1,&x<2\\x^2-k,&x\ge2\end{cases}$ is continuous at $x=2$. Is the resulting function differentiable there?
  8. Solve exactly $|x-1|+|x+2|=5$. Your method must show the relevant intervals.
  9. Use the limit definition of the derivative to find $f'(x)$ for $f(x)=x^2-3x$. Do not quote a differentiation rule.
  10. Use one-sided difference quotients to prove that $f(x)=|x-4|$ is not differentiable at $x=4$.
  11. Starting from the limit definition, derive $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\dfrac1x\right)$ for $x\ne0$.
  12. A particle moves according to $x=t^2-2t$, $y=t^3-3t$. Find every parameter value at which the tangent is horizontal or vertical, and give the corresponding points.

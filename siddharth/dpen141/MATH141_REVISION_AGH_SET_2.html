MATH141 Revision — AP / GP / Harmonic Set 2

12 exam-style questions · Arithmetic, geometric, harmonic sequences & series

Aligned with Lesson: Arithmetic / Geometric / Harmonic Series.

Scope: AP/GP terms and sums, infinite GP ($|r|<1$), harmonic sequences, harmonic-series divergence, AM–GM–HM. Not power series / Taylor.
  1. Find $n$ if the $n$th term of the AP with $a=-3$, $d=4$ is $45$.
  2. Find the sum of the first $n$ terms of an AP with $a=7$, $d=-2$, and hence find $n$ when $S_n=0$ (take the positive $n>1$).
  3. An auditorium has $18$ seats in the first row and each next row has $3$ more seats. There are $20$ rows. How many seats in total?
  4. Decide whether $5,10,20,40$ is arithmetic, geometric, both, or neither. Justify.
  5. A geometric sequence has $a=81$ and $r=\dfrac13$. Find $T_5$ and $S_5$.
  6. Find $S_6$ for $-2,6,-18,54,\dots$.
  7. A geometric series has $a=4$ and $r=\dfrac12$. Find the smallest $n$ such that $S_n>7.5$.
  8. Find $S_\infty$ for $9-3+1-\dfrac13+\cdots$, or explain why it does not exist.
  9. Express the recurring decimal $0.\overline{27}=0.272727\ldots$ as a fraction using a geometric sum to infinity.
  10. A ball is dropped from $10\,\mathrm{m}$ and rebounds to $60\%$ of the height fallen. Find the total vertical distance travelled (to infinity).
  11. Explain in one or two sentences why the harmonic series $1+\dfrac12+\dfrac13+\dfrac14+\cdots$ diverges, even though terms $\to0$.
  12. If $a,b,c$ are in geometric progression and $a,b,c>0$ with $a=4$, $c=16$, find $b$ using the GM (or $b^2=ac$).

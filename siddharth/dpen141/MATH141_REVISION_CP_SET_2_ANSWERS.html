CP Set 2 — Answers

  1. \(r=4\sqrt2,\ \theta=225^\circ\): \(4\sqrt2\cis225^\circ\).
  2. \((\cis(-45^\circ))^{12}=\cis(-540^\circ)=\cis180^\circ=-1\).
  3. \(z^3=-8=8\cis\pi\Rightarrow z=2\cis\left(\frac{\pi+2k\pi}{3}\right)\): \(2\cis\frac{\pi}{3},2\cis\pi,2\cis\frac{5\pi}{3}\).
  4. \(\bar z=3\cis(-40^\circ)\) (or \(320^\circ\)).
  5. Perpendicular bisector of \(2i,-2i\): \(y=0\).
  6. \(2+2i=2\sqrt2\cis45^\circ\Rightarrow (2+2i)^8=(2\sqrt2)^8\cis360^\circ=4096\).
  7. Point is on unit circle at \(120^\circ\), principal argument \(2\pi/3\).
  8. \(z=-3\pm2i\). Polar: \(r=\sqrt{13}\), angles \(\pi-\tan^{-1}(2/3)\) and \(\pi+\tan^{-1}(2/3)\).
  9. \(-16i=16\cis(-\pi/2)\). Roots: \(2\cis\left(\frac{-\pi/2+2k\pi}{4}\right)\), \(k=0,1,2,3\).
  10. \(\frac1z=\frac{1-\sqrt3 i}{4}\). Modulus \(1/2\), argument \(-60^\circ\).
  11. \(z=5\cis300^\circ=\frac52-\frac{5\sqrt3}{2}i\).
  12. Let \(z=x+iy\): \(x^2+y^2-8x+15=0\Rightarrow (x-4)^2+y^2=1\): circle center \((4,0)\), radius \(1\).

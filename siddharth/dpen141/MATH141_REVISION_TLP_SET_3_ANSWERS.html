TLP Set 3 — Answers

  1. \(2x-\pi/6=\pi/3+k\pi\Rightarrow x=\pi/4+\frac{k\pi}{2}\), list \(k=0,1,2,3\).
  2. Period \(=2\pi/5\), phase shift \(=2/5\) right, midline 7.
  3. \(x(x-4)=21\Rightarrow x^2-4x-21=0\Rightarrow x=7,-3\). Valid \(x>4\): \(x=7\).
  4. \(y(3)=18\cdot3^{1.2}\), \(y(6)=18\cdot6^{1.2}\). Ratio \(y(6)/y(3)=2^{1.2}\).
  5. \(y=10^{1.9}x^{0.55}\Rightarrow y=10^{1.9}\cdot40^{0.55}\approx272\).
  6. Power law becomes linear in logs; slope gives exponent, intercept gives coefficient.
  7. \(e^{-0.4t}=1.5/7\Rightarrow t=-\ln(1.5/7)/0.4\approx3.85\).
  8. \(\ln y=x\ln x\Rightarrow y'/y=\ln x+1\Rightarrow y'=x^x(\ln x+1)\).
  9. \((2\sin x-1)(\sin x-1)=0\Rightarrow \sin x=1/2\) or \(1\): \(x=\pi/6,5\pi/6,\pi/2\).
  10. Output scales more than linearly: \(x\) multiplied by \(m\) implies \(y\) multiplied by \(m^{1.82}\).
  11. Factor \(=2^{1.6}\approx3.03\).
  12. \(\int\sin(2x)\,dx=-\frac12\cos(2x)\). Value \(=\left[-\frac12\cos(2x)\right]_{\pi/6}^{\pi/2}=3/4\).

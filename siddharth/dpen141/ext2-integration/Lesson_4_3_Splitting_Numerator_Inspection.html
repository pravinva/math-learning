Splitting numerators & inspection

Rewrite a rational expression until each piece advertises its own antiderivative: a polynomial, a logarithm, an arctangent, or a simple partial fraction.

1 Make improper fractions proper

Division first

Partial fractions and inspection begin with a proper fraction: degree(numerator) $<$ degree(denominator). If not, divide polynomials first. The quotient integrates separately; the remainder becomes the rational fraction to analyse.

Worked example

$$\frac{x^2+3x+1}{x+1}=x+2-\frac1{x+1}.$$ Therefore its integral is $x^2/2+2x-\ln|x+1|+C$.

2 Match a denominator derivative

Inspection move

For a quadratic $Q(x)$, try to write the numerator as $A Q'(x)+B$. The $AQ'/Q$ term gives $A\ln|Q|$; the leftover $B/Q$ often uses completing the square.

Worked example

$$\frac{5x+1}{x^2+4x+8}=\frac{5}{2}\frac{2x+4}{x^2+4x+8}-\frac9{2((x+2)^2+4)}.$$

The antiderivative is $\frac{5}{2}\ln(x^2+4x+8)-\frac94\arctan((x+2)/2)+C$.

3 Simple linear factors

Inspection partial fractions

With distinct linear factors, write one constant numerator over each factor. Values that make a factor zero isolate a coefficient immediately: this is a fast consequence of an identity, not a separate magic method.

Worked example

$\frac{2x+5}{(x+1)(x+2)}=\frac A{x+1}+\frac B{x+2}$. At $x=-1$, $3=A$; at $x=-2$, $1=-B$, so $A=3,B=-1$. Integrate $3\ln|x+1|-\ln|x+2|+C$.

4 Exponential and trig variants

The same derivative idea

Inspection is broader than rational functions. $\frac{e^x}{1+e^x}$ is $f'/f$. Also $\frac{\cos x}{3+\sin x}$ is $f'/f$. In a trig denominator, rewrite first if necessary to expose the derivative.

Worked example

$\int\frac{\sin x}{2+\cos x}dx=-\ln|2+\cos x|+C$, because $d(2+\cos x)=-\sin xdx$.

5 Pitfalls and verification

Check structure

Do not split a fraction across a sum in the denominator: $\frac1{x+1} e\frac1x+1$. After a rewrite, recombine it once to check it equals the original. After integrating, differentiate the final answer to catch a missed coefficient.

P Practice with full solutions

Attempt each question before revealing the solution. A correct method matters as much as the final antiderivative: keep constants, show substitutions, and differentiate your answer when unsure.

Practice 1

$\int\frac{x^2+2x+3}{x+1}dx.$

Worked solution

Divide: $x^2+2x+3=(x+1)(x+1)+2$. Integrate $x+1+2/(x+1)$ to get $x^2/2+x+2\ln|x+1|+C$.

Practice 2

$\int\frac{2x+3}{x^2+3x+7}dx.$

Worked solution

$2x+3=2(x+3/2)$. So answer $\ln(x^2+3x+7)+C$.

Practice 3

$\int\frac{x+5}{x^2+4x+8}dx.$

Worked solution

$x+5=\frac{1}{2}(2x+4)+3$. Complete square $(x+2)^2+4$: $\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+4x+8)+\frac{3}{2}\arctan((x+2)/2)+C$.

Practice 4

$\int\frac{3x+2}{(x-1)(x+2)}dx.$

Worked solution

$3x+2=A(x+2)+B(x-1)$. $x=1$: $A=5/3$; $x=-2$: $B=4/3$. Result $\frac53\ln|x-1|+\frac43\ln|x+2|+C$.

Practice 5

$\int\frac{dx}{x^2-4}$.

Worked solution

$1/(x^2-4)=\frac14(1/(x-2)-1/(x+2))$. Answer $\frac14\ln|\frac{x-2}{x+2}|+C$.

Practice 6

$\int\frac{e^x}{4+e^x}dx.$

Worked solution

$u=4+e^x$, $du=e^xdx$. Answer $\ln(4+e^x)+C$.

Practice 7

$\int\frac{\cos x}{2-\sin x}dx.$

Worked solution

$u=2-\sin x$, $du=-\cos xdx$. Answer $-\ln|2-\sin x|+C$.

Practice 8

$\int\frac{x^3}{x^2+1}dx.$

Worked solution

Division: $x^3/(x^2+1)=x-x/(x^2+1)$. Answer $x^2/2-\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+1)+C$.

Practice 9

$\int\frac{4x+7}{x^2+2x+10}dx.$

Worked solution

$4x+7=2(2x+2)+3$ and denominator $(x+1)^2+9$. Answer $2\ln(x^2+2x+10)+\arctan((x+1)/3)+C$.

Practice 10

$\int\frac{2\sin x}{3-\cos x}dx.$

Worked solution

$u=3-\cos x$, $du=\sin xdx$. Answer $2\ln|3-\cos x|+C$.

Practice 11

Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^3+2x}{x+1}\,dx$ by polynomial rewriting.

Worked solution

$x^3+2x=(x+1)(x^2-x+3)-3$, so $\dfrac{x^3}{3}-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+3x-3\ln|x+1|+C$.

Practice 12

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^2}{x^2+9}\,dx$ by writing $1$ minus a proper fraction.

Worked solution

$1-\dfrac{9}{x^2+9}$: $x-3\tan^{-1}(x/3)+C$.

Practice 13

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x^3+3}{x^2+1}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$2x-\ln(x^2+1)+3\tan^{-1}x+C$.

Practice 14

By inspection, integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{5x-7}{(x-1)(x-2)}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$\dfrac{2}{x-1}+\dfrac{3}{x-2}$: $2\ln|x-1|+3\ln|x-2|+C$.

Practice 15

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{e^{2x}+e^{x}}{e^{x}+2}\,dx$.

Worked solution

With $u=e^{x}$: $e^{x}-\ln|e^{x}+2|+C$.

Practice 16

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{\sin 2x}{3+\sin^2 x}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$u=3+\sin^2 x$, $du=\sin 2x\,dx$: $\ln|3+\sin^2 x|+C$.

Practice 17

Evaluate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{4x+1}{x^2-1}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$\dfrac{5/2}{x-1}+\dfrac{3/2}{x+1}$: $\dfrac52\ln|x-1|+\dfrac32\ln|x+1|+C$.

Practice 18

Why does quick inspection fail for $\dfrac{x}{(x^2+1)(x+1)}$, and what form must you use?

Worked solution

Need $\dfrac{Ax+B}{x^2+1}+\dfrac{C}{x+1}$ — an irreducible quadratic forces a linear numerator.

Practice 19

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^4+1}{x^2+1}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$x^2-1+\dfrac{2}{x^2+1}$: $\dfrac{x^3}{3}-x+2\tan^{-1}x+C$.

Practice 20

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{3x+11}{x^2+6x+13}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$3x+11=\dfrac32(2x+6)+2$: $\dfrac32\ln(x^2+6x+13)+\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{x+3}{2}\right)+C$.

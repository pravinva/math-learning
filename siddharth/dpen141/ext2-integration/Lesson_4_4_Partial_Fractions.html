1 The setup rules

Templates follow factors

First make the fraction proper. A distinct linear factor $(x-a)$ needs $A/(x-a)$. A repeated factor $(x-a)^n$ needs every power: $A_1/(x-a)+\cdots+A_n/(x-a)^n$. An irreducible quadratic $x^2+bx+c$ needs $(Ax+B)/(x^2+bx+c)$.

Why a linear numerator?

A constant over a quadratic cannot represent every possible rational numerator after recombination. The $Ax+B$ template gives enough freedom to match both the $x$ coefficient and constant term.

2 Distinct linear factors

Worked example: cover-up

$$\frac{5x+1}{(x-1)(x+3)}=\frac A{x-1}+\frac B{x+3}.$$ Multiply through. At $x=1$, $6=4A$, so $A=3/2$. At $x=-3$, $-14=-4B$, so $B=7/2$. Integrate each log term.

When cover-up works

Substituting roots isolates a coefficient only for a factor that occurs once. Repeated factors and irreducible quadratics need coefficient comparison or extra equations.

3 Repeated factors

Do not skip powers

For $(x-2)^3$, include $A/(x-2)+B/(x-2)^2+C/(x-2)^3$. Missing a term creates too few degrees of freedom and makes coefficient equations inconsistent.

Worked example

$$\frac1{x(x-1)^2}=\frac Ax+\frac B{x-1}+\frac C{(x-1)^2}.$$ Multiplying out and using $x=0,1$ gives $A=1,C=1$; comparing $x^2$ coefficients gives $B=-1$. Integrate to $\ln|x|-\ln|x-1|-1/(x-1)+C$.

4 Irreducible quadratics

Log plus arctan

Once $(Ax+B)/(x^2+bx+c)$ is found, rewrite $Ax+B=K(2x+b)+L$. Integrate the first term as a logarithm and complete the square for the second, which becomes an arctangent pattern.

Worked example

$$\int\frac{x+4}{x^2+2x+5}dx=\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+2x+5)+\frac{3}{2}\arctan\frac{x+1}{2}+C.$$

5 Solving coefficients reliably

Three equivalent routes

After clearing denominators, you have a polynomial identity. Use convenient roots first, then compare coefficients; or expand and equate coefficients from the start; or solve the resulting simultaneous equations by elimination/substitution. Check by recombining before integrating.

P Practice with full solutions

Attempt each question before revealing the solution. A correct method matters as much as the final antiderivative: keep constants, show substitutions, and differentiate your answer when unsure.

Practice 1

$\int\frac{dx}{(x-1)(x+2)}$.

Worked solution

$1=A(x+2)+B(x-1)$. At $1$, $A=1/3$; at $-2$, $B=-1/3$. Answer $\frac{1}{3}\ln|x-1|-\frac{1}{3}\ln|x+2|+C$.

Practice 2

$\int\frac{3x+4}{(x+1)(x+2)}dx.$

Worked solution

$x=-1$ gives $A=1$; $x=-2$ gives $B=2$. Thus $\ln|x+1|+2\ln|x+2|+C$.

Practice 3

$\int\frac{dx}{x(x+1)^2}$.

Worked solution

$1=A(x+1)^2+Bx(x+1)+Cx$. $x=0$: $A=1$; $x=-1$: $C=-1$; $x^2$: $A+B=0$, $B=-1$. Integral $\ln|x|-\ln|x+1|+1/(x+1)+C$.

Practice 4

$\int\frac{dx}{(x-2)^2}$.

Worked solution

This elementary repeated-factor term is $\int(x-2)^{-2}dx=-1/(x-2)+C$.

Practice 5

$\int\frac{x+1}{x^2+4}dx.$

Worked solution

Split: $\frac{1}{2}\frac{2x}{x^2+4}+\frac1{x^2+4}$. Answer $\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+4)+\frac{1}{2}\arctan(x/2)+C$.

Practice 6

$\int\frac{x+3}{(x+1)(x^2+1)}dx.$

Worked solution

Use $A/(x+1)+(Bx+C)/(x^2+1)$. Clearing: $x+3=A(x^2+1)+(Bx+C)(x+1)$. Coefficients give $A=1,B=-1,C=2$. Integrate $\ln|x+1|-\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^2+1)+2\arctan x+C$.

Practice 7

$\int\frac{2x+1}{(x-1)^2}dx.$

Worked solution

Write $2x+1=2(x-1)+3$. Integral $2\ln|x-1|-3/(x-1)+C$.

Practice 8

$\int\frac{x^2+1}{x^2-1}dx.$

Worked solution

Divide: $1+2/(x^2-1)$. The remainder is $1/(x-1)-1/(x+1)$. Answer $x+\ln|x-1|-\ln|x+1|+C$.

Practice 9

$\int\frac{dx}{(x^2+1)(x^2+4)}$.

Worked solution

$1/((x^2+1)(x^2+4))=\frac{1}{3}(1/(x^2+1)-1/(x^2+4))$. Result $\frac{1}{3}\arctan x-\frac16\arctan(x/2)+C$.

Practice 10

$\int\frac{2x^2+3x+1}{x(x+1)}dx.$

Worked solution

Divide: $2x^2+3x+1=(2x+1)(x^2+x)+1? $ Division gives $2+(x+1)/(x^2+x)=2+1/x$. The cancellation is valid for $x\ne0,-1$, the original domain. Answer $2x+\ln|x|+C$.

Practice 11

Integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^2+2x}{(x+1)^2(x-1)}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$\dfrac{1/2}{x+1}-\dfrac{1/2}{(x+1)^2}+\dfrac{1/2}{x-1}$: $\dfrac12\ln|x^2-1|+\dfrac{1}{2(x+1)}+C$.

Practice 12

Integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x^2(x+1)}$.

Worked solution

$-\ln|x|-\dfrac1x+\ln|x+1|+C=\ln\left|\dfrac{x+1}{x}\right|-\dfrac1x+C$.

Practice 13

Integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x+3}{(x^2+1)(x+1)}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$\dfrac{-\frac12 x+\frac52}{x^2+1}+\dfrac{1/2}{x+1}$: $-\dfrac14\ln(x^2+1)+\dfrac52\tan^{-1}x+\dfrac12\ln|x+1|+C$.

Practice 14

Use cover-up on $\dfrac{7x-1}{(x-2)(x+3)}$, then integrate.

Worked solution

$A=\dfrac{13}{5}$, $B=\dfrac{22}{5}$: $\dfrac{13}{5}\ln|x-2|+\dfrac{22}{5}\ln|x+3|+C$.

Practice 15

Why can cover-up alone not finish $\dfrac{1}{x(x+1)^2}$?

Worked solution

It finds coefficients for $\dfrac1x$ and $\dfrac1{(x+1)^2}$, but the $\dfrac1{x+1}$ term needs equating coefficients or a second substitution.

Practice 16

Integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^3}{x^2-4}\,dx$ (divide first).

Worked solution

$x+\dfrac{4x}{x^2-4}$: $\dfrac{x^2}{2}+2\ln|x^2-4|+C$.

Practice 17

Integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{(x^2+4)(x^2+9)}$.

Worked solution

$\dfrac{1/5}{x^2+4}-\dfrac{1/5}{x^2+9}$: $\dfrac{1}{10}\tan^{-1}\dfrac{x}{2}-\dfrac{1}{15}\tan^{-1}\dfrac{x}{3}+C$.

Practice 18

Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{3x+5}{(x-1)^3}\,dx$ via $u=x-1$.

Worked solution

$-\dfrac{3}{x-1}-\dfrac{4}{(x-1)^2}+C$.

Practice 19

Decompose and integrate $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{2x^2+1}{x(x^2+1)}\,dx$.

Worked solution

$\dfrac1x+\dfrac{x}{x^2+1}$: $\ln|x|+\dfrac12\ln(x^2+1)+C$.

Practice 20

Show that $\dfrac{1}{(x-1)(x-2)}=\dfrac{1}{x-1}+\dfrac{1}{x-2}$ is wrong, and give the correct split.

Worked solution

RHS has numerator $2x-3$. Correct: $-\dfrac{1}{x-1}+\dfrac{1}{x-2}$.

  1. Direction \(\mathbf{d}=\overrightarrow{PQ}=(3,3,-6)\). Can simplify to \((1,1,-2)\).

    Vector form: \(\mathbf{r}=(1,-2,3)+\lambda(1,1,-2)\).

    Symmetric: \(\dfrac{x-1}{1}=\dfrac{y+2}{1}=\dfrac{z-3}{-2}\).

    \(\mathbf{r}=(1,-2,3)+\lambda(1,1,-2)\); \(\dfrac{x-1}{1}=\dfrac{y+2}{1}=\dfrac{z-3}{-2}\)

  2. \(\overrightarrow{AB}=(1,1,-2)\), \(\overrightarrow{AC}=(-1,-1,1)\).

    Normal \(\mathbf{n}=\overrightarrow{AB}\times\overrightarrow{AC}=\begin{vmatrix}\mathbf{i}&\mathbf{j}&\mathbf{k}\\1&1&-2\\-1&-1&1\end{vmatrix}=(-1,1,0)\).

    Through \(A\): \(-(x-1)+(y-0)=0\Rightarrow -x+y+1=0\Rightarrow x-y=1\).

    Check \(B\): \(2-1=1\) ✓; \(C\): \(0-(-1)=1\) ✓.

    \(x-y=1\)

  3. Normals \(\mathbf{n}_1=(1,2,-2)\), \(\mathbf{n}_2=(2,-1,2)\).

    \(\mathbf{n}_1\cdot\mathbf{n}_2=2-2-4=-4\), \(|\mathbf{n}_1|=3\), \(|\mathbf{n}_2|=3\).

    \(\cos\phi=\dfrac{|\mathbf{n}_1\cdot\mathbf{n}_2|}{|\mathbf{n}_1||\mathbf{n}_2|}=\dfrac{4}{9}\).

    \(\phi=\arccos\dfrac{4}{9}\)

  4. Point on line: \((t,1-t,2+t)\). Into plane: \(t+(1-t)+(2+t)=6\Rightarrow t+3=6\Rightarrow t=3\).

    Intersection point: \((3,-2,5)\).

    \((3,-2,5)\)

  5. \(d=\dfrac{|2(2)-(-1)+2(3)-4|}{\sqrt{4+1+4}}=\dfrac{|4+1+6-4|}{3}=\dfrac{7}{3}\).

    \(\dfrac{7}{3}\)

  6. \(\dfrac{x-0}{2}=\dfrac{y-0}{-1}=\dfrac{z-1}{3}\), i.e. \(\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{y}{-1}=\dfrac{z-1}{3}\).

    \(\dfrac{x}{2}=\dfrac{y}{-1}=\dfrac{z-1}{3}\)

  7. Normals \((1,1,0)\) and \((0,1,1)\). Direction of intersection: \((1,1,0)\times(0,1,1)=(1,-1,1)\).

    Find a point: set \(y=0\), then \(x=1\), \(z=2\). Point \((1,0,2)\).

    Line: \(\mathbf{r}=(1,0,2)+\lambda(1,-1,1)\).

    \(\mathbf{r}=(1,0,2)+\lambda(1,-1,1)\)

  8. Directions \((1,0,0)\) and \((0,1,0)\) not proportional \(\Rightarrow\) not parallel.

    Set \((1+s,2,3)=(0,2+t,3)\). From \(x\): \(1+s=0\Rightarrow s=-1\). From \(y\): \(2=2+t\Rightarrow t=0\). \(z\) matches.

    They intersect at \((0,2,3)\).

    intersect at \((0,2,3)\)

  9. Line direction \(\mathbf{d}=(1,0,-1)\), plane normal \(\mathbf{n}=(1,1,1)\).

    Line–plane angle \(\alpha\) satisfies \(\sin\alpha=\dfrac{|\mathbf{d}\cdot\mathbf{n}|}{|\mathbf{d}||\mathbf{n}|}=\dfrac{|1-1|}{\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt3}=0\).

    So \(\alpha=0\): the line is parallel to the plane (and in fact lies in it if a point satisfies — check \((1,1,1)\): \(1+1+1=3\neq0\), so parallel and distinct).

    \(\alpha=0\) (line parallel to plane)

  10. Line through \(P(1,0,-1)\) with \(\mathbf{d}=(2,-1,1)\). Vector \(\overrightarrow{PS}=(-1,2,2)\).

    Normal \(\mathbf{n}=\mathbf{d}\times\overrightarrow{PS}=\begin{vmatrix}\mathbf{i}&\mathbf{j}&\mathbf{k}\\2&-1&1\\-1&2&2\end{vmatrix}=(-4,-5,3)\).

    Plane through \(S\): \(-4(x-0)-5(y-2)+3(z-1)=0\Rightarrow -4x-5y+3z+7=0\), or \(4x+5y-3z=7\).

    \(4x+5y-3z=7\)

  11. \(d=\dfrac{|(-3)-(6)|}{\sqrt{1+4+4}}=\dfrac{9}{3}=3\).

    \(3\)

  12. Normal \(\mathbf{n}=(1,2,-2)\), \(|\mathbf{n}|=3\). Signed distance from \(A\): \(D=\dfrac{3+2+2-4}{3}=1\).

    Foot \(=A-D\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}=A-\dfrac{1}{3}(1,2,-2)=\left(3-\dfrac13,1-\dfrac23,-1+\dfrac23\right)=\left(\dfrac83,\dfrac13,-\dfrac13\right)\).

    \(\left(\dfrac83,\dfrac13,-\dfrac13\right)\)

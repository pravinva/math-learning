MATH141 Revision — AP / GP / Harmonic Set 2 — Worked Answers

Worked solutions — check formula choice ($S_n$ vs $S_\infty$) and the $|r|<1$ condition.
  1. $T_n=-3+(n-1)4=45\Rightarrow4(n-1)=48\Rightarrow n-1=12\Rightarrow n=13$. $13$
  2. $S_n=\dfrac{n}{2}(14+(n-1)(-2))=\dfrac{n}{2}(16-2n)=n(8-n)$. $S_n=0\Rightarrow n=8$ (since $n>1$). $S_n=n(8-n)$; $n=8$
  3. AP $a=18$, $d=3$, $n=20$: $S_{20}=\dfrac{20}{2}(36+19\cdot3)=10(36+57)=930$. $930$
  4. Differences $5,10,20$ not constant (not AP). Ratios $2,2,2$ constant (GP with $r=2$). Geometric only
  5. $T_5=81\cdot(\frac13)^4=1$. $S_5=\dfrac{81\bigl(1-(\frac13)^5\bigr)}{1-\frac13}=\dfrac{81\cdot\frac{242}{243}}{\frac23}=121$. $T_5=1$; $S_5=121$
  6. $a=-2$, $r=-3$: $S_6=\dfrac{-2\bigl(1-(-3)^6\bigr)}{1-(-3)}=\dfrac{-2(1-729)}{4}=\dfrac{-2(-728)}{4}=364$. $364$
  7. $S_n=\dfrac{4(1-(1/2)^n)}{1/2}=8\bigl(1-2^{-n}\bigr)>7.5\Rightarrow1-2^{-n}>15/16\Rightarrow2^{-n}<1/16\Rightarrow n>4$. Smallest $n=5$. Check $S_4=7.5$ not strictly greater. $n=5$
  8. $a=9$, $r=-1/3$, $|r|<1$: $S_\infty=\dfrac{9}{1-(-1/3)}=\dfrac{9}{4/3}=\dfrac{27}{4}$. $27/4$
  9. $0.\overline{27}=\dfrac{27}{100}+\dfrac{27}{10000}+\cdots$ with $a=\dfrac{27}{100}$, $r=\dfrac{1}{100}$: $S_\infty=\dfrac{27/100}{1-1/100}=\dfrac{27}{99}=\dfrac{3}{11}$. $3/11$
  10. First drop $10$. Up+down bounce heights GP: $a=2\cdot10\cdot0.6=12$, $r=0.6$. Total $=10+\dfrac{12}{1-0.6}=10+30=40$. $40\,\mathrm{m}$
  11. Group: $(1)+(\frac12)+(\frac13+\frac14)+(\frac15+\cdots+\frac18)+\cdots$ each bracket $\ge\frac12$, infinitely many brackets $\Rightarrow$ sum $\to\infty$. Terms $\to0$ is necessary but not sufficient for convergence. Diverges (grouping / integral test idea)
  12. $b=\sqrt{ac}=\sqrt{64}=8$. $b=8$

MATH141 Revision — Vectors & 3D Set 2

12 exam-style questions
  1. Find unit vector in direction of \(\mathbf{u}=(-2,1,2)\) and angle it makes with positive \(z\)-axis.
  2. Given \(\mathbf{a}=(3,0,-1)\), \(\mathbf{b}=(1,4,2)\), find vector rejection of \(\mathbf{a}\) from \(\mathbf{b}\).
  3. Find area of parallelogram spanned by \((2,1,3)\) and \((-1,4,0)\).
  4. Find Cartesian equation of line through \((1,-2,4)\) parallel to vector \((3,2,-5)\).
  5. Find equation of plane through point \(P(2,1,-3)\) with normal \((4,-2,1)\).
  6. Find angle between line \(\mathbf{r}=(1,0,0)+t(2,1,2)\) and plane \(x-2y+2z=9\).
  7. Find line of intersection of planes \(x+y+z=3\) and \(2x-y+z=1\).
  8. Find shortest distance between parallel planes \(3x-2y+6z-4=0\) and \(3x-2y+6z+17=0\).
  9. Classify and solve intersection of \(L_1:\mathbf{r}=(0,1,2)+s(1,2,1)\), \(L_2:\mathbf{r}=(2,5,4)+t(-1,-2,-1)\).
  10. Find point symmetric to \(A(3,-2,1)\) about plane \(x+2y-2z+5=0\).
  11. Classify triangle with vertices \(A(1,0,1)\), \(B(2,2,0)\), \(C(4,1,3)\) (scalene/isosceles/equilateral) and find its area.
  12. Find volume of parallelepiped with edges \((2,0,1)\), \((1,3,-1)\), \((0,2,4)\).

MATH141 Revision — Complex + Polar Set 2

12 exam-style questions
  1. Convert \(z=-4-4i\) to polar form.
  2. Evaluate \(\left(\frac{1-i}{\sqrt2}\right)^{12}\).
  3. Find all solutions to \(z^3+8=0\).
  4. If \(z=3\cis(40^\circ)\), find \(\bar z\) in polar form.
  5. Find \(|z-2i|=|z+2i|\) locus in Cartesian form.
  6. Write \((2+2i)^8\) in \(a+bi\) form.
  7. Find principal argument of \(\frac{-1+\sqrt3\,i}{2}\).
  8. Solve \(z^2+6z+13=0\), then express roots in polar form.
  9. Find fourth roots of \(-16i\).
  10. Given \(z=1+\sqrt3\,i\), compute \(\frac{1}{z}\) and its modulus-argument form.
  11. Find complex number with modulus \(5\), argument \(300^\circ\).
  12. Solve \(z\bar z-4(z+\bar z)+15=0\) geometrically.

Strand 1 — Q1–10

  1. $\ln y=2\ln x+\ln3\Rightarrow y=3x^2$. $y=3x^2$
  2. $1+i=\sqrt2\cis\dfrac{\pi}{4}$; $(1+i)^4=4\cis\pi=-4$. $-4$
  3. Quotient: $\dfrac{(1+x^2)-x(2x)}{(1+x^2)^2}=\dfrac{1-x^2}{(1+x^2)^2}$. $\dfrac{1-x^2}{(1+x^2)^2}$
  4. $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{3t^2}{2t}=\dfrac{3t}{2}$; at $t=2$: $3$. $3$
  5. $[-\cos x]_0^{\pi/2}=1$. $1$
  6. $u=x$, $dv=e^x dx$: $xe^x-\int e^x=e^x(x-1)+C$. $e^x(x-1)+C$
  7. $\dfrac{dy}{y^2}=x\,dx\Rightarrow-\dfrac1y=\dfrac{x^2}{2}+C$. $y(0)=1\Rightarrow C=-1$. So $y=\dfrac{1}{1-x^2/2}$. $y=\dfrac{2}{2-x^2}$
  8. RHS is a sum, not a product $f(x)g(y)$. Not separable
  9. $y=8e^{-t/2}$; $y(2\ln2)=8e^{-\ln2}=4$. $y=8e^{-t/2}$; $4$
  10. $z^6=2^6\cis\pi=64(-1)=-64$. $-64$

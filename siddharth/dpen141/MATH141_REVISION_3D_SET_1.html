MATH141 Revision — 3D Analytic Geometry Set 1

12 exam-style questions · 3D analytic geometry only
  1. Write the vector and symmetric equations of the line through \(P(1,-2,3)\) and \(Q(4,1,-3)\).
  2. Find the equation of the plane through \(A(1,0,2)\), \(B(2,1,0)\), \(C(0,-1,3)\).
  3. Find the angle between the planes \(x+2y-2z=1\) and \(2x-y+2z=4\).
  4. Find the intersection of the line \(\mathbf{r}=(0,1,2)+t(1,-1,1)\) with the plane \(x+y+z=6\).
  5. Find the shortest distance from the point \(R(2,-1,3)\) to the plane \(2x-y+2z-4=0\).
  6. Find the Cartesian equation of the line through \((0,0,1)\) parallel to the vector \((2,-1,3)\).
  7. Find the line of intersection of the planes \(x+y=1\) and \(y+z=2\).
  8. Determine whether the lines \(\mathbf{r}=(1,2,3)+s(1,0,0)\) and \(\mathbf{r}=(0,2,3)+t(0,1,0)\) intersect, are parallel, or are skew. If they intersect, find the point.
  9. Find the angle between the line \(\mathbf{r}=(1,1,1)+t(1,0,-1)\) and the plane \(x+y+z=0\).
  10. Find the equation of the plane containing the line \(\dfrac{x-1}{2}=\dfrac{y}{-1}=\dfrac{z+1}{1}\) and the point \(S(0,2,1)\).
  11. Find the distance between the parallel planes \(x-2y+2z-3=0\) and \(x-2y+2z+6=0\).
  12. Find the foot of the perpendicular from \(A(3,1,-1)\) to the plane \(x+2y-2z=4\).

MATH141 Revision — Integration Set 1

12 exam-style questions · Week 10: antidifferentiation & standard table
Scope: Find indefinite integrals from the standard table; include the constant of integration.
  1. Find $\displaystyle\int(6x^2-4x+3)\,dx$.
  2. Find $\displaystyle\int\left(x^3+\dfrac{1}{x^2}\right)\,dx$.
  3. Find $\displaystyle\int e^{5x}\,dx$.
  4. Find $\displaystyle\int\cos(3x)\,dx$.
  5. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{2x+1}\,dx$.
  6. Find $\displaystyle\int\sec^2(4x)\,dx$.
  7. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}\,dx$.
  8. Find $\displaystyle\int\sinh(2x)\,dx$.
  9. Find $f(x)$ if $f'(x)=3x^2-2$ and $f(1)=4$.
  10. Find $\displaystyle\int\left(2\sin x-\dfrac{3}{x}\right)\,dx$ for $x>0$.
  11. Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x}{x^2+1}\,dx$.
  12. Find $\displaystyle\int(1+\tan^2 x)\,dx$.

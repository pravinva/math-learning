DPEN104 · DPEN143 · Two-strand programme

Pre-reading both subjects, 21 Nov – 30 Jan

Four hours a day across two study blocks with a three-week break between them. The goal is a complete first pass of every DPEN104 and DPEN143 lesson — reading each page, solving its examples, and leaving a pattern sheet behind. Exam mastery belongs to the four-month revision that follows, then the 13-week class.

52 study days 208 hours at 4 h/day 19-day break Both subjects covered
This is lesson work, not summary reading. Every day you open the linked DPEN104 and DPEN143 pages, watch their short videos, and solve the worked examples and practice cards on paper before revealing the solutions. Tutorials and tests appear only as samples — the full banks are saved for revision mode.
Day 23 is Sunday 13 December. That is the freeze point: finish the shared circuits-and-fields package and write both sheets from memory. Nothing new starts on 14–15 December.

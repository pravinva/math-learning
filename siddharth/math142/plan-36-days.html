Strong mastery now, then keep it for MATH283

MATH141 is already revised and Ext 2 calculus is familiar. There is no imminent MATH142 exam. These 36 days lock methods so MATH283 self-study is not a restart. Full Paper B is saved for HD practice after the six-month gap.

Lesson first, then packs. Daily work is the weekly lessons, examples on paper, then the matching pack on the revision hub. Reveal answers only to mark. One-page sheet per topic: trigger, 3–5 formulas, one warning.
Days 1–13 run at about 90% effort (~3.2 h, lesson examples plus a handful of pack questions, stop on time even if a pack is unfinished). Closed-book mixed papers and full T-packs wait until Day 14, when the remaining 23 days go to full 4-hour effort.
If a later day overruns: cut extra geometry problems, never parts, partial fractions, DEs, or Taylor. Those four are daily tools in MATH283 (Laplace, Fourier, Gamma).

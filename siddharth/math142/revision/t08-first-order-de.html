QUESTION 1
Solve the separable equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=xy\) with \(y(0)=2\).
QUESTION 2
Solve \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}+2y=e^{-x}\) (linear first-order).
QUESTION 3
Solve the IVP \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}-\dfrac{1}{x}y=x\), \(y(1)=2\) (assume \(x>0\)).
QUESTION 4
Show that \(M\,dx+N\,dy=0\) with \(M=2xy+1\), \(N=x^2+3y^2\) is exact, and solve it.
QUESTION 5
Solve the homogeneous equation \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{x+y}{x-y}\).
QUESTION 6
Solve the Bernoulli equation \(y'+y=xy^3\).
QUESTION 7
A tank holds \(100\) L of water with \(5\) kg of salt. Brine with \(0.2\) kg/L salt enters at \(4\) L/min; the well-mixed solution exits at \(4\) L/min. Find the amount of salt \(Q(t)\) (kg) at time \(t\) minutes, and \(\lim_{t\to\infty}Q(t)\).
QUESTION 8
Newton’s law of cooling: \(\dfrac{dT}{dt}=-k(T-T_a)\). A body cools from \(90^\circ\mathrm{C}\) to \(60^\circ\mathrm{C}\) in \(10\) minutes in a \(20^\circ\mathrm{C}\) room. Find \(T(t)\) and the temperature at \(t=20\) min.
